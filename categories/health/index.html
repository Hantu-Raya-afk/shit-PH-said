📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Perdana Menteri Anwar Ibrahim memberitahu perhimpunan kementerian di Putrajaya pada 4 Mei bahawa langkah jimat cermat yang sedang dilaksanakan di seluruh kementerian persekutuan tidak akan menjejaskan “program teras,” termasuk perkhidmatan penjagaan kesihatan kritikal dan pengambilan doktor serta jururawat baharu. Anwar Promises Austerity Won’t Touch Healthcare — Days After His Own Treasury Proposed Slashing RM3 Billion From the Health Budget

2026-05-04
#Health 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister & Finance Minister

Category: Health

Date: 2026-05-04

📌 What Happened

Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim told a ministry assembly in Putrajaya on May 4 that austerity measures currently being rolled out across federal ministries will not affect “core programmes,” including critical healthcare services and the recruitment of new doctors and nurses. This came days after the Treasury issued a directive on April 29 ordering all ministries to review and cut their 2026 operating expenditures — with a suggested RM5.4 billion in cuts targeting the Health Ministry (RM3.06 billion from its RM46.5 billion allocation) and the Higher Education Ministry (RM2.39 billion). The cuts are driven by a subsidy bill that has ballooned from RM15 billion to a projected RM58.4 billion this year, as the Middle East conflict sends energy prices surging.
