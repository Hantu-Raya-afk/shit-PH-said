[BM] Johari Sahkan Perjanjian Perdagangan AS-Malaysia Dibatalkan — ‘Kemenangan Diplomatik’ PH Bertahan Lima Bulan

2026-03-15
#Economy 

Said by: Johari Abdul Ghani, Investment, Trade and Industry Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Pada 15 Mac 2026, Menteri Pelaburan, Perdagangan dan Industri Johari Abdul Ghani mengesahkan bahawa Perjanjian Perdagangan Timbal Balik Malaysia-AS (ART), ditandatangani oleh Perdana Menteri Anwar Ibrahim dan Presiden AS Donald Trump pada Oktober 2025, telah dibatalkan. “Ia bukan ditangguhkan. Ia terbatal dan tidak sah,” kata Johari kepada wartawan. Mahkamah Agung AS memutuskan tarif menyeluruh Trump adalah menyalahi undang-undang, meruntuhkan rangka kerja tarif yang menjadi asas ART. Perjanjian itu telah mewajibkan Malaysia menyediakan “akses pasaran keutamaan yang signifikan” untuk produk AS merentasi bahan kimia, jentera, kenderaan, tenusu, ayam, babi, beras, dan etanol bahan api. Sebagai pertukaran, AS mengekalkan tarif timbal balik 19% ke atas barangan Malaysia, dengan kadar sifar hanya untuk produk terpilih. Johari berkata AS kini bergantung kepada Seksyen 122 Akta Perdagangan 1974, membenarkan tarif sementara sehingga 10%. Empat ahli parlimen PKR sebelum ini menggesa Putrajaya menangguhkan semua langkah ratifikasi selepas keputusan mahkamah. Sementara itu, AS telah melancarkan siasatan Seksyen 301 ke atas 60 negara, termasuk Malaysia, atas lebihan kapasiti pengeluaran dan buruh paksa.

[BM] Johari Sahkan Perjanjian Perdagangan AS-Malaysia Sudah Mati — Perjanjian Yang PH Raikan Sebenarnya Lebih Buruk Dari Tiada Perjanjian Langsung

2026-03-15
#Economy 

Said by: Johari Abdul Ghani, Investment, Trade and Industry Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Pada 15 Mac 2026, Menteri Perdagangan Antarabangsa dan Industri Johari Abdul Ghani sahkan Perjanjian Perdagangan Timbal Balik Malaysia-AS (ART), yang ditandatangani pada Oktober 2025, kini “terbatal” selepas Mahkamah Agung AS membatalkan tarif timbal balik menyeluruh Presiden Trump. Beliau nyatakan AS akan mengkaji dalam tempoh lima bulan sama ada mahu kenakan tarif baharu di bawah Seksyen 301.

[BM] Johari Sahkan Perjanjian Perdagangan Malaysia-AS ‘Terbatal dan Tidak Sah’ — PH Habiskan Modal Politik Mempertahankan Perjanjian Yang Sudah Tiada

2026-03-15
#Economy 

Said by: Johari Abdul Ghani, Investment, Trade and Industry Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Akmal Says Malaysia Must ‘Accelerate’ Energy Transition — Three Years Into Power, and a War Had to Tell Them

2026-03-15
#Economy 

Said by: Akmal Nasrullah Mohd Nasir, Economy Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

Economy Minister Akmal Nasrullah Mohd Nasir told reporters in Johor Bahru on March 15, 2026, that Malaysia needs to “accelerate” the implementation of the National Energy Transition Roadmap (NETR), particularly the development of solar and hydropower, to reduce reliance on fossil fuels. He made the remarks after attending a “Jualan Rahmah Kasih Sayang” programme, responding to suggestions by academics that Malaysia should cut its fossil fuel dependence following the West Asia conflict that has pushed oil past $100 per barrel and effectively closed the Strait of Hormuz. Akmal noted that Malaysia currently has about 25% installed capacity from renewable sources and said the government is “continuously assessing measures” to accelerate the process.

Guan Eng Claims RM1.6 Billion ‘Windfall’ From 2019 Samurai Bond — The Profit Is Forex Luck, Not Policy Genius

2026-03-15
#Economy 

Said by: Lim Guan Eng, DAP Chairman and MP for Bagan

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

Lim Guan Eng, DAP chairman and former Finance Minister, announced on March 15 that Malaysia stands to record a RM1.6 billion profit from the 200 billion yen samurai bond he issued in March 2019. Citing a parliamentary reply from Prime Minister and Finance Minister Anwar Ibrahim dated March 2, Guan Eng said the yen’s 27.8% depreciation against the ringgit — from RM3.62 per 100 yen at issuance to RM2.59 by December 2025 — has reduced the bond’s principal value from RM7.2 billion to RM5.2 billion. After deducting an estimated RM400 million in interest costs, the net foreign exchange gain is approximately RM1.6 billion. The bond matures in 2029.
