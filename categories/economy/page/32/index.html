#Economy 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2024-10-18

📌 WHAT HAPPENED: In September 2023, Anwar announced a multi-tier levy system for foreign workers designed to reduce Malaysia’s dependency on imported labour. Diesel Price Jumps 56% Overnight as PH Removes Subsidies — Rakyat Told to ‘Tighten Belts’

#Economy 

Said by: Amir Hamzah Azizan, Finance Minister II; Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2024-06-10

📌 WHAT HAPPENED: On 10 June 2024, the PH government removed diesel subsidies in Peninsular Malaysia, causing the price to jump from RM2.15 to RM3.35 per litre overnight — a 56% increase. The impact was immediate: transport costs surged, food prices climbed, and businesses passed the costs on to consumers. Dual 5G Network: Billions Wasted on Political Compromise — One Network Would Have Sufficed

2024-06-01
#Economy 

Said by: Telecom Analysts / Consumers

Category: Economy

Date: 2024-06-01

📌 WHAT HAPPENED: In mid-2024, the PH government shifted Malaysia’s 5G rollout from a single wholesale network (Digital Nasional Berhad, DNB) to a dual-network model — allowing a second 5G network to be built alongside DNB. The move was widely seen as a political compromise to accommodate major telecom operators with political connections who had opposed the single-network model. The dual-network approach doubles infrastructure costs, splits spectrum efficiency, and delays rural coverage expansion — all to solve a political problem, not a technical one.
