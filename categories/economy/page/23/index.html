Foreign Worker Levy Multi-Tier System — Announced 2023, Still Not Fully Implemented

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2024-10-18

📌 WHAT HAPPENED: In September 2023, Anwar announced a multi-tier levy system for foreign workers designed to reduce Malaysia’s dependency on imported labour. The system was repeated in Budget 2025 with further details. The Malaysian Employers Federation (MEF) complained the system was still unfair and insufficiently implemented. Anwar’s promise to cut foreign worker dependency to 15% of the workforce by 2025 was not met. Dual 5G Network: Billions Wasted on Political Compromise — One Network Would Have Sufficed

Said by: Telecom Analysts / Consumers

Category: Economy

Date: 2024-06-01

📌 WHAT HAPPENED: In mid-2024, the PH government shifted Malaysia’s 5G rollout from a single wholesale network (Digital Nasional Berhad, DNB) to a dual-network model — allowing a second 5G network to be built alongside DNB. The move was widely seen as a political compromise to accommodate major telecom operators with political connections who had opposed the single-network model. The dual-network approach doubles infrastructure costs, splits spectrum efficiency, and delays rural coverage expansion — all to solve a political problem, not a technical one.
