#Economy 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-04-17

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Jawapannya: ‘Tengok dulu.’ Anwar memberitahu wartawan bahawa gaji menteri ‘sudah agak rendah’ dan ‘yang penting ialah mereka berkhidmat dengan baik.’ Beliau berkata kerajaan ‘bersedia mempertimbangkan’ pemotongan gaji selanjutnya jika ekonomi merosot, tetapi beralih kepada isyaratnya sendiri tidak mengambil gaji sejak 2022. Anwar Says ‘Let’s See’ on Cabinet Pay Cuts — The PM Who Hasn’t Drawn His Salary Wants Credit for Sacrifice While His Ministers Keep Theirs

2026-04-17
#Economy 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-04-17

📌 What Happened

On April 17, after Friday prayers at Surau Bomba in Cyberjaya, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim was asked whether Malaysia would follow Indonesia in cutting ministerial salaries amid the worsening global energy crisis. His answer: ‘Let’s see.’ Anwar told reporters that ministers’ salaries are ‘already relatively low’ and that ‘what matters is that they serve well.’ He said the government is ‘prepared to consider’ further pay reductions if the economy deteriorates, but pivoted to his own gesture of not drawing a salary since 2022. Two days later, Fahmi Fadzil confirmed Cabinet members are ‘ready for further pay cuts’ — without actually committing to them.

[BM] Fahmi Tangguh Keputusan WFH Penjawat Awam Sampai Lepas Raya — Kerajaan Yang Suruh Rakyat ‘Kurangkan Tenaga’ Tak Mampu Putuskan Soal Kerja Dari Rumah

2026-03-17
#Economy 

Said by: Fahmi Fadzil, Communications Minister (Government Spokesperson)

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-17

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Pada 17 Mac, Menteri Komunikasi Fahmi Fadzil umum selepas mesyuarat pasca-Kabinet bahawa keputusan mengenai pelaksanaan kerja dari rumah (WFH) untuk penjawat awam ditangguhkan sehingga selepas Hari Raya Aidilfitri. Ini hanya empat hari selepas FMT laporkan pada 13 Mac bahawa keputusan itu dijangka dibuat pada hari Selasa, 17 Mac. Fahmi kata “beberapa aspek masih perlu diperhalusi” dan perkara itu akan dibawa ke Majlis Tindakan Ekonomi Negara (MTEN) melalui Ketua Setiausaha Negara. Beliau tekankan perkhidmatan barisan hadapan seperti kesihatan dan pendidikan tidak akan terjejas.
