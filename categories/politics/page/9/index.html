[BM] Rafizi Menjerit ‘Penganiayaan Politik’ Selepas Saman SPRM Atas Perjanjian ARM RM1.1B — Lelaki Yang Bina Kerjaya Atas Anti-Rasuah Kini Tak Tahan Disiasat

2026-05-01
#Politics 

Said by: Rafizi Ramli, Former Economy Minister (PKR, Pandan MP)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-05-01

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Rafizi Ramli, bekas Menteri Ekonomi dan Ahli Parlimen PKR Pandan, mengumumkan pada 1 Mei bahawa beliau menerima saman SPRM untuk memberi keterangan di ibu pejabatnya di Putrajaya pada hari Isnin pukul 10 pagi. Siasatan berkaitan perjanjian usaha sama bernilai RM1.1 bilion antara kerajaan Malaysia dan firma semikonduktor UK Arm Holdings, yang ditandatangani pada Mac 2025 semasa beliau menjadi menteri. Rafizi berkata siasatan tertumpu kepada sama ada beliau menekan Kabinet untuk menandatangani perjanjian itu dengan tergesa-gesa, dan beliau menjangkakan kemungkinan pertuduhan termasuk pecah amanah jenayah atau memberikan maklumat mengelirukan. Beliau menolak siasatan itu sebagai bermotif politik, menyatakan aduan dibuat oleh Perkasa, NGO yang beliau dakwa “tidak pernah sebelum ini menunjukkan minat dalam isu rasuah.”

📌 What Happened

Rafizi Claims MACC Probe Is Political, Says ‘No Money Involved’ — The Anti-Corruption Crusader Who Suddenly Sounds Like Every Politician He Ever Exposed

#Politics 

Said by: Rafizi Ramli, Former Economy Minister (PKR, Pandan MP)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-05-01

📌 What Happened

On May 1, 2026, former Economy Minister Datuk Seri Rafizi Ramli revealed he had been summoned by the Malaysian Anti-Corruption Commission (MACC) to give a statement at its Putrajaya headquarters on May 4. The summons relates to an ongoing investigation into the government’s RM1.1 billion joint venture with UK-based semiconductor firm Arm Holdings, signed during Rafizi’s tenure. The probe, initiated after a complaint by NGO Perkasa, centres on allegations that Rafizi pressured the Cabinet to expedite approval of the deal. In his response posted on X, Rafizi said he expects to be charged with breach of trust, but insisted “there is no monetary motive — no money is involved,” and questioned why Perkasa — an NGO with no track record on corruption issues — suddenly filed the report.
