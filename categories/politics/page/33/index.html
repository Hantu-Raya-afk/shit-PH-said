Anwar Says Sabah 40% Revenue ‘Case Closed, Period’ — While Filing a Court Application to Reopen Parts of the Judgment

2026-03-15
#Politics 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

At a Madani Iftar event at Universiti Malaysia Sabah on March 15, Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim declared that Sabah’s 40 per cent revenue entitlement under the Malaysia Agreement 1963 is “final, case closed” and that there will be “no appeal, no negotiations, period.” In the same speech, he explained that the federal government is going to court — not to appeal the 40 per cent, he insists, but to “seek clarification” on parts of the High Court judgment that he says unfairly questioned the roles of past leaders including Tunku Abdul Rahman, Tun Abdul Razak, and Sabah stalwarts like Tun Fuad Stephens and Tan Sri Harris Salleh. A stay of execution application is scheduled for hearing at the Court of Appeal on March 31. The Sabah government has indicated it will oppose the stay.

Anwar Says Sabah 40% Revenue Is ‘Case Closed’ — While His Government Heads to Court to Challenge the Judgment

2026-03-15
#Politics 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister and Finance Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

On March 15, 2026, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim told a Madani Iftar event at Universiti Malaysia Sabah in Kota Kinabalu that the federal government’s commitment to Sabah’s 40 per cent revenue entitlement under the Malaysia Agreement 1963 is ‘final’ and ’not subject to appeal.’ He declared: ‘No negotiations. We are committed to honour our commitment of 40 per cent to the people of Sabah. Period.’ He repeated the line at a separate event, calling it ‘case closed.’ Yet in the same breath, he confirmed the government is going to court — not to challenge the 40 per cent, he insists, but to ‘seek clarification’ on parts of the October 2025 High Court judgment that he says unfairly questioned the legacies of past national and state leaders. A stay of execution hearing is set for March 31. The Sabah government has said it will oppose the stay.

Puah Admits PH Core Voters Are ‘Swinging’ — Three Years In, And They Still Need to ‘Convince’ People They’re Reformists

2026-03-15
#Politics 

Said by: Jimmy Puah Wee Tse, PKR MP for Tebrau

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

On March 15, 2026, PKR MP Jimmy Puah Wee Tse — the Tebrau MP and Johor PKR vice-chief — told FMT that Pakatan Harapan’s core voter base, particularly the M40 group and urban electorate, has been “swinging.” Puah said the coalition must convince these voters that it has “actually delivered” on reforms, or at least is “in the midst of delivering,” before it can negotiate coalition partnerships for GE16. Political scientist Wong Chin Huat of Sunway University echoed the warning, noting that PH’s core supporters are closely watching how Anwar has managed Malaysia’s racial and religious balance, and that “flip-flopping between appeasing Muslim nationalists and wooing minorities and liberals” could alienate them further.

Zafrul Preaches ‘Courageous Politics’ in Op-Ed — From the Man Whose Government Shields MACC Chief and Failed Its Own Reforms

2026-03-15
#Politics 

Said by: Tengku Zafrul Abdul Aziz, Senior Political Advisor to the Prime Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

Tengku Zafrul Abdul Aziz, Senior Political Advisor to the Prime Minister, published an opinion column in The Edge Malaysia’s March 16–22 edition titled Can Malaysian politics unite the country when the world is coming apart? In it, he called for ‘courageous politics’ — ’the courage to speak honestly to the rakyat,’ ’the courage to make tough but unpopular decisions,’ and ’the courage to reject divisive politics for short-term gain.’ He warned that ‘Malaysia cannot afford the luxury of self-indulgent partisanship’ and argued that social cohesion is ‘fundamental to economic strength.’
