Saifuddin Nasution Bans Books on Anti-Colonial History Using Authoritarian Law PH Promised to Repeal — Then U-Turns in a Week

2026-04-15
#Politics 

Said by: Saifuddin Nasution Ismail, Home Minister (PKR)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-04-15

📌 What Happened

On April 15, 2026, Malaysia’s Home Ministry — led by PKR’s Saifuddin Nasution Ismail — issued a prohibition order banning two books published by independent bookstore Gerakbudaya: Memoir Shamsiah Fakeh: Dari Awas ke Rejimen ke-10 and Komrad Asi (Rejimen 10): Dalam Denyut Nihilisme Sejarah. Both recount the struggles of the Malayan Communist Party against British colonialism. A third book — a Malay translation of an academic study on Mao Zedong, funded by the PKR-led Selangor state government — was also banned. The Home Ministry cited “communist ideologies” as the basis, invoking the Printing Presses and Publications Act 1984. After immediate backlash from civil society groups, PH’s own coalition members, publishers, and international press freedom organisations including ARTICLE 19, Saifuddin announced on April 23 that the ministry was in the process of revoking all three bans.
