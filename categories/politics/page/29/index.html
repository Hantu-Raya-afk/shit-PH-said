Zafrul Preaches ‘Courageous Politics’ in Op-Ed — From the Man Whose Government Shields MACC Chief and Failed Its Own Reforms

2026-03-15
#Politics 

Said by: Tengku Zafrul Abdul Aziz, Senior Political Advisor to the Prime Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

Tengku Zafrul Abdul Aziz, Senior Political Advisor to the Prime Minister, published an opinion column in The Edge Malaysia’s March 16–22 edition titled Can Malaysian politics unite the country when the world is coming apart? In it, he called for ‘courageous politics’ — ’the courage to speak honestly to the rakyat,’ ’the courage to make tough but unpopular decisions,’ and ’the courage to reject divisive politics for short-term gain.’ He warned that ‘Malaysia cannot afford the luxury of self-indulgent partisanship’ and argued that social cohesion is ‘fundamental to economic strength.’

📌 What Happened

On March 14, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim addressed PKR Sarawak members at a ‘mini convention’ in Kuching, where he backed Yang di-Pertuan Agong Sultan Ibrahim’s call to fight corruption, describing graft as ’treason to the nation.’ He urged party members to remain united and reaffirmed PKR’s commitment to reform, justice, and inclusive progress. DAP’s Sangeet Kaur Calls Topple-Government Probe ‘Vague’ and ‘Intimidating’ — While Her Own Coalition Built the Narrative

2026-03-14
#Politics 

Said by: Sangeet Kaur Deo, DAP Women’s Wing Legal Bureau Chief

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-14

📌 What Happened

On March 14, 2026, DAP women’s wing legal bureau chief Sangeet Kaur Deo issued a statement criticising the police investigation into the alleged plot to overthrow the government. She called the probe based on ‘vague allegations’ and warned that ‘sweeping calls for interrogation inevitably create an atmosphere of intimidation and raise concerns about the potential misuse of investigative powers.’ She stressed that ‘state machinery must never be used to silence critics’ and said that if genuine crimes exist, authorities should identify them clearly and state the specific conduct said to constitute those offences. This comes after commentators and civil society figures were summoned for questioning in connection with the probe.
