[BM] Fahmi Salahkan 'Kekeliruan' Pembangkang Atas Kegagalan Rang Undang-Undang Had Penggal PM — Sementara 8 Ahli Parlimen Kerajaan Tak Hadir Pun

Said by: Fahmi Fadzil, Communications Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-03

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Pada 2 Mac 2026, Rang Undang-Undang Perlembagaan (Pindaan) 2026 untuk menghadkan penggal Perdana Menteri kepada dua penggal (10 tahun) dibawa ke undian blok di Dewan Rakyat. Ia memerlukan majoriti dua pertiga sebanyak 148 undi. Ia dapat 146. Keesokan harinya, Menteri Komunikasi Fahmi Fadzil menyalahkan pembangkang kerana ‘kekeliruan’ dan berkata ketidakhadiran mereka membuktikan mereka tidak mahu menghadkan kuasa perdana menteri — sambil mengabaikan hakikat bahawa blok kerajaannya sendiri tidak mampu pastikan kehadiran penuh untuk apa yang sepatutnya reformasi utama.

Anwar Claims 'Prominent Zionist Group' Behind Plot to Topple Government — Daim's Widow Calls It an 'Outright Lie'

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-03

📌 What Happened

On March 3, 2026, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim told Parliament that a “prominent Zionist group” was involved in an alleged plot to topple his government. Anwar claimed documents obtained by authorities showed a suspect — linked to a large-scale MACC corruption investigation — had engaged a UK-based PR firm to coordinate an international campaign to undermine Malaysian institutions. He named Bloomberg as one of the media agencies targeted and said the strategy, running from August 2024 to the next general election, involved foreign lobbyists, bankers, and lawmakers. Na’imah Abdul Khalid, the widow of former Finance Minister Daim Zainuddin, through her lawyers Rajesh Nagarajan and Sachpreetraj Singh, called Anwar’s claims “false, preposterous, outrageous, and a blatant attempt to mislead Parliament and the public,” stating there was “not one word about Zionism or Israel” in the communications plan.
