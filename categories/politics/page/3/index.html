Anwar Says ‘Bring the Evidence’ on Stolen Funds Allegations — The PM Who Promised Transparency Now Demands Others Do the Investigating

2026-04-24
#Politics 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-04-24

📌 What Happened

On April 24, 2026, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim was confronted by reporters at the Temenggong Ibrahim Teachers Education Campus in Johor Bahru about a viral 49-second video by a woman named Sofia Rini Buyong, who alleged that a former officer of Anwar’s had been instructed to move stolen public money to Africa and possessed unexplained wealth worth “billions of ringgit.” Anwar dismissed the allegations outright, saying he would not entertain baseless claims from social media, asking “How do I entertain such allegations? If I have 1,000 comments, do I have to answer every one?” He challenged accusers to “bring the evidence,” adding that without proof, such allegations were merely a “breeding ground for lies.” Opposition politicians from PAS and Bersatu had called for an investigation. No evidence was provided in the video.

Ramanan Says ‘No Issue’ Giving Bestinet Another Migrant Worker Contract — His Own PKR MPs Demand an Investigation

2026-04-16
#Politics 

Said by: R. Ramanan, Human Resources Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-04-16

📌 What Happened

On April 16, Human Resources Minister R. Ramanan told The Star that his ministry sees “no issue” with Bestinet Sdn Bhd — the company founded by tycoon Aminul Islam — developing a new foreign worker recruitment system called TURAP (The Universal Recruitment Advanced Platform). This came weeks after Bloomberg published an investigation into endemic corruption in Malaysia’s migrant worker recruitment from Bangladesh, naming both Bestinet and Aminul. Ramanan initially rejected the Bloomberg report as “unverified and inaccurate,” but then told The Star his ministry was open to the system. By April 20, nine PKR backbenchers — including Wong Chen (Subang), Nik Nazmi (Setiawangsa), and Lee Chean Chung (Petaling Jaya) — issued a joint statement demanding an investigation into Ramanan’s ministry over the Bestinet connection.
