Saifuddin’s Home Ministry Bans Three History Books — Then U-Turns After Backlash, Still Using the Colonial Law PH Promised to Repeal

2026-04-23
#Politics 

Said by: Saifuddin Nasution Ismail, Home Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-04-23

📌 What Happened

On April 15, 2026, Malaysia’s Home Ministry issued a prohibition order on two books published by Gerakbudaya — Memoir Shamsiah Fakeh: Dari AWAS ke Regimen ke-10 and Komrad Asi Regimen 10: Dalam Denyut Nihilisme Sejarah — declaring them a national security threat for containing “communist elements and ideologies.” A week later, a third title — the Malay translation of an academic book on Mao Zedong, Mao Zedong: China dalam Dunia Abad ke-20 — was also banned. On April 23, Home Minister Saifuddin Nasution Ismail announced the government would reverse all three bans after fierce backlash from civil society groups, publishers, academics, and opposition lawmakers, and introduced a new process where he would personally review future bans.

Setakat Mac 2026, kerajaan MADANI sudah mengharamkan 27 buku — lebih daripada keseluruhan 24 buku yang diharamkan pada 2025.
