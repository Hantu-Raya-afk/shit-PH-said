Anwar Says ‘Bring the Evidence’ on Stolen Funds Allegations — The PM Who Promised Transparency Now Demands Others Do the Investigating

2026-04-24
#Politics 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-04-24

📌 What Happened

On April 24, 2026, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim was confronted by reporters at the Temenggong Ibrahim Teachers Education Campus in Johor Bahru about a viral 49-second video by a woman named Sofia Rini Buyong, who alleged that a former officer of Anwar’s had been instructed to move stolen public money to Africa and possessed unexplained wealth worth “billions of ringgit.” Anwar dismissed the allegations outright, saying he would not entertain baseless claims from social media, asking “How do I entertain such allegations? If I have 1,000 comments, do I have to answer every one?” He challenged accusers to “bring the evidence,” adding that without proof, such allegations were merely a “breeding ground for lies.” Opposition politicians from PAS and Bersatu had called for an investigation. No evidence was provided in the video.

Saifuddin Admits He Only Learned About Book Bans After They Were Enforced — Madani Government Banned 3 Books as ‘Communist Threats,’ Then U-Turned Under Pressure

2026-04-23
#Politics 

Said by: Saifuddin Nasution Ismail, Home Minister (PKR)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-04-23

📌 What Happened

On April 15, Malaysia’s Home Ministry issued a prohibition order on two Malay-language books — Memoir Shamsiah Fakeh: Dari AWAS ke Rejimen Ke-10 and Komrad Asi (Rejimen 10): Dalam Denyut Nihilisme Sejarah — declaring them dangers to national security for containing “communist elements and ideologies.” By April 22, a third book, a Malay translation of an academic study on Mao Zedong funded by the PKR-led Selangor state government, was also banned. Then on April 23, Home Minister Saifuddin Nasution Ismail announced the government was reversing all three bans — and admitted he only learned about the prohibition orders after enforcement had already taken place.

Saifuddin Nasution Bans Three Books for ‘Communist Ideologies’ — Then Reverses the Ban a Week Later After His Own Coalition Calls It Out

2026-04-23
#Politics 

Said by: Saifuddin Nasution Ismail, Home Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-04-23

📌 What Happened

On April 16, 2026, Malaysia’s Home Ministry — under Minister Saifuddin Nasution Ismail (PKR) — banned two books published by Gerakbudaya: Memoir Shamsiah Fakeh: Dari Awas ke Rejimen Ke-10 and Komrad Asi (Rejimen 10): Dalam Denyut Nihilisme Sejarah, claiming they contained communist ideologies that threatened national security. A third book, Mao Zedong: China dalam Dunia Abad ke-20 — funded by the PKR-led Selangor state government — was also banned. Just seven days later, on April 23, Saifuddin announced the government was in the process of revoking all three bans and introduced a new review process where he would personally vet future bans.
