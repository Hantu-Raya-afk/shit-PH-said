DAP Brings Forward Congress to July — Will Vote on Whether Leaders Should Quit Government, But Promises to Stay Anyway

2026-03-09
#Politics 

Said by: Anthony Loke, DAP Secretary-General / Transport Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-09

📌 What Happened

DAP, the largest component party in Anwar’s Pakatan Harapan coalition by parliamentary seats, has brought forward its national congress from September to July 12, 2026. At the congress, more than 4,000 delegates will vote in an internal referendum on whether party leaders should resign from their government posts. DAP secretary-general and Transport Minister Anthony Loke told Malaysiakini on February 24 that “the government’s response has been inadequate and does not meet public expectations” regarding the MACC scandal. The move comes after DAP lost every seat it contested in November’s Sabah state polls and faces mounting grassroots anger over stalled reforms, the MACC crisis, and the perception that DAP is enabling a government that has abandoned its own platform.

📌 What Happened

Ramanan Threatens ‘Firm Action’ Over Free Speech — PH’s Reform Promise Lasts Until Someone Uses It

2026-03-09
#Politics 

Said by: R. Ramanan, Human Resources Minister and PKR Vice-President

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-09

📌 What Happened

On March 9, 2026, Human Resources Minister and PKR Vice-President Datuk Seri R. Ramanan issued a public statement warning that the government will take “firm action” against anyone who “deliberately threatens national peace,” particularly on matters involving religion, race, and the monarchy — the so-called 3R issues. Ramanan said there has been “a worrying rise in statements, social media posts and online comments aimed at provoking racial tensions” and warned that “freedom of speech should not be misused to incite or divide society.” The following day, he told reporters he would personally raise the issue of unnamed “provocateurs” at the March 11 Cabinet meeting. Entrepreneur and Cooperative Development Minister Steven Sim echoed the call, demanding police “act firmly against all perpetrators.”
