Saifuddin Nasution Bans Books First, Asks Questions Later — Home Minister Admits He Only Found Out About His Own Ministry’s Ban After Enforcement

2026-04-23
#Politics 

Said by: Saifuddin Nasution Ismail, Home Affairs Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-04-23

📌 What Happened

On April 16, 2026, Malaysia’s Home Affairs Ministry issued prohibition orders on two books — Memoir Shamsiah Fakeh: Dari Awas ke Rejimen Ke-10 and Komrad Asi (Rejimen 10): Dalam Denyut Nihilisme Sejarah — along with a Malay translation of a study on Mao Zedong, citing “communist ideologies” as a threat to national security. One of the banned memoirs was authored by the grandmother of a DAP state assemblyman. The ban triggered immediate backlash from civil society, publishers, international press freedom groups including Article 19, and even members of Anwar’s own Pakatan Harapan coalition. By April 23, Home Minister Saifuddin Nasution Ismail announced the government was in the process of revoking all three bans — and admitted he had only learned about the prohibition orders after enforcement had already taken place.

Saifuddin Nasution Bans Books for ‘Communist Ideologies’ — Then Reverses After Backlash, Admits He Didn’t Even Know About It

2026-04-23
#Politics 

Said by: Saifuddin Nasution Ismail, Home Affairs Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-04-23

📌 What Happened

On April 16, 2026, Malaysia’s Home Affairs Ministry declared a ban on two books — Memoir Shamsiah Fakeh: Dari Awas ke Rejimen Ke-10 and Komrad Asi (Rejimen 10): Dalam Denyut Nihilisme Sejarah — for allegedly containing communist elements and threatening national security. A third book, Mao Zedong: China dalam Dunia Abad ke-20, a Selangor state-funded academic translation, was also prohibited. One week later, on April 23, Home Affairs Minister Saifuddin Nasution Ismail announced he was in the process of revoking all three bans. He admitted the previous book-banning process had forced him to “firefight” because he only learned about the bans after enforcement had already taken place. He announced a new mechanism where Special Branch reports would no longer be enforced immediately — the minister would personally review them first.
