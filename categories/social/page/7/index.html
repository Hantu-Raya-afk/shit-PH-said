📌 What Happened

Tourism, Arts and Culture Minister Datuk Seri Tiong King Sing defended the Rain Rave Water Music Festival 2026 — a government-backed three-day water rave in Bukit Bintang running April 30 to May 2 — against mounting criticism from opposition parties, civil society, and even a federal religious agency. Fahmi’s MCMC Escalates Digital Crackdown — Grindr and Blued Purged From App Stores as Government Replaces ‘LGBT’ With ‘Deviant Culture’

2026-03-12
#Social 

Said by: Fahmi Fadzil, Communications Minister / Marhamah Rosli, Deputy Minister (Religious Affairs)

Category: Social

Date: 2026-03-12

📌 What Happened

Communications Minister Fahmi Fadzil confirmed on February 25, in a written parliamentary reply, that the Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission (MCMC) had blocked access to LGBTQ dating websites Grindr and Blued, and was “studying legal measures” to curb their mobile apps. By early March, both platforms were removed from Google Play and the Apple App Store in Malaysia — an escalation from website blocking to full digital erasure. Deputy Minister for Religious Affairs Marhamah Rosli separately urged Malaysians to stop using the term “LGBT” and replace it with “budaya songsang” (deviant culture) to prevent normalisation, while the government rolled out state-level programs including “spiritual guidance camps,” coordinated Friday sermons via state Islamic councils, and a multi-agency committee to address LGBTQ issues among Muslims. Deputy Prime Minister Ahmad Zahid Hamidi announced a proposed Cyber Crime Bill that would expand the government’s legal powers to restrict such platforms.

Deputy Religious Affairs Minister Orders Public to Replace ‘LGBT’ With ‘Deviant Culture’ — PH’s Reform Government Now Policing Language

2026-02-26
#Social 

Said by: Marhamah Rosli, Deputy Minister in the Prime Minister’s Department (Religious Affairs)

Category: Social

Date: 2026-02-26

📌 What Happened

On February 26, 2026, Deputy Minister in the Prime Minister’s Department (Religious Affairs) Marhamah Rosli told the Dewan Negara that the public should stop using the term ‘LGBT’ and replace it with ‘deviant culture’ (budaya songsang). Responding to a question from Senator Hussin Ismail about government efforts to monitor private gatherings, Marhamah said the change was intended to “avoid normalisation” and “curb the expansion of related content algorithms on social media platforms.” She added it would ensure “consistent terminology in speech and writing, while avoiding indirect promotion.” The directive was framed as an immediate, government-wide policy change.
