Nga Kor Ming Tells Rain Rave Critics to ‘Stay Home’ — A Knife Incident, 80,000 Crowd, and Zero Accountability Later

2026-05-03
#Social 

Said by: Nga Kor Ming, Housing and Local Government Minister (DAP)

Category: Social

Date: 2026-05-03

📌 What Happened

On May 3, 2026, Housing and Local Government Minister Nga Kor Ming defended the government-backed Rain Rave Water Music Festival in Bukit Bintang, which drew 80,000 participants and 200,000 online viewers. Echoing Tourism Minister Tiong King Sing, Nga told critics: “For those who disagree, it is your right to choose to stay at home and not to come. Simple as that.” He framed the event as proof that “Malaysia Truly Asia is not a slogan but our culture and way of life.” His statement came the same day the South China Morning Post reported that a 28-year-old woman carrying a knife had been tackled and arrested by the crowd at the festival on Saturday night.
