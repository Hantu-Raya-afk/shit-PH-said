Anwar Promised to End Hardcore Poverty by Q1 2026 — It’s March 2026 and Nobody’s Talking About It

2025-12-07
#Economy 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister and Finance Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2025-12-07

📌 What Happened

On December 7, 2025, at the closing ceremony of the Rancakkan Madani programme in Putrajaya, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim publicly declared that Malaysia’s hardcore poverty rate had fallen to 0.09 per cent — affecting roughly 7,000 households — and told civil servants that since the number was “so small,” the government could “end it by the first quarter of 2026.” Multiple outlets — Bernama, Malay Mail, The Star, Business Today — ran the claim as headline news. It is now mid-March 2026, deep into Q1. There has been no victory declaration, no update on the 7,000 households, and no one in government appears to be talking about it.

#Politics 

Said by: Reuters / Malaysian Anti-Corruption Commission

Category: Politics

Date: 2025-12-04

📌 WHAT HAPPENED: On 4 December 2025, Anwar Ibrahim’s senior political secretary Shamsul Iskandar Mohd Akin was charged with receiving bribes totalling RM176,829 from a local businessman. 1MDB Debt Still RM50 Billion — Malaysia’s Largest Fiscal Burden Under Anwar’s Watch

2025-12-03
#Economy 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2025-12-03

📌 WHAT HAPPENED: As of December 2025, Malaysia’s 1MDB debt still stands at approximately RM50 billion — the largest single fiscal burden in the country’s history. [BM] Anwar Bela Surat Sokongan Di Parlimen — Kemudian Serang Pembangkang Kerana Buat PERKARA SAMA

2025-12-02
#Politics 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim in Parliament / The Star

Category: Politics

Date: 2025-12-02

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Apabila dihadapkan di parlimen mengenai pembelaannya terhadap ‘surat sokongan’ (surat cadangan) untuk tender kerajaan, Anwar berpusing menyerang pembangkang dengan mendedahkan bahawa pegawai negeri Kelantan dan Terengganu juga mengeluarkan surat serupa. Beliau berkata: ‘Saya mesti menyeru kemunafikan — tiada satu perkataan pun mengenai surat oleh exco Kelantan dan Terengganu yang meminta kelulusan.’

🔥 Kenapa Ini Penting

Daripada menangani sama ada surat sokongan itu wajar (ia tidak — ia menjejaskan perolehan kompetitif), pembelaan Anwar ialah ‘pihak sana pun buat juga.’ Ini setaraf politik kanak-kanak yang ditangkap mencuri berkata ’tapi Johnny pun curi juga!’ Ia bukan pembelaan — ia pengakuan bahawa amalan itu sejagat dan tiada siapa, termasuk PH, berniat menghentikannya.
