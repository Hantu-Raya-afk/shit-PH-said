#Politics 

Said by: Rafizi Ramli, Former Economy Minister (PKR, Pandan MP)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-05-04

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Bekas Menteri Ekonomi Datuk Seri Rafizi Ramli melaporkan diri ke ibu pejabat Suruhanjaya Pencegahan Rasuah Malaysia (SPRM) di Putrajaya pada 4 Mei untuk disoal siasat berhubung dakwaan penyelewengan dalam perjanjian pelaburan semikonduktor bernilai RM1.1 bilion antara Kementerian Ekonomi dan syarikat cip gergasi Britain, Arm Holdings. SPRM setakat ini telah merakamkan 22 kenyataan dalam siasatan yang bermula pada 13 Februari. Siasatan tertumpu kepada sama ada Rafizi menekan Kabinet untuk meluluskan perjanjian itu semasa beliau menjadi Menteri Ekonomi.

Anwar Admits Government Can Only ‘Partly’ Help With Cost of Living — Promises to Discuss It at Cabinet on Wednesday After 2.5 Years in Power

2026-05-04
#Economy 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister & Finance Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-05-04

📌 What Happened

Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim, speaking at a monthly assembly with ministry staff at Putrajaya on May 4, 2026, admitted that the government can only “partly” help Malaysians struggling with the cost of living. “Are we able to help? My answer is partly yes — we are not able to help everyone,” he said. He acknowledged that official reports claim prices are under control, but conceded that feedback from petty traders — food stall operators — shows prices are rising “gradually week by week” with significant impact on business operations. His big plan: raise the issue at Cabinet this Wednesday.

Anwar Hides Behind Royal Decree to Keep PH in Power in Negeri Sembilan — After Losing Majority When 14 UMNO Assemblymen Walk Out

2026-05-04
#Politics 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister & Finance Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-05-04

📌 What Happened

On May 4, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim responded to the Negeri Sembilan political crisis by refusing to comment on UMNO’s decision to withdraw support from the PH-led state government, instead invoking the Ruler’s decree to maintain the status quo. “The Umno (Negeri Sembilan) statement is not yet clear. I do not wish to comment on it,” Anwar told reporters at a ministry assembly in Putrajaya. All 14 UMNO assemblymen in the state withdrew support for Menteri Besar Aminuddin Harun on April 27, citing his interference in a constitutional crisis involving traditional chieftains attempting to depose the state ruler Tuanku Muhriz. Rather than address the loss of majority, Anwar said: “We abide by the Ruler’s decree, which calls for stability and for the Menteri Besar to continue his duties.” DAP secretary-general Anthony Loke separately insisted the state government “remains intact and stable.”

Anwar Promises Austerity Won’t Touch Healthcare — Days After His Own Treasury Proposed Slashing RM3 Billion From the Health Budget

2026-05-04
#Health 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister & Finance Minister

Category: Health

Date: 2026-05-04

📌 What Happened

Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim told a ministry assembly in Putrajaya on May 4 that austerity measures currently being rolled out across federal ministries will not affect “core programmes,” including critical healthcare services and the recruitment of new doctors and nurses. This came days after the Treasury issued a directive on April 29 ordering all ministries to review and cut their 2026 operating expenditures — with a suggested RM5.4 billion in cuts targeting the Health Ministry (RM3.06 billion from its RM46.5 billion allocation) and the Higher Education Ministry (RM2.39 billion). The cuts are driven by a subsidy bill that has ballooned from RM15 billion to a projected RM58.4 billion this year, as the Middle East conflict sends energy prices surging.

Anwar Tells Civil Servants to ‘Explain’ RM5 Billion Monthly Subsidy Burden — Because the Government Can’t Do the Explaining Itself

2026-05-04
#Economy 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister & Finance Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-05-04

📌 What Happened

Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim, speaking at the Natural Resources and Environmental Sustainability Ministry’s monthly assembly in Putrajaya on May 4, urged civil servants to help “explain” the government’s fiscal strain amid the Middle East energy crisis. He admitted the government is now paying up to RM5 billion a month for RON95 petrol subsidies alone under the Budi95 scheme, conceded that cost of living is a real problem — “Do we have a problem with living cost? Yes, I am not denying it” — and said the government can only provide “partial” help, while insisting fuel prices will stay subsidised.
