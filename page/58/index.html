FMT Journalist Rex Tan Arrested Under Sedition Act for Asking a ‘Sensitive Question’

2026-01-17
#Social 

Said by: Royal Malaysia Police

Category: Social

Date: 2026-01-17

📌 WHAT HAPPENED: In January 2026, Free Malaysia Today journalist Rex Tan was arrested at dawn under the colonial-era Sedition Act 1948 for asking a question at a public debate that linked the Gaza crisis to the treatment of ethnic Chinese in Malaysia. Police arrived at Tan’s home early on a Saturday morning — five days after the debate — and arrested him under Section 4(1) of the Sedition Act. The question was provocative but fell squarely within the bounds of legitimate public discourse.
