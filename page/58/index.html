Race-Based Economic Policies NEP Still Untouchable — ‘Unanimous’ Critics Ignored

2025-12-15
#Social 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim Administration / Think Tanks

Category: Social

Date: 2025-12-15

📌 WHAT HAPPENED: Eurasia Review published a comprehensive analysis in December 2025 finding “almost unanimous opinion” among analysts that Malaysia’s race-based economic policies — rooted in the New Economic Policy (NEP) of 1971 — have failed their “overarching goal of national unity.” Yet the analysis concluded the government is “highly unlikely to abandon” them. 2025-12-07
#Economy 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister and Finance Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2025-12-07

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku
