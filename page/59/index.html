Madani Government Uses Section 124B Against 14 Activists and Academics — The Coalition That Promised Reform Now Wields BN’s Favourite Weapon

2026-03-16
#Politics 

Said by: Fahmi Fadzil, Communications Minister (Government Spokesperson)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-16

📌 What Happened

The Royal Malaysia Police have summoned 14 activists, academics, and political researchers for questioning under Section 124B of the Penal Code — a provision carrying up to 20 years in prison — as part of an investigation into an alleged plot to “topple the government and sabotage national stability.” Among those questioned was C4 Centre CEO Pushpan Murugiah, grilled for 90 minutes on March 10. On March 16, a coalition of civil society groups issued a joint statement condemning the Madani government, asking: “Do we want to return to a time when the government cannot be criticised?” Communications Minister Fahmi Fadzil, the government’s spokesperson, set the tone on February 27 when he called the alleged plot “a very serious matter” and said the government would “leave it to the police.” The government has made no public statement to narrow the scope of the investigation or address concerns about civil liberties since the probe widened to include civil society figures.
