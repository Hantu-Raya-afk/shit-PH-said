#Politics

Category: Politics

Date: 2025-11-30

📌 WHAT HAPPENED: On 30 November 2025, Pakatan Harapan was demolished in the Sabah state election. The coalition won just 1 out of 20 seats it contested — down from 7 previously. Penang PTMP Procurement: No Open Tenders, Just ‘Request for Proposals’ for Billions in Contracts

2025-11-21
#Economy 

Said by: Chow Kon Yeow / DAP Penang / Civil Society Critics

Category: Economy

Date: 2025-11-21

📌 WHAT HAPPENED: Multiple reports, including from FMT and the academic community, documented that DAP’s Penang Transport Master Plan (PTMP) relied on ‘request-for-proposal’ (RFP) procurement mechanisms rather than open competitive tenders for billions of ringgit in infrastructure contracts.
