📌 What Happened

Lim Guan Eng, DAP chairman and former Finance Minister, announced on March 15 that Malaysia stands to record a RM1.6 billion profit from the 200 billion yen samurai bond he issued in March 2019. Citing a parliamentary reply from Prime Minister and Finance Minister Anwar Ibrahim dated March 2, Guan Eng said the yen’s 27.8% depreciation against the ringgit — from RM3.62 per 100 yen at issuance to RM2.59 by December 2025 — has reduced the bond’s principal value from RM7.2 billion to RM5.2 billion.
