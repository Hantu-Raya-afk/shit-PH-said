National Debt Servicing Eats 16% of Revenue — Every Sixth Ringgit Goes to Paying Interest

2025-12-01
#Economy 

Said by: Ministry of Finance / Bloomberg

Category: Economy

Date: 2025-12-01

📌 WHAT HAPPENED: Malaysia’s debt servicing costs consume approximately 16% of federal government revenue — meaning roughly every sixth ringgit collected in taxes goes straight to paying interest on accumulated debt rather than funding schools, hospitals, or infrastructure. With the debt-to-GDP ratio at 64.6% — above the government’s own 60% target — and 1MDB legacy debt still totalling RM50 billion on the books, fiscal space remains severely constrained.
