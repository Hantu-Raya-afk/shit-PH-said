Orang Asli Land Rights Violations Continue — Indigenous Communities Displaced for Logging and Plantations

2024-12-01
#Social 

Said by: Amnesty International / Indigenous Rights Groups

Category: Social

Date: 2024-12-01

📌 WHAT HAPPENED: Throughout 2024, Orang Asli and indigenous communities in Peninsular Malaysia continued to face systematic violations of customary land rights. Ancestral forests were cleared for logging concessions and palm oil plantations, often without free, prior, and informed consent. Amnesty International flagged indigenous rights as one of seven areas where Malaysia fell short of its human rights obligations. Orang Asli make up just 0.2% of Malaysia’s civil service despite comprising approximately 0.7% of the population — even proportional representation is denied.

Government Confirms Sedition Act Will NOT Be Repealed — PH’s Signature Promise Officially Dead

2024-11-14
#Politics 

Said by: Shamsul Anuar Nasarah, Deputy Home Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2024-11-14

📌 What Happened

On November 14, 2024, Deputy Home Minister Datuk Seri Dr Shamsul Anuar Nasarah told Parliament that the government has no plans to amend or repeal the Sedition Act 1948. He said the Act would be maintained to ‘preserve public order and security.’ This statement was made in Anwar’s own Dewan Rakyat, by Anwar’s own deputy minister, ending any pretence that PH would deliver on one of its most defining reform promises. By early 2026, at least 90 arrests had been made under the Sedition Act since 2024 — under PH’s watch.
