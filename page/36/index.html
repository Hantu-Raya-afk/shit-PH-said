📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Pada 29 April 2026, Menteri Komunikasi dan jurucakap kerajaan Fahmi Fadzil mengesahkan di taklimat akhbar mingguan bahawa semua kementerian persekutuan telah diarahkan oleh Kementerian Kewangan untuk menyemak dan memotong perbelanjaan operasi bagi 2026. #Economy 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister & Finance Minister; Johan Mahmood Merican, Treasury Secretary-General

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-04-29

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Pada 29 April 2026, Ketua Setiausaha Perbendaharaan Johan Mahmood Merican mengeluarkan arahan kerajaan mengarahkan semua kementerian dan agensi persekutuan memotong bajet mengurus mereka untuk 2026, memetik kesan kenaikan harga tenaga akibat konflik Timur Tengah. Fahmi Fadzil Claims ‘Budget 2026 Unchanged’ While Treasury Orders All Ministries to Slash Spending by May 15

2026-04-29
#Economy 

Said by: Fahmi Fadzil, Communications Minister & Government Spokesperson (PKR)

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-04-29

📌 What Happened

On April 29, 2026, Communications Minister and government spokesperson Datuk Fahmi Fadzil confirmed at his weekly press conference that all ministries had received directives from the Ministry of Finance to review and cut operating expenditures for Budget 2026. Finance Ministry secretary-general Datuk Johan Mahmood Merican issued the formal directive the same day, ordering all ministries, departments, and agencies to propose spending cuts by May 15. The reason cited was the impact of the Middle East conflict — specifically the US-Israeli war on Iran — which has sent global energy prices soaring and forced Malaysia to spend billions more on fuel subsidies. In the same breath, Fahmi claimed that “the government has no intention of tabling Budget 2026 in Parliament again” and that the budget “remains unchanged” — insisting the adjustments are merely about “optimising existing spending.”
