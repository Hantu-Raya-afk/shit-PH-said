Akmal Says Malaysia Must ‘Accelerate’ Energy Transition — Three Years Into Power, and a War Had to Tell Them

2026-03-15
#Economy 

Said by: Akmal Nasrullah Mohd Nasir, Economy Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

Economy Minister Akmal Nasrullah Mohd Nasir told reporters in Johor Bahru on March 15, 2026, that Malaysia needs to “accelerate” the implementation of the National Energy Transition Roadmap (NETR), particularly the development of solar and hydropower, to reduce reliance on fossil fuels. He made the remarks after attending a “Jualan Rahmah Kasih Sayang” programme, responding to suggestions by academics that Malaysia should cut its fossil fuel dependence following the West Asia conflict that has pushed oil past $100 per barrel and effectively closed the Strait of Hormuz. Akmal noted that Malaysia currently has about 25% installed capacity from renewable sources and said the government is “continuously assessing measures” to accelerate the process.

Anwar Says Sabah 40% Revenue ‘Case Closed, Period’ — While Filing a Court Application to Reopen Parts of the Judgment

2026-03-15
#Politics 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

At a Madani Iftar event at Universiti Malaysia Sabah on March 15, Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim declared that Sabah’s 40 per cent revenue entitlement under the Malaysia Agreement 1963 is “final, case closed” and that there will be “no appeal, no negotiations, period.” In the same speech, he explained that the federal government is going to court — not to appeal the 40 per cent, he insists, but to “seek clarification” on parts of the High Court judgment that he says unfairly questioned the roles of past leaders including Tunku Abdul Rahman, Tun Abdul Razak, and Sabah stalwarts like Tun Fuad Stephens and Tan Sri Harris Salleh. A stay of execution application is scheduled for hearing at the Court of Appeal on March 31. The Sabah government has indicated it will oppose the stay.
