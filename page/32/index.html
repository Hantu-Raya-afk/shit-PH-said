Akmal Admits Fuel Supply Only Secured Until June — Economy Minister Goes on Bloomberg to Say ‘We’re Still Negotiating’

2026-04-30
#Economy 

Said by: Akmal Nasrullah Mohd Nasir, Economy Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-04-30

📌 What Happened

On April 30, 2026, Economy Minister Akmal Nasrullah Mohd Nasir went on Bloomberg Television — interviewed by Haslinda Amin — and admitted that Malaysia can only sustain its fuel supply until the end of June 2026. He confirmed that Petronas “is having conversations with potential suppliers” but stressed that negotiations must secure “the right price.” He also noted the government has already reduced the subsidised fuel quota from 300 litres to 200 litres per Malaysian and introduced civil servant work-from-home arrangements to manage demand. He claimed the government “still has flexibility” to manage subsidies and the fiscal deficit.

Fahmi Confirms RM10 Billion Budget Slash But Says Government Won’t Go Back to Parliament — The Dewan Rakyat Approved It, the Cabinet Rewrote It

2026-04-30
#Economy 

Said by: Fahmi Fadzil, Communications Minister & Government Spokesperson

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-04-30

📌 What Happened

On April 29, Malaysia’s Treasury issued a directive ordering all federal ministries, departments, and agencies to slash their 2026 operating budgets — a move expected to save RM10 billion — after the government’s subsidy bill ballooned from the originally approved RM15 billion to a projected RM58.4 billion due to the Iran war energy crisis. The next day, Communications Minister and government spokesperson Fahmi Fadzil confirmed the directive at a press briefing but stated there were “no plans by the government to re-submit its 2026 budget to parliament for now,” framing the massive spending overhaul as routine “recalibration” rather than what it plainly is: a wholesale rewrite of Parliament-approved expenditure.

Fahmi Says RM5.4 Billion Health and Education Cuts Are ‘Only Non-Critical Spending’ — While the Health Ministry Loses RM3 Billion

2026-04-30
#Economy 

Said by: Fahmi Fadzil, Communications Minister & Government Spokesperson

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-04-30

📌 What Happened

On April 30, Communications Minister and government spokesperson Fahmi Fadzil defended a Treasury directive ordering RM10 billion in operating expenditure cuts across all federal ministries, including RM3.06 billion from the Health Ministry and RM2.39 billion from the Higher Education Ministry. Fahmi told reporters after a Cabinet meeting that the proposed cuts target “only non-critical spending” and that “all core ministry expenditures will continue as approved under the 2026 budget.” The directive, dated April 29, was issued in response to the fiscal strain caused by the Iran conflict and ballooning fuel subsidy costs estimated at RM7 billion monthly.

Finance Ministry Says RM5.4B Cuts to Health and Education Are ‘Non-Critical’ — RM3 Billion From Hospitals Isn’t Essential?

2026-04-30
#Economy 

Said by: Finance Ministry / Treasury (under Amir Hamzah Azizan, Finance Minister II)

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-04-30

📌 What Happened

On April 29, the Treasury proposed RM5.4 billion in operational spending cuts targeting the Ministry of Health (RM3.06 billion) and the Ministry of Higher Education as part of a broader RM10 billion austerity drive across all federal agencies. The cuts — framed as a response to the Iran conflict’s impact on fuel subsidies — include postponing events, limiting overseas trips, saving on utilities, delaying recruitment for “non-critical positions,” and optimising agency reserves. On April 30, the Finance Ministry issued a statement insisting that “essential services will not be disrupted” and that adjustments only involve “non-critical items.”

Government Trumpets RM1.9 Million WFH Fuel Savings in 10 Days — While Bleeding RM4 Billion a Month on Subsidies

2026-04-30
#Economy 

Said by: Wan Ahmad Dahlan Abdul Aziz, Director-General of Public Service

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-04-30

📌 What Happened

On April 30, 2026, Director-General of Public Service Tan Sri Wan Ahmad Dahlan Abdul Aziz proudly announced that the government’s work-from-home (WFH) initiative — introduced on April 15 as an emergency response to soaring fuel costs from the Middle East conflict — had saved RM1.9 million in RON95 petrol subsidies over its first 10 days. That works out to 979,632 litres of petrol, or RM239,215.68 per day. Only 20,136 civil servants are currently participating in the scheme, up from 15,452 at launch. The DG described the savings as “a meaningful contribution from civil servants in helping the government manage financial pressures.”

Malaysia’s Press Freedom Drops to 95th Under Fahmi’s Watch — Communications Minister Once Dismissed RSF as ‘Not the Gold Standard’

2026-04-30
#Politics 

Said by: Fahmi Fadzil, Communications Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-04-30

📌 What Happened

Malaysia dropped seven places to 95th out of 180 countries in the 2026 Reporters Without Borders (RSF) World Press Freedom Index, published on April 30. The country’s overall score fell from 56.09 to 52.73, with declines in four of five indicators. The political indicator — measuring media independence from political pressure — slipped from 44.66 to 43.00, while the economic indicator plunged from 48.67 to 40.28. Communications Minister Fahmi Fadzil, who oversees the MCMC and media policy, had previously dismissed the RSF index as “not the gold standard” when Malaysia dropped 34 spots to 107th in 2024.
