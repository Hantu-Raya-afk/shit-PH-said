2026-03-15
#Education 

Said by: Mohd Azam Ahmad, Education Director-General (under Education Minister Fadhlina Sidek)

Category: Education

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

On March 15, Education Director-General Datuk Dr Mohd Azam Ahmad announced the Ministry of Education will hold a special meeting next week, chaired by Education Minister Fadhlina Sidek, to review the implementation of Work From Home and Home-Based Teaching and Learning (PdPR) mechanisms as part of the government’s austerity response to the global oil crisis. When asked whether the savings policy would extend to schools and students — effectively reviving the COVID-era PdPR system — Mohd Azam said “further study is required” and that the ministry would “examine whether there is a rationale to involve schools.”

Category: Education

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

Deputy Education Minister Wong Kah Woh told reporters at a breaking-of-fast event at Taiping Lake Gardens on March 15 that the Ministry of Education will conduct a “comprehensive review” of PdPR — Teaching and Learning from Home — for school students and ministry civil servants. The DG acknowledged that the MOE would “leverage its experience with WFH during the Covid-19 pandemic” to develop a strategy.

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-14

2026-03-14
#Economy 

Said by: Johari Abdul Ghani, Investment, Trade and Industry Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-14

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Pada 14 Mac 2026, Menteri Pelaburan, Perdagangan dan Industri Datuk Seri Johari Abdul Ghani memberitahu wartawan bahawa kerajaan pada masa ini tidak merancang untuk memotong subsidi, tetapi mengakui ia hanya boleh ‘bertahan untuk dua atau tiga bulan seterusnya’ jika konflik Asia Barat terus menolak harga minyak melebihi AS$100 setong.
