📌 What Happened

Tourism, Arts and Culture Minister Datuk Seri Tiong King Sing on May 1 told critics of the Rain Rave Water Music Festival (RRWMF) to stop “politicising” the event, which has drawn objections from religious authorities, opposition parties, and even members of the ruling coalition.
