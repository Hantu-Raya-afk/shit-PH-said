Fahmi Delays WFH Decision for Civil Servants Until After Hari Raya — The Government That Told You to ‘Reduce Energy Usage’ Can’t Even Decide on Working From Home

2026-03-17
#Economy 

Said by: Fahmi Fadzil, Communications Minister (Government Spokesperson)

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-17

📌 What Happened

On March 17, Communications Minister Fahmi Fadzil announced after a post-Cabinet meeting that the government’s decision on implementing work-from-home for civil servants would be delayed until after Hari Raya Aidilfitri. This came just four days after FMT reported on March 13 that the decision was supposed to be made on Tuesday, March 17. Fahmi said “several aspects still need to be refined” and the matter would go to the National Economic Action Council (MTEN) via the Chief Secretary to the Government. He stressed frontline services like healthcare and education would not be affected.
