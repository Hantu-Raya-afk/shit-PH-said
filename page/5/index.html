📌 What Happened

On May 1, 2026, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim took the stage at the National Workers’ Day celebration at Unifi Arena, Bukit Jalil, to unveil the RM710 million PACE Economic Resilience Package — billed as a measure to “strengthen jobs, skills and wage resilience.” He acknowledged that oil prices had surged from US$70 to US$115 per barrel and pledged the government would “continue taking necessary measures” to face global economic uncertainty. 📌 What Happened

Health Minister Datuk Seri Dr Dzulkefly Ahmad revealed on May 1, 2026 that he expects a cut of approximately 10% to the Ministry of Health’s RM46.5 billion budget for 2026 — a figure significantly higher than the RM3.06 billion (6.6%) initially proposed by the Treasury in its April 29 directive.
