2026-01-08
#Politics 

Said by: Attorney-General’s Chambers

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-01-08

📌 WHAT HAPPENED: On 8 January 2026, the Attorney-General’s Chambers (AGC) ended all 47 criminal charges against Deputy Prime Minister Ahmad Zahid Hamidi, citing “insufficient evidence.” The charges — filed in 2018 after UMNO lost power — involved RM31 million from Yayasan Akalbudi, a charitable foundation Zahid controlled. [BM] Platform Media Sosial Dipaksa ‘Dianggap Berlesen’ — MCMC Ketatkan Cengkaman Internet

2026-01-01
#Social 

Said by: Fahmi Fadzil, Communications Minister

Category: Social

Date: 2026-01-01

📌 APA YANG BERLAKU: Mulai 1 Januari 2026, platform media sosial dan pemesejan segera dengan 8 juta atau lebih pengguna Malaysia secara automatik “dianggap berlesen” di bawah kerangka kawal selia MCMC — sama ada mereka memohon lesen atau tidak.
