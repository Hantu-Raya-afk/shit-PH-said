📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Bekas Menteri Ekonomi Datuk Seri Rafizi Ramli melapor ke ibu pejabat Suruhanjaya Pencegahan Rasuah Malaysia (SPRM) di Putrajaya pada 4 Mei untuk disoal siasat berhubung dakwaan penyelewengan dalam perjanjian pelaburan semikonduktor bernilai RM1.1 bilion antara Kementerian Ekonomi dan gergasi cip British, Arm Holdings. Anwar Promises Austerity Won’t Touch Healthcare — Days After His Own Treasury Proposed Slashing RM3 Billion From the Health Budget

2026-05-04
#Health 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister & Finance Minister

Category: Health

Date: 2026-05-04

📌 What Happened

Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim told a ministry assembly in Putrajaya on May 4 that austerity measures currently being rolled out across federal ministries will not affect “core programmes,” including critical healthcare services and the recruitment of new doctors and nurses. This came days after the Treasury issued a directive on April 29 ordering all ministries to review and cut their 2026 operating expenditures — with a suggested RM5.4 billion in cuts targeting the Health Ministry (RM3.06 billion from its RM46.5 billion allocation) and the Higher Education Ministry (RM2.39 billion). The cuts are driven by a subsidy bill that has ballooned from RM15 billion to a projected RM58.4 billion this year, as the Middle East conflict sends energy prices surging.

Rafizi Reports to MACC Over RM1.1B Arm Deal He Pushed as PH Economy Minister — Expects Charges, Calls Probe’s Timing Suspicious

2026-05-04
#Politics 

Said by: Rafizi Ramli, Former Economy Minister (PKR, Pandan MP)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-05-04

📌 What Happened

Former Economy Minister Datuk Seri Rafizi Ramli reported to the Malaysian Anti-Corruption Commission (MACC) headquarters in Putrajaya on May 4 for questioning over alleged irregularities in a RM1.1 billion semiconductor investment deal between the Economy Ministry and UK-based chip giant Arm Holdings. MACC has recorded 22 statements so far in the probe, which began on February 13. Rafizi, who quit the cabinet in mid-2025 and became one of PM Anwar Ibrahim’s most vocal critics, said on May 1 that he received a summons notice at 3pm requiring him to appear at 10am on Monday — and that he expects to be charged. The probe centres on whether Rafizi pressured the Cabinet to approve the deal during his tenure as Economy Minister.
