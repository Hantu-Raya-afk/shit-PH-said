#Politics 

Said by: Rafizi Ramli, Former Economy Minister (PKR, Pandan MP)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-05-01

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Rafizi Ramli, bekas Menteri Ekonomi dan Ahli Parlimen Pandan (PKR), mengumumkan pada 1 Mei bahawa beliau menerima saman SPRM untuk memberi keterangan di ibu pejabat SPRM Putrajaya pada hari Isnin jam 10 pagi. Beliau menolak siasatan ini sebagai bermotifkan politik, menyatakan aduan dibuat oleh Perkasa, sebuah NGO yang didakwanya “tidak pernah berminat dengan isu rasuah sebelum ini.”

#Economy 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-05-01

📌 What Happened

On May 1, 2026, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim took the stage at the National Workers’ Day celebration at Unifi Arena, Bukit Jalil, to unveil the RM710 million PACE Economic Resilience Package — billed as a measure to “strengthen jobs, skills and wage resilience.” He acknowledged that oil prices had surged from US$70 to US$115 per barrel and pledged the government would “continue taking necessary measures” to face global economic uncertainty. 2026-05-01
#Politics 

Said by: Rafizi Ramli, Former Economy Minister (PKR, Pandan MP)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-05-01

📌 What Happened

Rafizi Ramli, former Economy Minister and PKR’s Pandan MP, announced on May 1 that he received a MACC summons to give a statement at its Putrajaya headquarters on Monday at 10am. The investigation concerns the RM1.1 billion joint venture agreement between the Malaysian government and UK-based semiconductor firm Arm Holdings, signed in March 2025 during his tenure as minister. Rafizi said the probe focuses on whether he pressured Cabinet to sign the deal hastily, and that he expects possible charges including criminal breach of trust or providing misleading information. He dismissed the investigation as politically motivated, noting the complaint was lodged by Perkasa, an NGO he claims has “never previously shown interest in corruption issues.”
