Deputy Religious Affairs Minister Orders Public to Replace ‘LGBT’ With ‘Deviant Culture’ — PH’s Reform Government Now Policing Language

2026-02-26
#Social 

Said by: Marhamah Rosli, Deputy Minister in the Prime Minister’s Department (Religious Affairs)

Category: Social

Date: 2026-02-26

📌 What Happened

On February 26, 2026, Deputy Minister in the Prime Minister’s Department (Religious Affairs) Marhamah Rosli told the Dewan Negara that the public should stop using the term ‘LGBT’ and replace it with ‘deviant culture’ (budaya songsang). Responding to a question from Senator Hussin Ismail about government efforts to monitor private gatherings, Marhamah said the change was intended to “avoid normalisation” and “curb the expansion of related content algorithms on social media platforms.” She added it would ensure “consistent terminology in speech and writing, while avoiding indirect promotion.” The directive was framed as an immediate, government-wide policy change.

Fahmi Confirms MCMC Blocked Grindr and Blued — PH’s ‘Reform Government’ Now Runs Digital Censorship Against Minorities

2026-02-25
#Social 

Said by: Fahmi Fadzil, Communications Minister

Category: Social

Date: 2026-02-25

📌 What Happened

On February 25, 2026, Communications Minister Fahmi Fadzil confirmed in a written parliamentary reply that the Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission (MCMC) has blocked the websites of Grindr and Blued — two of the world’s largest dating and networking platforms used by gay and bisexual men. Fahmi said MCMC is now “studying several legislative measures” to curb the apps from Google Play and the Apple App Store, though he acknowledged that control over foreign-owned platforms is “subject to regulations and policies set by the said platform providers.” By early March, both apps were removed from Malaysian app stores entirely. Deputy Prime Minister Ahmad Zahid Hamidi separately announced a proposed Cyber Crime Bill to expand the government’s digital enforcement powers, including over “immoral” content.
