Said by: MalaysiaNow report

Category: Economy

Date: 2025-11-03

📌 WHAT HAPPENED: On 3 November 2025, MalaysiaNow reported that the United States had “declared victory against China” after securing a pledge from Anwar Ibrahim to not restrict exports of critical minerals and rare earth elements to the US.
