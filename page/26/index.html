Johari Confirms US-Malaysia Trade Deal Nullified — PH’s ‘Diplomatic Win’ Lasted Five Months

2026-03-15
#Economy 

Said by: Johari Abdul Ghani, Investment, Trade and Industry Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

On March 15, 2026, Investment, Trade and Industry Minister Johari Abdul Ghani confirmed that the Malaysia-US Agreement on Reciprocal Trade (ART), signed by Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim and US President Donald Trump in October 2025, has been nullified. “It is not on hold. It’s null and void,” Johari told reporters. The US Supreme Court ruled that Trump’s blanket tariffs were unlawful, striking down the tariff framework the ART was built on. The deal had committed Malaysia to providing “significant preferential market access” for US products across chemicals, machinery, vehicles, dairy, poultry, pork, rice, and fuel ethanol. In exchange, the US maintained a 19% reciprocal tariff on Malaysian goods, with zero rates only for selected products. Johari said the US is now relying on Section 122 of its Trade Act of 1974, allowing temporary tariffs of up to 10%. Four PKR MPs had earlier urged Putrajaya to suspend all ratification steps following the court ruling. Meanwhile, the US has launched Section 301 investigations into 60 countries, including Malaysia, over excess production capacity and forced labour.
