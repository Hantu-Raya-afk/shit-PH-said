High Court Rules Steven Sim Was ‘Clearly Biased’ in Bank Festive Aid Dispute — The ‘Workers’ Champion’ Sided With the Banks

2026-03-17
#Politics 

Said by: Steven Sim Chee Keong, Entrepreneur and Cooperatives Development Minister (former HR Minister)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-17

📌 What Happened

On March 17, 2026, the Kuala Lumpur High Court ruled that former Human Resources Minister Steven Sim Chee Keong failed to maintain neutrality when he intervened in a trade dispute between the Malayan Commercial Banks’ Association (MCBA) and the National Union of Bank Employees (NUBE) over 2024 Hari Raya festive aid payments. Justice Aliza Sulaiman found that Sim had met with MCBA representatives while the matter was undergoing conciliation at the Industrial Relations Department — after which the banks agreed to pay only a half-month bonus instead of the one-month payment the union had demanded. The judge said Sim had ‘improperly aligned his office with the association’ and ‘subverted the statutory process designed for impartial resolution.’ Sim, now Entrepreneur and Cooperatives Development Minister, and the MCBA have filed an appeal.
