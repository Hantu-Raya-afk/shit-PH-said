[BM] Guan Eng Dakwa ‘Durian Runtuh’ RM1.6 Bilion Dari Bon Samurai 2019 — Untungnya Nasib Forex, Bukan Kepakaran Dasar

2026-03-15
#Economy 

Said by: Lim Guan Eng, DAP Chairman and MP for Bagan

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Lim Guan Eng, Pengerusi DAP dan bekas Menteri Kewangan, mengumumkan pada 15 Mac bahawa Malaysia bakal meraih keuntungan RM1.6 bilion daripada bon samurai bernilai 200 bilion yen yang diterbitkan pada Mac 2019. Merujuk jawapan Parlimen daripada Perdana Menteri merangkap Menteri Kewangan Anwar Ibrahim bertarikh 2 Mac, Guan Eng berkata kejatuhan yen sebanyak 27.8% berbanding ringgit — dari RM3.62 setiap 100 yen ketika terbitan kepada RM2.59 pada Disember 2025 — telah mengurangkan nilai prinsipal bon daripada RM7.2 bilion kepada RM5.2 bilion. Selepas ditolak anggaran kos faedah RM400 juta, keuntungan pertukaran asing bersih adalah kira-kira RM1.6 bilion. Bon ini matang pada 2029.

📌 What Happened

Guan Eng Claims RM1.6 Billion ‘Windfall’ From 2019 Samurai Bond — The Profit Is Forex Luck, Not Policy Genius

2026-03-15
#Economy 

Said by: Lim Guan Eng, DAP Chairman and MP for Bagan

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

Lim Guan Eng, DAP chairman and former Finance Minister, announced on March 15 that Malaysia stands to record a RM1.6 billion profit from the 200 billion yen samurai bond he issued in March 2019. Citing a parliamentary reply from Prime Minister and Finance Minister Anwar Ibrahim dated March 2, Guan Eng said the yen’s 27.8% depreciation against the ringgit — from RM3.62 per 100 yen at issuance to RM2.59 by December 2025 — has reduced the bond’s principal value from RM7.2 billion to RM5.2 billion. After deducting an estimated RM400 million in interest costs, the net foreign exchange gain is approximately RM1.6 billion. The bond matures in 2029.
