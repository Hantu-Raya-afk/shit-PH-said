Rafizi Reports to MACC Over RM1.1B Arm Deal He Pushed as PH Economy Minister — Expects Charges, Calls Probe’s Timing Suspicious

2026-05-04
#Politics 

Said by: Rafizi Ramli, Former Economy Minister (PKR, Pandan MP)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-05-04

📌 What Happened#

Former Economy Minister Datuk Seri Rafizi Ramli reported to the Malaysian Anti-Corruption Commission (MACC) headquarters in Putrajaya on May 4 for questioning over alleged irregularities in a RM1.1 billion semiconductor investment deal between the Economy Ministry and UK-based chip giant Arm Holdings. MACC has recorded 22 statements so far in the probe, which began on February 13. Rafizi, who quit the cabinet in mid-2025 and became one of PM Anwar Ibrahim’s most vocal critics, said on May 1 that he received a summons notice at 3pm requiring him to appear at 10am on Monday — and that he expects to be charged. The probe centres on whether Rafizi pressured the Cabinet to approve the deal during his tenure as Economy Minister.

🔥 Why It Matters#

This is the first time a former PH senior minister faces a potential corruption prosecution over a deal made under the PH government itself. The RM1.1 billion Arm Holdings joint venture was a flagship Economy Ministry initiative under Rafizi’s watch — if it was irregular, that is a governance failure at the heart of PH’s economic agenda, signed off by PH’s own Cabinet. More critically, the probe’s timing — accelerating only after Rafizi broke ranks, lost the PKR deputy presidency, and became Anwar’s most prominent internal critic — raises uncomfortable questions about whether MACC is being deployed as a political weapon against dissent, the exact “corporate mafia” behaviour that has dogged the agency in recent months.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

PH came to power promising institutional reform and an end to political persecution through state agencies. Now a former PH minister who dared to criticize the PM is being hauled before the same anti-graft body that PH promised to make independent. If the probe is legitimate, it exposes that PH’s own Cabinet pushed through a billion-ringgit deal with serious governance flaws — on PH’s watch, with PH’s due diligence (or spectacular lack thereof). If it is political, then PH has become the thing it swore to destroy: a government that uses MACC as a tool of political control against inconvenient voices. Either way, PH loses. You cannot claim reformasi credentials while your anti-corruption agency conveniently targets the one ex-minister who turned critic.

📜 Previous Cases#

Malaysian politics has a long, ugly history of weaponizing anti-corruption agencies against political rivals. Anwar Ibrahim himself was twice prosecuted on charges widely seen as politically motivated — first under Mahathir in 1998, then under Najib in 2015. The MACC “corporate mafia” scandal — where the agency was accused of using its sweeping powers to freeze bank accounts and enable corporate manipulation — erupted just weeks ago, with the Straits Times reporting that Anwar chose to ride out the storm rather than call snap polls. Now the same agency is summoning the PM’s most vocal critic from within PH’s own ranks. The pattern is depressingly familiar across Malaysian administrations: dissent triggers investigation, investigation triggers charges, and the institution’s independence becomes a convenient fiction.

🎯 PH Callout#

You promised Reformasi. You promised an independent MACC. You promised that no government under Pakatan Harapan would use state institutions to silence critics. Rafizi was your Economy Minister — if the Arm deal was corrupt, why did PH’s own governance fail to catch it before signing? If the timing is coincidental, explain why 22 statements have been recorded and charges are expected only after Rafizi turned against the PM. Malaysia is watching. The answer “trust us, we’re different” stopped working the moment your anti-corruption agency started behaving exactly like it did under every previous administration. Either demonstrate MACC’s independence with transparent process, or admit that PH plays the same game as everyone who came before.
