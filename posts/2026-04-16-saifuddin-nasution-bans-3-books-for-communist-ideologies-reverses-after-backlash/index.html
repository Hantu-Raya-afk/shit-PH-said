Saifuddin Nasution Bans 3 Books for ‘Communist Ideologies,’ Reverses After Backlash — PH Has Banned 26 Books in 4 Months, More Than All of 2025

2026-04-16
#Politics 

Said by: Saifuddin Nasution Ismail, Home Minister (PKR)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-04-16

📌 What Happened#

On April 16, 2026, the Home Ministry under Minister Saifuddin Nasution Ismail banned two books — Memoir Shamsiah Fakeh: Dari Awas ke Rejimen Ke-10 and Komrad Asi (Rejimen 10): Dalam Denyut Nihilisme Sejarah — claiming they contained “communist ideologies” that threatened national security. Days later, a third book was banned: Mao Zedong: China dalam Dunia Abad ke-20, a Malay translation of an NYU academic work — funded by the PKR-led Selangor state government. After backlash from civil society, publishers, and even PH’s own MPs, Saifuddin announced on April 23 that the ministry was revoking the bans and would consult publishers before future prohibitions.

🔥 Why It Matters#

This is not an isolated incident — it is a pattern. Government data show 26 book titles were banned in the first four months of 2026 alone, surpassing the 23 books banned in all of 2025. Under Anwar Ibrahim’s supposedly reformist MADANI government, book bans have accelerated, not slowed. The ban-then-reverse cycle on these three books exposed a government that either does not know what its own ministries are doing, or one that tests authoritarian waters and retreats only when the backlash gets loud enough. Either way, it shreds whatever remains of PH’s reformist credentials on free expression and intellectual freedom.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

Anwar Ibrahim built his brand on being the intellectual reformer — the university lecturer, the man of ideas, the antidote to BN’s authoritarianism. Now his own Home Ministry is banning academic books faster than any government in recent Malaysian history. Worse: one of the banned books was literally published with funding from PH’s own Selangor state government. The left hand bans what the right hand paid for. And when PH’s own PKR MP Tan Kar Hing reminded Anwar of his lecturing days, the absurdity was complete. You cannot credibly call yourself MADANI — “civil” — while your ministry treats history books like seditious pamphlets.

📜 Previous Cases#

Malaysia’s book-banning apparatus is nothing new — the Printing Presses and Publications Act has been a censorship tool since 1984. But PH promised to be different. In opposition, PH politicians routinely criticized BN’s censorship. DW reported that 24 books were banned in 2025 alone, already a sharp increase, with nearly half targeting LGBTQ+ themes. The trend under PH has been one of escalation, not reform. Article 19, the international press freedom organization, called on Malaysia to reverse the bans and repeal the Act entirely — the same demand civil society made under BN.

🎯 PH Callout#

You told Malaysians you were bringing reform, openness, and a civil society worthy of the name MADANI. Instead, you are banning books at a pace that would make BN Home Ministers blush. Saifuddin’s retreat does not erase the fact that his ministry’s first instinct was to ban, intimidate, and investigate a publisher. If your idea of reform is to censor first and apologize later, then MADANI is just a slogan on a poster. Repeal the Printing Presses and Publications Act. That was the promise. Deliver it or stop pretending.
