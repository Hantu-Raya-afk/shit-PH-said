Deputy Religious Affairs Minister Orders Public to Replace ‘LGBT’ With ‘Deviant Culture’ — PH’s Reform Government Now Policing Language

2026-02-26
#Social 

Said by: Marhamah Rosli, Deputy Minister in the Prime Minister’s Department (Religious Affairs)

Category: Social

Date: 2026-02-26

📌 What Happened#

On February 26, 2026, Deputy Minister in the Prime Minister’s Department (Religious Affairs) Marhamah Rosli told the Dewan Negara that the public should stop using the term ‘LGBT’ and replace it with ‘deviant culture’ (budaya songsang). Responding to a question from Senator Hussin Ismail about government efforts to monitor private gatherings, Marhamah said the change was intended to “avoid normalisation” and “curb the expansion of related content algorithms on social media platforms.” She added it would ensure “consistent terminology in speech and writing, while avoiding indirect promotion.” The directive was framed as an immediate, government-wide policy change.

🔥 Why It Matters#

This is a sitting PH government minister using state authority to dictate how Malaysians refer to a minority group — and the term she chose is explicitly designed to dehumanise. ‘Deviant culture’ is not a neutral descriptor; it is a label that frames an entire community as aberrant and dangerous. This comes from a government that ran on a reform platform, promised to defend civil liberties, and positioned itself as a break from BN-era authoritarianism. Instead, a PH deputy minister is issuing language directives from the Dewan Negara floor that would make JAKIM bureaucrats proud. This is institutional stigmatisation dressed up as algorithmic hygiene.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

The ‘algorithm’ justification is transparently absurd. Renaming ‘LGBT’ to ‘deviant culture’ does not change social media algorithms — it changes the framing of public discourse to make discrimination more palatable. Amnesty International Malaysia condemned the move, saying it has “nothing to do with upholding morality” but rather “legitimising discrimination against an already vulnerable community.” Sisters in Islam called it “a political act, not a minor semantic adjustment.” And Marhamah’s own logic collapses on contact: if the goal is to stop ’normalisation,’ then using a government platform to discuss the topic at length — and to issue a national directive about it — does the exact opposite. PH didn’t stop talking about LGBT people. They just gave them a worse name.

📜 Previous Cases#

This continues a pattern of the Madani government using religious state apparatus to target minorities. In January 2026, Religious Affairs Minister Zulkifli Hasan caused international controversy by linking work stress to ‘LGBT behaviour’ in a parliamentary written reply — drawing ridicule from health professionals, civil society, and international media. Under the Madani government, the religious affairs portfolio has become indistinguishable from its BN predecessors — same rhetoric, same targets, same institutional machinery. PH’s 2018 promise of “a Malaysia for all” has quietly been shelved alongside the IPCMC and every other reform that required political courage.

🎯 PH Callout#

You came to power promising Malaysia that Madani meant dignity, reform, and a break from the politics of division. Now your deputy minister is standing in Parliament directing Malaysians to call their fellow citizens ‘deviants.’ This is not governance. This is not reform. This is a PH government using the same religious state machinery it once criticised, targeting the same people BN and PAS targeted, with the same dehumanising language. The only difference is the coalition logo on the letterhead. If ‘Malaysia Madani’ means anything, it cannot mean a government that officially classifies its own citizens as deviant. Drop the directive, or drop the pretence.
