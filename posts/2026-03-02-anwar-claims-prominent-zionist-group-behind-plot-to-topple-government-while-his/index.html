Anwar Claims 'Prominent Zionist Group' Behind Plot to Topple Government — While His Own Party Once Lobbied Washington

Hantu Raya

March 2, 2026

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister and PKR President

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-02

📌 What Happened

On March 2, 2026, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim told Parliament that an alleged plot to topple his government involved a “prominent Zionist group,” foreign lobbyists, bankers, and international media. He claimed the conspiracy was driven by those targeted in large-scale MACC corruption investigations and was planned to run from August 2024 until the next general election. The claim came amid an ongoing police probe into allegations that the widow of late former finance minister Daim Zainuddin was working with foreign agents. Anwar specifically accused the plotters of using contacts among parliamentarians and mobilising networks at Bloomberg to praise corruption suspects.

🔥 Why It Matters

This is the Prime Minister of a reformist government using “Zionist” as a political weapon in Parliament — the same Parliament where his own coalition just failed to pass its flagship reform bill. The allegation shifts public attention from MACC’s growing credibility crisis and the government’s own governance failures onto a shadowy foreign conspiracy. It also sets a dangerous precedent: labelling domestic political opponents as foreign agents backed by Zionists, a trope with deep resonance in Malaysian politics, legitimises security crackdowns on dissent and media scrutiny.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit

Anwar spent decades in opposition cultivating foreign support — including from Washington think tanks, Western media, and international human rights networks — to pressure Malaysian governments. PKR’s own 2016 Washington project, which sought to build US pressure on Putrajaya, resurfaced almost immediately after the Parliament speech. The hypocrisy is industrial-grade: when Anwar needed foreign media and lobbyists, it was democratic solidarity; when his opponents allegedly do the same, it’s a Zionist conspiracy to destabilise the nation. MalaysiaNow condemned the claim as an “outright lie.” The Daim family sued, calling the police investigation unlawful, unconstitutional, and tainted by bad faith.

📜 Previous Cases

Malaysian leaders have a long history of invoking foreign conspiracies to deflect domestic criticism. Mahathir blamed George Soros during the 1997 Asian financial crisis. Najib Razak blamed foreign interference when 1MDB allegations mounted. Anwar himself was the target of conspiracy-tinged prosecutions during his sodomy trials. Now in power, he’s deploying the same playbook — vague allegations of foreign plots, security investigations into critics, and politically convenient timing. The pattern is consistent across Malaysian administrations: when the heat rises at home, find an external bogeyman.

🎯 PH Callout

You promised reformasi. You promised transparency, accountability, and an end to the politics of fear. Instead, your Prime Minister stood in Parliament and blamed Zionists for his political problems — a move straight from the authoritarian playbook you spent 25 years fighting against. If the evidence is real, table it. If the plot is genuine, let the courts handle it with full due process. But waving “Zionist” in Parliament while your own MACC chief faces RM14 million in unexplained shares, while your reform bills collapse, while your coalition frays — that’s not leadership. That’s deflection. And Malaysians can see right through it.
