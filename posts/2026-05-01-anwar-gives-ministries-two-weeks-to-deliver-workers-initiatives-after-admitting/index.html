Anwar Gives Ministries Two Weeks to Deliver Workers’ Initiatives — After Admitting His Own Government Takes ‘Six Months, a Year or Even Longer’

2026-05-01
#Economy 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister & Finance Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-05-01

📌 What Happened#

On May 1, 2026, at the national Workers’ Day celebration at Unifi Arena, Bukit Jalil — attended by 15,000 workers — Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim announced a RM710 million PACE Economic Resilience Package and a Post-Maternity Leave Allowance, then gave all relevant agencies a two-week deadline to submit implementation reports to their ministers. “I do not want a repeat of past practices where announcements are made, but implementation takes six months, a year or even longer,” he said. Within three weeks, the reports must reach the Cabinet.

🔥 Why It Matters#

This is not a bold move. This is an admission. After 2.5 years in power, the Prime Minister is publicly acknowledging — on a stage, in front of 15,000 workers — that his own government has a chronic implementation problem. The fact that he has to explicitly demand that announcements be followed by action tells you everything about the gap between PH rhetoric and PH delivery. If your government needed 2.5 years to figure out that announcements without implementation are worthless, the problem isn’t the bureaucracy. It’s the leadership.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

Anwar has made this exact kind of “no more delays” demand before — multiple times. Every few months, there’s a new speech about urgency, a new deadline, a new promise that “this time” the government will deliver. The MADANI framework was supposed to fix this. The Cabinet reshuffles were supposed to fix this. The KPI system was supposed to fix this. And yet here he is, in May 2026, telling his own ministers to stop sitting on announcements. If the PM can’t get his own Cabinet to implement policy within a reasonable timeframe, what exactly is the point of the two-week deadline? Who enforces it? What happens when it’s missed? Nothing. Because it’s always nothing.

📜 Previous Cases#

This pattern is deeply embedded in the Anwar administration. In 2024, he announced the Gig Workers Social Security initiative — implementation lagged for months. The MADANI Economy framework was launched with great fanfare but independent assessments repeatedly flagged delayed rollouts. The promised separation of the Attorney General and Public Prosecutor roles took years of “we’ll do it soon” before finally failing in Parliament in March 2026. Malaysia’s political tradition of announcing policies at rallies and letting them die in committee meetings didn’t start with Anwar, but he was supposed to end it.

🎯 PH Callout#

You stood on that stage and told 15,000 workers you won’t tolerate delays anymore. But you are the delay. You lead this government. You chair the Cabinet. If announcements take six months to become action, that is your failure — not the bureaucracy’s, not the opposition’s, yours. Stop announcing deadlines for implementing your announcements. Start announcing results. Workers don’t need another speech about urgency. They need a government that treats delivery as the default, not the exception.
