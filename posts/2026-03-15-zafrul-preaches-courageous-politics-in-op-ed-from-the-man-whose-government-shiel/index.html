Zafrul Preaches ‘Courageous Politics’ in Op-Ed — From the Man Whose Government Shields MACC Chief and Failed Its Own Reforms

2026-03-15
#Politics 

Said by: Tengku Zafrul Abdul Aziz, Senior Political Advisor to the Prime Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened#

Tengku Zafrul Abdul Aziz, Senior Political Advisor to the Prime Minister, published an opinion column in The Edge Malaysia’s March 16–22 edition titled Can Malaysian politics unite the country when the world is coming apart? In it, he called for ‘courageous politics’ — ’the courage to speak honestly to the rakyat,’ ’the courage to make tough but unpopular decisions,’ and ’the courage to reject divisive politics for short-term gain.’ He warned that ‘Malaysia cannot afford the luxury of self-indulgent partisanship’ and argued that social cohesion is ‘fundamental to economic strength.’

🔥 Why It Matters#

This is the Prime Minister’s own senior political advisor writing a 1,500-word lecture on political courage, unity, and trust — directed at nobody in particular and everyone by implication. It matters because it reads as a sermon from a man whose own government has, in the space of three weeks, (a) shielded MACC chief Azam Baki from immediate accountability by routing a shareholding scandal through a disciplinary board instead of suspension, (b) lost its own PM term limit bill because eight government MPs didn’t bother showing up, (c) tripled the blanket RON95 subsidy to RM2 billion instead of implementing the targeted subsidy reform it promised for three years, and (d) had police interrogate the CEO of C4, an anti-corruption NGO, under Section 124B. The gap between what Zafrul preaches and what his government practises is not a crack — it’s a canyon.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

Zafrul writes that ‘without trust, even good policies fall victim to cynicism’ and ’needed reforms become impossible to implement.’ He is describing his own government’s predicament and blaming the rakyat’s cynicism rather than the government’s performance. He invokes his time at multiracial Bukit Bintang Boys School and his BAM presidency to burnish his credentials as a unity figure — as though personal biography substitutes for policy delivery. The man who told the public the economy was ‘on firm footing’ days ago while KLCI crashed below 1700 is now lecturing the nation about the importance of trust and honest leadership. This is not courage. This is reputation management dressed as statesmanship.

📜 Previous Cases#

Zafrul has form. As Finance Minister under Muhyiddin’s Perikatan Nasional government, he oversaw the controversial RM530 billion LAKSANA economic stimulus packages that were criticised for poor targeting and opaque disbursement. He crossed the floor to Barisan Nasional, then joined Anwar’s unity government — a political journey that itself undermines his own call for politicians to reject ’narrow interests.’ His March 15 statement that Malaysia’s economy is ‘still strong’ — issued on the same day as this op-ed — mirrors the same pattern of reassurance without substance that has defined his public communications since 2020.

🎯 PH Callout#

You promised reformasi. You promised institutional reform. You promised targeted subsidies and anti-corruption. Three years in, your own senior advisor is reduced to writing op-eds begging for ‘courageous politics’ because your government won’t practise it. Zafrul asks: ‘Can Malaysian politics unite the country when the world is coming apart?’ The real question is: can PH unite its own coalition when its reforms keep falling apart? Eight of your own MPs couldn’t be bothered to show up for the term limit vote. Your MACC chief still has his job. Your subsidy reform is dead. And your answer is a newspaper column about badminton and school friends. The rakyat doesn’t need more sermons about courage — they need a government that actually demonstrates it.
