Anwar Claims ‘Zionist Plot’ to Topple Government in Parliament — Daim’s Widow’s Lawyers Call It an ‘Outright Lie’

2026-03-03
#Politics 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-03

📌 What Happened#

On March 3, 2026, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim told Parliament that “prominent Zionist groups” and “bankers” were involved in a communications plan allegedly designed to destabilise Malaysia and undermine national institutions before the next election. Anwar made the claim in response to a question about a MalaysiaNow report revealing that he and senior PKR leaders had themselves engaged a US consultant in 2016 to lobby the White House to pressure then-PM Najib Razak. Instead of addressing those decade-old emails, Anwar pivoted to a PR plan prepared for Na’imah Abdul Khalid, widow of former Finance Minister Daim Zainuddin, and alleged it contained a Zionist conspiracy. Na’imah’s lawyers, Rajesh Nagarajan and Sachpreetraj Singh, issued a statement the same day calling Anwar’s claims “false, preposterous, outrageous, and a blatant attempt to mislead Parliament and the public.”

🔥 Why It Matters#

This is the Prime Minister of Malaysia using parliamentary privilege to make an incendiary, unsubstantiated claim about a Zionist conspiracy — inside the Dewan Rakyat, where he cannot be sued. The timing is critical: Anwar was cornered by revelations about PKR’s own history of engaging foreign consultants for political pressure. Rather than address the hypocrisy, he weaponised the most emotionally charged accusation available in Malaysian politics. The lawyers’ rebuttal was devastating: “There is not one word about Zionism or Israel or anything related to it in the entire communications plan.” They challenged Anwar to repeat the claim outside Parliament, where he would not enjoy immunity. This comes from a prime minister who danced with Donald Trump two weeks after the US president pledged full support to Netanyahu, and who faced over 300 police reports for signing a controversial trade deal with the US.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

Anwar Ibrahim — the man who welcomed Trump to Malaysia, signed a lopsided trade deal with Washington, and waved away anti-Israel protesters — suddenly discovered Zionists when he needed a distraction from his own party’s foreign lobbying history. The PR plan he cited was, according to the lawyers who drafted it, a routine business communications plan to “reassure bankers” of the Daim family’s financial viability. The only references to “bankers” in the entire document were about creditworthiness reassurance. Anwar took a mundane corporate PR exercise and turned it into a national security threat on the floor of Parliament. If this is what passes for evidence of a plot to topple the government, the real conspiracy is against the intelligence of every Malaysian watching.

📜 Previous Cases#

Anwar has a well-documented pattern of invoking foreign conspiracies when politically cornered. In February 2026, police launched an investigation under Section 124B — activities detrimental to parliamentary democracy — against critics and activists, framing domestic dissent as a foreign-backed plot. The “topple the government” narrative has been deployed repeatedly: against Daim’s family, against civil society groups, against academics. This is the same playbook Najib used — and the same playbook Anwar’s own coalition spent a decade condemning. Meanwhile, PKR’s 2016 engagement with a US consultant to pressure the Najib government — revealed the day before Anwar’s Zionist claim — remains unaddressed. The double standard is breathtaking: when PKR lobbied Washington, it was fighting corruption; when critics engage a PR firm, it’s a Zionist conspiracy.

🎯 PH Callout#

You ran on transparency. You ran on reformasi. You ran on ending the politics of distraction and fear. And here is your prime minister, in the Dewan Rakyat, invoking “Zionists” to dodge a question about his own party’s foreign lobbying history. Na’imah’s lawyers dared Anwar to repeat the claim outside Parliament. He won’t, because he knows it’s indefensible without immunity. You promised Malaysia a government that deals in facts, not manufactured hysteria. This is the opposite. If there is a genuine plot to destabilise the country, present the evidence to the public — not a fairy tale about Zionists crafted to survive a single news cycle. The Dewan Rakyat is not your personal distraction machine.
