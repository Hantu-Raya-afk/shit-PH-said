Treasury Orders RM10 Billion in Budget Cuts — Health and Education Take the Biggest Hit While Subsidy Bill Explodes From RM5B to RM58.4B

2026-04-29
#Economy 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister & Finance Minister; Johan Mahmood Merican, Treasury Secretary-General

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-04-29

📌 What Happened#

On April 29, 2026, Treasury Secretary-General Johan Mahmood Merican issued a government directive ordering all federal ministries and agencies to cut their operating budgets for 2026, citing the impact of rising energy prices from the Middle East conflict. The directive proposed a staggering RM10 billion in total cuts, with the Health Ministry taking the biggest hit at RM3.06 billion and the Higher Education Ministry at RM2.39 billion. The reason: the government’s subsidy bill had exploded from the RM5 billion originally approved in Budget 2026 to a projected RM58.4 billion — a 1,068% overrun — driven by rising global oil prices. Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim, who doubles as Finance Minister, is directly responsible for the fiscal policy that budgeted RM5 billion for subsidies while maintaining expensive universal subsidy programmes that were supposed to have been reformed.

🔥 Why It Matters#

Health and education are not fat to be trimmed — they are the foundation of a government’s social contract with its people. SUHAKAM, Malaysia’s human rights commission, warned that cutting healthcare spending “during periods of economic pressure” has “long-term consequences for public health outcomes.” The MADANI government promised to strengthen public services, invest in human capital, and protect the B40. Instead, the first things on the chopping block when fiscal reality bites are hospitals and universities. This is not fiscal discipline — this is a government that promised everything, budgeted for nothing, and is now making the rakyat pay for its miscalculations.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

Anwar budgeted RM5 billion for subsidies in a world where Middle East tensions were already escalating and oil prices were volatile. Any competent Finance Minister would have built contingency buffers. Instead, the actual subsidy cost ballooned to RM58.4 billion — nearly twelve times the original budget — and now the solution is to gut health and education spending. This is the same government that tabled a “record RM419.2 billion” budget in October 2025 with great fanfare about “transformation” and “inclusive growth.” Six months later, they are slashing essential services because they got the most basic fiscal projection catastrophically wrong. The rich got their targeted subsidies; the rakyat get fewer hospital beds and underfunded universities.

📜 Previous Cases#

Malaysian governments have a habit of raiding social spending when fiscal crises hit. Under Najib, 1MDB-related fiscal pressures led to GST implementation and subsidy rationalisation that hurt the B40. Under Muhyiddin, COVID spending was paired with cuts to non-essential services. PH criticised both governments for putting fiscal management ahead of people. Now PH is doing the same thing — except they also promised to rationalise subsidies in a targeted way, which they delayed for political reasons, and are now paying the price. The subsidy bill explosion is a direct consequence of PH’s refusal to implement the fuel subsidy reform they themselves designed.

🎯 PH Callout#

You are the Finance Minister, Dato’ Seri. You wrote this budget. You approved the RM5 billion subsidy allocation knowing full well that global oil prices could spike. You delayed subsidy reform because it was politically inconvenient. And now you are cutting RM3 billion from healthcare and RM2.4 billion from higher education to cover the gap. You promised the rakyat a government that puts people first. Cutting health and education spending while subsidy costs explode twelve-fold is not “Malaysia MADANI” — it is fiscal negligence with human consequences. Fix the subsidy framework or admit the MADANI budget was fiction from day one.
