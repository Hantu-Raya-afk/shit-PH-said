Mat Sabu Tells Malaysians to ‘Spend Prudently’ as Food Prices Set to Spike — After 2.5 Years, His Food Security Plan Is Still ‘Eat Less’

2026-05-03
#Economy 

Said by: Mohamad Sabu, Agriculture and Food Security Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-05-03

📌 What Happened#

Agriculture and Food Security Minister Mohamad Sabu, on May 3, 2026, acknowledged that cost pressures in the food supply chain may push prices higher in the coming months. Rather than announcing new interventions or structural reforms, Mat Sabu’s prescription for Malaysians was to “spend prudently, avoid waste, and prioritise essential needs.” This came just days after Economy Minister Akmal Nasrullah confirmed that food input costs were already rising — chicken up 3.3%, eggs up 7.3%, and vegetables climbing — and weeks after traders warned that prices could surge by as much as 50% due to soaring fuel costs linked to the Iran conflict.

🔥 Why It Matters#

Malaysia’s food security minister is effectively telling the rakyat that prices will go up and there is nothing he can do about it except offer lifestyle advice. This is not a policy response — it is a surrender dressed as reassurance. The government has had months to prepare buffer mechanisms, expand local food production, or roll out targeted subsidies. Instead, the message from the minister whose entire portfolio is food security boils down to: trust us and eat less. This is the same government that promised MADANI would deliver a more resilient and equitable economy.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

Mat Sabu has been Agriculture Minister for over two years. In that time, Malaysia has lurched from one food supply crisis to the next — chicken shortages, egg price spikes, and now an energy-driven cost spiral that threatens a 50% price surge. His response each time has been the same: reassure, deflect, advise. “Don’t panic buy.” “Supplies are sufficient.” “Spend prudently.” These are not policy positions — they are PR holding lines. The man responsible for making sure Malaysians can afford to eat is telling them to budget better. That is not governance. That is a financial literacy seminar from a minister who has run out of ideas.

📜 Previous Cases#

This is not the first time Mat Sabu has offered platitudes instead of plans. In March 2026, as diesel prices surged 77% and traders warned of imminent food price hikes, he told Malaysians not to panic buy and assured that rice stockpiles would last nine months. In April 2026, the Economy Minister flagged emerging price pressures across the food chain. Mat Sabu’s response has been consistently reactive — waiting for crises to arrive rather than building resilience in advance. Malaysia’s food security infrastructure remains heavily import-dependent, and no structural reform has materialised under his watch.

🎯 PH Callout#

PH promised a government that would protect the rakyat from the squeeze of rising costs. “Kerajaan yang menjaga rakyat,” remember? Two and a half years later, your Agriculture Minister’s best advice is “spend prudently.” That is not reform. That is resignation. If Mat Sabu cannot deliver a credible food security strategy — one that goes beyond hope, assurances, and budgeting tips — then PH needs to find someone who can. The rakyat did not vote for a government that tells them to eat less when times get hard. They voted for one that would make sure the food stays on the table.
