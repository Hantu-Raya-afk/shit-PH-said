Amir Hamzah Rules Out Stimulus Package Amid Oil Crisis — Government’s Plan Is to ‘Wait and See’

2026-03-16
#Economy 

Said by: Amir Hamzah Azizan, Finance Minister II

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-16

📌 What Happened#

On March 16, Finance Minister II Datuk Seri Amir Hamzah Azizan told reporters after launching the Malaysia Co-Investment Fund engagement day in Kuala Lumpur that the government will not introduce an economic stimulus package despite the escalating West Asia conflict. His reasoning: “We do not yet know whether the crisis in West Asia will continue or not. If it does not continue, I think there is no need to implement something like that.” His stated approach: “Look at the short term and ensure the stability that we have.” He confirmed the government is now spending RM3.2 billion a month on fuel subsidies — RM2 billion on petrol and RM1.2 billion on diesel — and encouraged the public and businesses to “reduce energy use.”

🔥 Why It Matters#

This is the government’s senior finance official confirming, on the record, that there is no contingency plan beyond hoping the war stops. Global oil has topped $100/barrel. The Strait of Hormuz — carrying 20% of the world’s oil — is under threat. The KLCI has crashed below 1700. Monthly subsidy bills have ballooned from roughly RM700 million to RM3.2 billion. And the Finance Minister’s answer is: we’ll see. No stimulus. No structural response. No hedging. Just short-termism dressed as prudence. This is a government that spent three years promising fiscal reform and targeted subsidies — and when the first real external shock arrives, the only plan is to wait it out.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

Every crisis catches this government flat-footed, and every time the response is the same: monitor, review, wait. The special Cabinet meeting on March 13 produced a WFH review deferred to March 17. The economy minister discovered energy transition on March 15 — three years late. And now the finance minister says there’s no stimulus because the crisis might end. What if it doesn’t? What’s Plan B? The answer, apparently, is to tell Malaysians to use less electricity. That’s not fiscal policy — that’s a parent telling the kids to turn off the lights. Meanwhile, RM3.2 billion is walking out the door every month with no reform mechanism, no timeline, and no exit strategy. The entire Madani fiscal framework has been reduced to a prayer that Brent crude comes back down.

📜 Previous Cases#

This is the same Amir Hamzah who told Malaysians not to “speculate” about fuel supplies on March 13, then declared the economy “expected to remain strong” on the same day the KLCI crashed below 1700. Two days earlier, his ministry tripled the RON95 subsidy to RM2 billion — the very blanket subsidy PH spent three years promising to replace with targeted transfers. The pattern is unmistakable: deny, delay, default to blanket spending. When BN did this, PH called it “fiscal irresponsibility.” When Madani does it, it’s called “short-term stability.”

🎯 PH Callout#

You had three years to build fiscal shock absorbers. You had three years to implement the targeted subsidy mechanism you campaigned on. You had three years to diversify energy dependence and build a credible contingency framework. You did none of it. And now, when the shock arrives, your Finance Minister tells the country to wait and see. This is not prudence — it is the absence of planning. The rakyat deserves better than a government whose crisis strategy is inshallah the war ends soon. If it doesn’t, you will own every ringgit of pain that follows.
