Tiong King Sing Tells Critics to ‘Stop Politicising’ Government-Backed Water Rave — After His Own Ministry Drew PM’s Wrath Over Alcohol

2026-05-01
#Social 

Said by: Tiong King Sing, Tourism, Arts and Culture Minister

Category: Social

Date: 2026-05-01

📌 What Happened#

On May 1, 2026, Tourism, Arts and Culture Minister Datuk Seri Tiong King Sing dismissed mounting criticism of the Rain Rave Water Music Festival 2026, held the previous night at Bukit Bintang under the Visit Malaysia 2026 campaign. Tiong told critics to “stop politicising” the event, claimed over 50,000 people attended, boasted about getting soaked on stage, and insisted the festival “is not Songkran” — while pointing to what he called a “double standard” because a Songkran celebration in Kedah on April 18 faced no similar backlash. He confirmed he had received a memorandum from critics calling for his resignation over the festival.

🔥 Why It Matters#

A federal minister using taxpayer-backed tourism funds to stage a water rave in the capital, then dismissing all criticism as “politicising,” signals a government that has confused spectacle with substance. The festival drew objections from the Federal Territories Islamic Religious Department (JAWI), which said the event could affect moral standards and social norms. Multiple civil society groups and members of the public also raised concerns. Tiong’s response — deflection, comparison to an unrelated event in Kedah, and a claim that critics are being unfair — is the signature PH move: rebrand criticism as sabotage, reframe accountability as politicisation.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

The “stop politicising” defence is the laziest shield in Malaysian politics. A government minister backing a government-funded event under a government tourism campaign doesn’t get to pretend the event is apolitical. The Kedah comparison is a red herring: JAWI didn’t flag Songkran — it flagged this specific event for its format and context. Tiong’s boast about “50,000” attendees and his stage antics aren’t evidence of success — they’re evidence that a Cabinet minister is more interested in playing emcee than governing. This is the same minister Anwar himself publicly rebuked in October 2025 for allowing alcohol at an official tourism gala dinner. Nothing was learned. Nothing changed.

📜 Previous Cases#

Tiong has form. In October 2025, PM Anwar warned him and his ministry after alcohol was served at a Global Travel Meet gala dinner — an incident Anwar called “entirely inappropriate.” Before that, Tiong’s aggressive personal style and repeated controversies made him one of the most polarising figures in Anwar’s Cabinet. The broader pattern is a PH government that talks about “Madani” values — compassion, respect, civil governance — while its own ministers treat public accountability like an inconvenience. Dismissing criticism as “politicising” is now standard operating procedure for this administration, deployed every time a minister is caught doing something embarrassing.

🎯 PH Callout#

You ran on reformasi. You promised Malaysia Madani — civility, accountability, respect for institutions. Now your Tourism Minister is getting soaked on stage at a government-sponsored rave, dismissing legitimate public concern as political noise, and telling people who filed a resignation memorandum to mind their own business. If a BN minister had done this in 2017, PH would have turned it into a three-week campaign issue. Fix the double standard, or stop claiming you have one.
