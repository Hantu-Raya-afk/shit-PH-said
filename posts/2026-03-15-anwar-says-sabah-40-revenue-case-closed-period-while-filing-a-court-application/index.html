Anwar Says Sabah 40% Revenue ‘Case Closed, Period’ — While Filing a Court Application to Reopen Parts of the Judgment

2026-03-15
#Politics 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened#

At a Madani Iftar event at Universiti Malaysia Sabah on March 15, Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim declared that Sabah’s 40 per cent revenue entitlement under the Malaysia Agreement 1963 is “final, case closed” and that there will be “no appeal, no negotiations, period.” In the same speech, he explained that the federal government is going to court — not to appeal the 40 per cent, he insists, but to “seek clarification” on parts of the High Court judgment that he says unfairly questioned the roles of past leaders including Tunku Abdul Rahman, Tun Abdul Razak, and Sabah stalwarts like Tun Fuad Stephens and Tan Sri Harris Salleh. A stay of execution application is scheduled for hearing at the Court of Appeal on March 31. The Sabah government has indicated it will oppose the stay.

🔥 Why It Matters#

The High Court ruled on October 17, 2025 that Sabah is entitled to 40 per cent of net federal revenue derived from the state for the period between 1974 and 2021 — decades of underpayment often called the “Lost Years.” The court set a 180-day deadline expiring April 15, 2026 for the federal government to act. Instead of simply honouring the ruling, Putrajaya is filing court applications that — regardless of how they are framed — create legal uncertainty, consume time, and delay the actual disbursement of what is owed. The distinction between “appeal” and “seeking clarification” is the kind of legal hairsplitting that erodes trust in a government that promised a new deal for Sabah.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

“Case closed. Period.” — followed immediately by “but we’re going to court.” If the case is closed, why does the government need a courtroom? Anwar’s explanation — that the judgment unfairly questioned past leaders — is a convenient sideshow. The judgment’s commentary on historical figures is not the operative part of the ruling; the 40 per cent entitlement is. By filing for “clarification” and a stay of execution, the federal government is doing exactly what it says it is not doing: buying time and creating legal grounds to delay payment. Sabahans have heard decades of promises about MA63 rights. They know what “clarification” means when it comes from Putrajaya. It means: not yet.

📜 Previous Cases#

Every federal government since 1974 has found reasons to defer Sabah’s revenue entitlement. BN did it through outright denial. PH 1.0 promised a special committee and delivered nothing before the Sheraton Move. The Muhyiddin and Ismail Sabri governments kicked the can further. Now the Madani government, armed with a court ruling and a deadline, is filing court applications while insisting the matter is “settled.” The pattern is not partisan — it is structural. Putrajaya does not want to pay, regardless of who sits in the PM’s office.

🎯 PH Callout#

You promised Sabah a new deal. You promised MA63 would be honoured. The High Court agreed and gave you a deadline. Your response? “Case closed, period” — and a court filing. Sabahans are not stupid. They can see the difference between words at an iftar event and actions in a courtroom. If you mean what you say, withdraw the stay application, honour the deadline, and start paying. Otherwise, spare Sabah the speeches. They’ve heard enough.
