Zafrul Says Malaysia 'On Firm Footing' Amid Oil Crisis — While Telling Critics to Stop 'Misleading' the Public

Hantu Raya

March 15, 2026

Said by: Tengku Zafrul Abdul Aziz, Senior Political Advisor to the Prime Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

On Sunday, March 15, Tengku Datuk Seri Zafrul Abdul Aziz — Senior Political Advisor to the Prime Minister — told reporters at a Singgah Sahur event at Ampang Jaya district police headquarters that Malaysia’s economy remains ‘on firm footing’ despite the West Asia crisis. He said the government has taken ‘strong, proactive fiscal measures,’ pointed to RON95 staying at RM1.99 under Budi95, and then warned ‘certain parties’ not to ‘mislead the public or politicise these issues.’ He added: ‘Compared to other countries, we are in a stronger position to face these challenges.’

🔥 Why It Matters

This is the third senior government figure in three days to tell Malaysians the economy is fine while every indicator says otherwise. The KLCI crashed below 1700 on March 13. The monthly RON95 subsidy bill has ballooned from RM700 million to RM3.2 billion. Cabinet just announced austerity measures including cancelling Raya open houses and cutting foreign trips. Zafrul’s messaging isn’t reassurance — it’s a pattern of government figures using ‘don’t politicise’ as a shield against legitimate scrutiny of a fiscal crisis they have no structural plan to address.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit

The RON95 price staying at RM1.99 is not evidence of ‘strong fiscal measures’ — it is the government tripling the blanket subsidy it spent three years promising to reform. The RM1.99 price tag costs RM2 billion a month and rising. Telling Malaysians to trust the government’s ‘strengthened fundamentals’ while the Cabinet’s actual response has been deferred WFH decisions, a backyard vegetable directive from Mat Sabu, and prayer from the PM is not governance — it’s narrative management. And weaponising the phrase ‘don’t mislead the public’ against critics while distributing food packs for a photo op at 2am is peak Madani optics.

📜 Previous Cases

Two days earlier, Finance Minister II Amir Hamzah told Malaysians not to ‘speculate’ about fuel supply while the KLCI was crashing. On the same day, he claimed the economy would ‘remain strong in 2026’ based on 2025’s ‘impressive performance’ — ignoring every forward indicator. Before that, Johari admitted the government could only ‘hold out two to three months’ on subsidies while simultaneously insisting there would be ’no subsidy cuts for now.’ The pattern is clear: deny, deflect, distribute food packs, repeat.

🎯 PH Callout

You promised Malaysians a ’new Malaysia’ built on transparency, reform, and evidence-based governance. Three years in, your senior advisor’s best fiscal messaging is ‘don’t mislead the public’ while handing out 300 food baskets at a police station during sahur. The subsidy bill is RM3.2 billion a month. The KLCI is below 1700. Your own ministers admit you can only ‘hold out’ a few months. And your answer is to tell critics to shut up? Malaysians deserve a plan, not a PR circuit. If the economy is truly on ‘firm footing,’ show the numbers. If it isn’t, stop pretending.
