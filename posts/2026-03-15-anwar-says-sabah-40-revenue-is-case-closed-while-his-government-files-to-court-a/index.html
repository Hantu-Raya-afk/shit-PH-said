Anwar Says Sabah 40% Revenue Is ‘Case Closed’ — While His Government Files to Court and Seeks a Stay of Execution

2026-03-15
#Politics 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister and Finance Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened#

At a Madani Iftar event at Universiti Malaysia Sabah on March 15, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim told the audience — including Sabah Chief Minister Hajiji Noor — that the federal government’s position on Sabah’s 40 per cent revenue entitlement under MA63 is ‘final’ and ‘case closed.’ In the same breath, he confirmed Putrajaya is going to court — not to appeal the 40 per cent, he insists, but to ‘seek clarification’ on parts of the High Court judgment that he claims unfairly questioned the legacies of Tunku Abdul Rahman, Tun Abdul Razak, Tun Mustapha, Tun Fuad Stephens, and others. A stay of execution application is scheduled to be heard at the Court of Appeal on March 31, and the Sabah government has indicated it will oppose it.

🔥 Why It Matters#

This is not just a legal technicality — it is a political manoeuvre dressed up as historical piety. The High Court ruled on October 17 last year that Sabah is constitutionally entitled to 40 per cent of net federal revenue derived from the state between 1974 and 2021, a period known as the ‘Lost Years.’ The court imposed a 180-day deadline expiring April 15, 2026. By seeking a ‘clarification’ and a stay of execution, Putrajaya can delay actual payment while claiming it has already ‘settled’ the matter. Sabah is a crucial electoral battleground, and Anwar cannot afford to alienate East Malaysian voters ahead of GE16 — but he also cannot afford to write the cheque.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

‘Case closed’ means you stop going to court. It does not mean you file new court applications, seek stays of execution, and then tell the people whose money you owe that you’re just protecting the reputation of dead leaders. Anwar’s framing — that the judgment was ’not objective, too driven by sentiment, and politically inclined’ — is itself a political statement. He is simultaneously telling Sabahans ‘we respect the ruling’ and telling the Court of Appeal ’the ruling is flawed.’ This is the Madani version of having your cake and eating it too, except the cake is RM billions in revenue owed to Sabah for half a century.

📜 Previous Cases#

This is not the first time Putrajaya has used procedural manoeuvring to delay MA63 commitments. The Malaysia Agreement 1963 has been a source of broken promises for over 60 years. PH came to power partly on the promise of restoring Sabah and Sarawak’s rights under MA63 — Anwar himself made this a centrepiece of his East Malaysian pitch. But actual delivery has been glacial. The AG’s advice to appeal ‘defects’ in the judgment mirrors a pattern across administrations: acknowledge the right, dispute the details, delay the payment. Previous governments did exactly the same. PH promised to be different.

🎯 PH Callout#

You told Sabahans you would honour MA63. You told them you were not like the governments that came before. You told them the 40 per cent was ‘final.’ So why is your Attorney-General in court? Why is there a stay of execution hearing on March 31? If the case is truly closed, close it. Pay Sabah. Drop the court applications. Every day between now and April 15 that Putrajaya spends in court instead of at the negotiating table is another day that ‘Madani’ means ‘more of the same’ for East Malaysia.
