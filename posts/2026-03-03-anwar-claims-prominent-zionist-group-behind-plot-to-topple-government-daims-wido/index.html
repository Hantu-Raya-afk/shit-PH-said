Anwar Claims 'Prominent Zionist Group' Behind Plot to Topple Government — Daim's Widow Calls It an 'Outright Lie'

Hantu Raya

March 3, 2026

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-03

📌 What Happened

On March 3, 2026, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim told Parliament that a “prominent Zionist group” was involved in an alleged plot to topple his government. Anwar claimed documents obtained by authorities showed a suspect — linked to a large-scale MACC corruption investigation — had engaged a UK-based PR firm to coordinate an international campaign to undermine Malaysian institutions. He named Bloomberg as one of the media agencies targeted and said the strategy, running from August 2024 to the next general election, involved foreign lobbyists, bankers, and lawmakers. Na’imah Abdul Khalid, the widow of former Finance Minister Daim Zainuddin, through her lawyers Rajesh Nagarajan and Sachpreetraj Singh, called Anwar’s claims “false, preposterous, outrageous, and a blatant attempt to mislead Parliament and the public,” stating there was “not one word about Zionism or Israel” in the communications plan.

🔥 Why It Matters

This is the Prime Minister of Malaysia standing in Parliament and invoking “Zionists” as shadowy enemies of the state — the oldest conspiratorial playbook in authoritarian politics. The claim serves multiple purposes: it deflects from the Azam Baki scandal engulfing his own MACC, it delegitimises critical media coverage (particularly Bloomberg’s reporting on Azam’s RM14 million shareholdings), and it frames anyone questioning the government’s handling of corruption as an agent of foreign sabotage. The “Zionist plot” narrative also poisons the well ahead of any royal commission of inquiry, which coalition partners DAP and Amanah had been pushing for. This is not reformist governance — it is narrative control through paranoia.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit

Na’imah’s lawyers laid it bare: the PR plan was about reassuring bankers of the Daim family’s financial viability during a domestic legal dispute — “Write to bankers setting out that international assets are not frozen and that this is a domestic legal dispute which we are confident will be resolved as there is no case to answer.” That’s it. No Zionists, no toppling, no sabotage. Anwar invoked parliamentary immunity to make claims he would not dare make outside the chamber, where he could be sued. Worse, MalaysiaNow revealed that PKR leaders themselves had engaged a US consultant in 2016 to lobby the White House to pressure then-PM Najib Razak. The hypocrisy is staggering: when Anwar’s party lobbied foreign powers against a sitting government, it was democracy. When his opponents allegedly hire a PR firm, it’s a Zionist conspiracy to topple the nation.

📜 Previous Cases

Malaysia has a long history of leaders crying “foreign plot” when domestic accountability gets too close. Najib Razak dismissed the 1MDB investigation as a “political conspiracy” involving foreign agents. Mahathir Mohamad blamed “Jewish bankers” and George Soros for the 1997 Asian financial crisis. Anwar himself was the victim of similar tactics — the Sodomy I and II prosecutions were widely seen as politically motivated attempts to silence opposition using state machinery. That he now deploys the same playbook of paranoia and foreign bogeymen against his own critics is a betrayal of everything he claimed to stand for during 25 years in opposition.

🎯 PH Callout

You promised reformasi. You promised transparency, accountability, and a government that answers to the people — not one that hides behind conspiracy theories when the people ask uncomfortable questions. You promised to protect press freedom, not to stand in Parliament and name Bloomberg as part of a Zionist sabotage campaign because they reported on your anti-corruption chief’s RM14 million in shares. You told Malaysians you were different from Najib, different from Mahathir. But the moment accountability knocked on your door, you reached for the same tired playbook: foreign enemies, shadowy plots, and the weaponisation of Parliament to smear a dead man’s widow. If this is Madani governance, Malaysians deserve a refund.
