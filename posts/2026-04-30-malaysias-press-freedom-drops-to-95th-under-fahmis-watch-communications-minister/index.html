Malaysia’s Press Freedom Drops to 95th Under Fahmi’s Watch — Communications Minister Once Dismissed RSF as ‘Not the Gold Standard’

2026-04-30
#Politics 

Said by: Fahmi Fadzil, Communications Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-04-30

📌 What Happened#

Malaysia dropped seven places to 95th out of 180 countries in the 2026 Reporters Without Borders (RSF) World Press Freedom Index, published on April 30. The country’s overall score fell from 56.09 to 52.73, with declines in four of five indicators. The political indicator — measuring media independence from political pressure — slipped from 44.66 to 43.00, while the economic indicator plunged from 48.67 to 40.28. Communications Minister Fahmi Fadzil, who oversees the MCMC and media policy, had previously dismissed the RSF index as “not the gold standard” when Malaysia dropped 34 spots to 107th in 2024.

🔥 Why It Matters#

Pakatan Harapan campaigned on media freedom and institutional reform. Instead, under Anwar Ibrahim’s watch, Malaysia has gone from 73rd in 2023 to 107th in 2024, climbed briefly to 88th in 2025, and now sunk again to 95th. MalaysiaNow called it “yet another indictment” of PH’s coalition government, which has “increasingly gone after critics.” The government’s use of MCMC takedowns, sedition investigations against journalists, and crackdowns on social media posts about fuel prices shows that PH’s media policy is indistinguishable from its predecessors — police the narrative first, defend freedom later, if ever.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

Fahmi’s 2024 response to the RSF drop was to dismiss the index itself. When the ranking improved slightly in 2025, PH was happy to take credit. Now that it’s dropped again, expect the same playbook: blame methodology, cite ASEAN comparisons, and insist things are “better than before.” Meanwhile, MCMC has blocked opposition media, the government has called for crackdowns on “fake news” about fuel prices, and Malaysia has banned more books under Anwar than any recent administration. You don’t get to cherry-pick which press freedom metrics count based on whether they flatter you.

📜 Previous Cases#

This is a pattern. In 2024, Malaysia plunged 34 spots to 107th and Fahmi shrugged it off. MCMC has blocked opposition news portals before state elections. Anwar called for action against “fake news” on fuel prices in April 2026, directing police and MCMC to crack down. The Printing Presses and Publications Act — a colonial-era censorship tool PH once promised to repeal — remains fully operational and was recently used to ban books. Every index drop triggers the same cycle: dismiss, deflect, and do nothing.

🎯 PH Callout#

You promised reformasi. You promised media freedom. You promised to repeal the Printing Presses Act, the Sedition Act, and SOSMA. Instead, you weaponised MCMC, banned books, investigated journalists, and told the Communications Minister to dismiss the world’s most widely cited press freedom index when it made you look bad. Fahmi, if RSF is “not the gold standard,” what is? Your weekly press conferences where you control the questions? PH’s press freedom record is now a documented, indexed, year-on-year failure — and no amount of spin changes the numbers.
