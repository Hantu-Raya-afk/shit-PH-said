Anwar Says Sabah 40% Revenue ‘Case Closed’ — While Simultaneously Filing a Court Application Over the Same Judgment

2026-03-15
#Politics 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister and Finance Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened#

At a Madani Iftar event at Universiti Malaysia Sabah on the night of March 15, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim declared the federal government’s commitment to Sabah’s 40 per cent revenue entitlement under MA63 is ‘final’ and ‘case closed.’ In the same speech, he confirmed the government is still going to court — not to appeal the 40%, he insists, but to ‘seek clarification’ on parts of the High Court judgment that he says ‘unfairly questioned the roles of past national and state leaders’ including Tunku Abdul Rahman and Tun Abdul Razak. A stay of execution application is scheduled for the Court of Appeal on March 31. The High Court’s 180-day compliance deadline expires April 15.

🔥 Why It Matters#

This is textbook Anwar: say the right thing, do the opposite, and frame the contradiction as nuance. The High Court ruled in October 2025 that Sabah is owed its 40% share of net federal revenue from 1974 to 2021 — the so-called ‘Lost Years.’ The federal government said it wouldn’t appeal. Then it filed a court application anyway, calling it a ‘clarification.’ Now it’s seeking a stay of execution — meaning it wants the court to pause enforcement of the very ruling it claims to accept. With the April 15 deadline approaching and state elections looming, this isn’t about protecting historical legacies. It’s about delaying payment while sounding like you’re not.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

‘Case closed’ doesn’t mean what Anwar thinks it means when your Attorney-General’s Chambers is filing court papers on the same case. CNA quoted Johan Ariffin Samad, former CEO of Sabah’s Institute for Development Studies, saying: ‘It doesn’t make sense. You either appeal the whole judgment or not at all.’ The ‘clarification’ framing is legal gymnastics — you don’t seek a stay of execution on a ruling you agree with. And doing all this at a Madani Iftar in Sabah, in front of the Chief Minister, while telling Sabahans their money is safe, is the kind of political performance that makes voters cynical about every promise that follows.

📜 Previous Cases#

Anwar’s government has form on this. In November 2025, after the High Court issued its grounds of judgment, Anwar said the judgment ‘suggests past governments acted unlawfully’ — framing it as a criticism of his predecessors rather than an obligation on his government. Four PKR MPs urged the government to simply comply. The Sabah Law Society, which won the judicial review, has indicated it will oppose the stay. This follows a broader PH pattern of accepting court rulings in press conferences while contesting them in courtrooms — see also the MACC chief saga and the Zahid DNAA.

🎯 PH Callout#

You told Sabahans ‘case closed’ at a dinner they hosted for you. Then your AGC filed a stay application. The April 15 deadline is a month away. Sabah has been owed this money since 1974 — that’s 52 years. You’ve been in power for three. If the 40% is truly ‘final,’ withdraw the court application, pay up, and stop hiding behind the reputations of dead leaders. Sabahans can read between the lines. They’ve been reading between the lines for half a century. If you want their votes, show them the money, not another speech.
