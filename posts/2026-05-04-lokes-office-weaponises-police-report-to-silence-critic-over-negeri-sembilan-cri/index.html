Loke’s Office Weaponises Police Report to Silence Critic Over Negeri Sembilan Crisis — Political Secretary Invokes ‘3R’ Card Against Chegubard

2026-05-04
#Politics 

Said by: Anthony Loke, Transport Minister & DAP Secretary-General (via political secretary Ho Weng Wah)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-05-04

📌 What Happened#

On May 4, 2026, Ho Weng Wah — the political secretary to Transport Minister and DAP Secretary-General Anthony Loke — lodged a police report at the Seremban District Police Headquarters against former Bersatu division leader Badrul Hisham Shaharin, better known as Chegubard. The report was filed over a Facebook post titled “Anthony Loke must be investigated for funding an illegal entity — Majlis Dato Lembaga Adat Sungei Ujong,” which alleged that Loke had channelled a substantial amount of funds to an entity claimed to be illegal and linked the matter to disturbances involving the royal institution in Negeri Sembilan. Ho told reporters the post “touches on sensitive 3R issues (race, religion and royalty), which could potentially incite tension and divide the community.”

🔥 Why It Matters#

This is not just a defamation complaint — it is a PH minister’s office deploying the police apparatus to suppress political criticism during an active state-level constitutional crisis. The Negeri Sembilan political impasse — in which PH lost its legislative majority after 14 UMNO assemblymen withdrew support — is one of the most significant governance crises of the current term. Instead of addressing the substantive allegation about funding an entity tied to the Undang institution, Loke’s office invoked the catch-all “3R” framing to turn a political dispute into a criminal matter. This pattern — using police reports and sedition-adjacent language to silence critics — is exactly the kind of institutional behaviour PH promised to dismantle when it was in opposition.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

PH campaigned for years against the weaponisation of police reports and sedition law as tools to suppress dissent. Loke himself, as DAP secretary-general, has repeatedly positioned DAP as a party that defends press freedom and civil liberties. Yet here is his own office filing a police report — not even Loke personally, but his political secretary, providing a convenient layer of plausible deniability — against a political opponent making allegations that are clearly within the bounds of political speech. The “3R” invocation is the most cynical part: labelling criticism of a politician’s alleged financial dealings as a threat to racial and religious harmony is the exact playbook that UMNO and BN used for decades. PH has not reformed the system. It has inherited the toolkit and learned to use it.

📜 Previous Cases#

This sits within a growing pattern under the PH-led MADANI government. In April 2026, Bukit Aman CID director M. Kumar openly justified using the Sedition Act 1948 against social media users who make “provocative” comments on government policies — a move that drew condemnation from Suhakam and opposition MPs. A TikTok user was reportedly arrested under the Sedition Act for criticising the Prime Minister. Separately, Nga Kor Ming threatened to call a journalist’s editor for asking a legitimate question about the I Lite U campaign in October 2025. DAP’s track record in government increasingly mirrors the authoritarian instincts it once railed against.

🎯 PH Callout#

You promised Malaysia Madani — a government built on compassion, institutional reform, and democratic accountability. You promised to repeal the Sedition Act. You promised press freedom. You promised political maturity. And now your Transport Minister’s office is filing police reports against critics who question where public money is going during a constitutional crisis in Negeri Sembilan. If the allegation against Loke is false, let it be rebutted in public. If it is serious, let it be investigated properly. What you do not get to do is use the police to make political speech disappear and then call yourselves reformers. That is not reform. That is BN with better branding.
