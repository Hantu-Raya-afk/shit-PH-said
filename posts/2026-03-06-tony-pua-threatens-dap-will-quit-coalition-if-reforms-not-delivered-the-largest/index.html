Tony Pua Threatens DAP Will Quit Coalition If Reforms Not Delivered — The Largest Party in Government Just Admitted It Might Walk

2026-03-06
#Politics 

Said by: Tony Pua, DAP Disciplinary Chief

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-06

📌 What Happened#

On March 6, 2026, Tony Pua — DAP’s disciplinary chief and one of the party’s most prominent voices — told Reuters that DAP will “evaluate its position in the relevant coalitions when the time comes for elections.” He confirmed the party plans a July meeting to decide whether its leaders should resign from all government positions, including the cabinet, if promised reforms are not implemented. Pua said DAP would not pull support this term to avoid instability, but warned: “If we don’t withdraw, the party will lose all credibility and can expect a punishing outcome from the elections.” The statement came amid the MACC/Azam Baki scandal, DAP’s November 2025 regional election loss, and the failed PM term-limit bill on March 2. Separately, PKR lawmaker Hassan Karim demanded Anwar “terminate the contract of the MACC chief immediately and promptly form the RCI.”

🔥 Why It Matters#

This is the largest party in the ruling coalition publicly flirting with an exit. DAP holds 40 seats — more than Anwar’s own PKR (31 seats) — and its threat to leave represents the most serious internal rupture since the Madani government was formed. Pua’s statement confirms what analysts have been saying: PH’s reform agenda has stalled, the MACC scandal has exposed Anwar’s reluctance to act decisively, and the party that was supposed to be the moral spine of the coalition is now questioning whether staying in government is worth the reputational damage. Reuters also reported that several PKR lawmakers are considering contesting the next election as independents, having lost confidence in Anwar’s leadership. If DAP follows through in July, it could trigger a cabinet reshuffle, snap elections, or a complete realignment of Malaysian politics.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

Here’s the problem: DAP has been threatening to hold PH accountable since 2023, and every time it counts, they fold. They accepted Anwar’s refusal to form an RCI on Azam Baki. They sat quietly while the government wielded Section 124B against activists and academics. They voted for Budget 2026 without demanding conditions. And now Tony Pua — who isn’t even in cabinet — delivers the ultimatum, while making sure to clarify that DAP won’t actually withdraw support this term. This isn’t accountability. This is managed disappointment. DAP is telling its base: “We hear you, we’re angry too, but we’ll do something about it… eventually… maybe in July… if it still matters.” It’s a threat designed to be loud enough for the base and quiet enough for Anwar.

📜 Previous Cases#

This pattern is DAP’s signature move. In 2024, Lim Guan Eng publicly warned that PH was losing urban support over delayed reforms — DAP stayed. When the MACC scandal first broke, DAP leaders demanded an RCI — Anwar refused, and DAP stayed. After the November 2025 state election loss, which Pua himself described as a “shock,” DAP leaders called for urgent internal reflection — then carried on as normal. Even PKR’s own Hassan Karim has been more direct than DAP leadership, publicly demanding Anwar terminate Azam Baki and form the RCI immediately. The playbook is consistent: express concern, make a conditional threat, set a future deadline, and hope the news cycle moves on before the deadline arrives.

🎯 PH Callout#

You promised reformasi. You promised transparency, accountability, and a government that doesn’t protect its own. Three years in, your largest coalition partner is publicly telling international wire services it might walk because you haven’t delivered. Tony Pua says DAP will “lose all credibility” if it stays without reforms. Here’s the uncomfortable truth: that credibility is already bleeding out. Every day DAP stays silent on Azam Baki, every bill that fails because your own MPs don’t show up, every time you use BN’s playbook while wearing reformist clothing — that’s another voter who decides PH isn’t worth the benefit of the doubt. July isn’t a deadline. It’s an obituary notice if nothing changes before then.
