Anwar Calls US-Israel Actions ‘Neo-Imperialistic’ and Says Iran Can’t Be Asked to ‘Simply Contain Themselves’ — While Malaysia Bleeds RM2 Billion a Month

2026-03-16
#Politics 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-16

📌 What Happened#

At the launch of the Mushaf Malaysia MADANI in Putrajaya on March 16, Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim disclosed that he had held a second round of discussions with leaders from Iran, Oman, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, the UAE, and Türkiye about the West Asia conflict. He described the US-Israeli strikes on Iran as a “sheer display of arrogance” and “neo-imperialistic design,” and said: “It is difficult to ask them simply to contain themselves.” He confirmed the conflict is costing Malaysia an “additional subsidy of RM2 billion a month” to maintain RON95 prices. He also warned against “irresponsible elements exploiting the case and using it as a political weapon, weaponising it to attack.”

🔥 Why It Matters#

This is the Prime Minister of a country haemorrhaging RM2 billion a month in additional subsidies using a religious event to signal diplomatic sympathy for Iran’s military posture — while offering no new domestic measures to protect Malaysians from the economic fallout. The language is carefully chosen: “neo-imperialistic design” and the suggestion that Iran cannot be expected to restrain itself positions Malaysia closer to Tehran’s framing of the conflict. This is not neutral diplomacy — it is rhetorical alignment with one side of a war that is bleeding Malaysia’s treasury dry. And the kicker? Anyone who questions this approach is dismissed as an “irresponsible element” who is “weaponising” the crisis.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

Anwar has perfected the art of sounding consequential while doing nothing consequential. He holds phone calls with Gulf leaders. He describes the geopolitics in vivid, morally charged language. He expresses solidarity with Muslim nations. And then? Nothing changes domestically. The subsidies keep haemorrhaging. There’s no stimulus. No energy diversification plan that wasn’t forced by the crisis itself. No timeline for when the RM3.2 billion monthly subsidy bill becomes unsustainable. The “irresponsible elements” line is the real tell — it is Anwar’s reflex whenever criticism gets too close to home. Question his MACC defence? You’re part of a Zionist plot. Question his crisis management? You’re weaponising the conflict. The pattern is: perform statesmanship abroad, silence accountability at home.

📜 Previous Cases#

Anwar’s rhetorical approach to international crises has been consistent since taking office. During the Gaza escalation, he delivered impassioned speeches at the UN and Islamic summits while critics pointed out the absence of concrete diplomatic or economic measures. His government invoked the “Zionist agenda” narrative in Parliament just weeks ago to explain away Bloomberg’s reporting on the MACC scandal. The “irresponsible elements” framing has been deployed against C4 (the anti-corruption NGO whose CEO was questioned under Section 124B), against media outlets, and now against anyone raising questions about the oil crisis response. It is the Madani government’s all-purpose shield: if the criticism stings, call it a weapon.

🎯 PH Callout#

You are not the foreign minister of Iran. You are the Prime Minister of Malaysia. Your people are not asking you to adjudicate the morality of great-power warfare — they are asking you to protect them from its economic consequences. Every phone call with a Gulf leader that does not produce a concrete benefit for Malaysia is theatre. Every speech that describes the geopolitics in dramatic terms but ends with zero new domestic measures is performance. And every time you dismiss critics as “irresponsible elements,” you are telling Malaysians that accountability is a weapon only the opposition is allowed to use. RM2 billion a month. The meter is running. What’s the plan?
