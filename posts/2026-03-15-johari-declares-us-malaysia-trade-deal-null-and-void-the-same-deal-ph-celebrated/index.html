Johari Declares US-Malaysia Trade Deal ‘Null and Void’ — The Same Deal PH Celebrated as a Diplomatic Triumph Five Months Ago

2026-03-15
#Economy 

Said by: Johari Abdul Ghani, Investment, Trade and Industry Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened#

On March 15, Investment, Trade and Industry Minister Johari Abdul Ghani confirmed that the Agreement on Reciprocal Trade (ART) between the United States and Malaysia, signed in October 2025, is now ’null and void.’ The deal collapsed after the US Supreme Court struck down most of the sweeping tariff measures introduced by President Donald Trump. Speaking at an Aidilfitri contribution event in Desa Pandan, Johari said Malaysia is currently subject to a 10 per cent global tariff under Section 122 of the US Trade Act of 1974, down from the 19 per cent rate negotiated under the ART. However, the US has simultaneously launched Section 301 investigations into 15 trade partners including Malaysia, covering structural excess capacity, production practices, and forced labour allegations.

🔥 Why It Matters#

The ART was one of PH’s flagship diplomatic achievements. When it was signed in October 2025, ministers and government-aligned commentators hailed it as proof that Anwar’s administration could navigate great-power competition and protect Malaysian exporters. Now the deal is dead — not because of anything Malaysia did, but because the US Supreme Court gutted the legal basis for Trump’s tariff regime. The problem is what comes next: the 10 per cent global tariff is still in effect, and the Section 301 investigations could lead to new, potentially higher tariffs targeting Malaysia specifically. The ART at least gave Malaysia a defined rate and some predictability. Now there is neither. Five months of diplomatic capital, ministerial travel, and press conferences — evaporated.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

Johari’s tone was oddly celebratory for a man whose biggest trade deal just died. He framed the Supreme Court ruling as vindication — as if Malaysia refusing to accept unfair tariffs is the same as Malaysia’s trade deal collapsing. The reality is simpler: the ART locked Malaysia into a 19 per cent tariff rate that was higher than the 10 per cent global rate now in effect. The deal was arguably bad from the start — Malaysia accepted punitive terms to avoid worse ones. Now that the legal basis has been struck down, Johari is retroactively spinning a collapse into a victory. Meanwhile, the Section 301 investigations — covering forced labour, excess capacity, and manufacturing practices — are the real threat, and Johari’s response so far has been to promise MITI will ’explain’ and ‘clarify.’ Explaining is not a trade strategy.

📜 Previous Cases#

This is not the first time a PH trade initiative has aged poorly. The government spent 2023 and 2024 courting foreign direct investment with headline-grabbing MOU signings, many of which never materialised into actual investment. The pattern is consistent: announce the deal loudly, claim credit immediately, then go quiet when the deal underperforms or collapses. On the forced labour front, Malaysia has been on the US Customs and Border Protection watchlist for years, with Withhold Release Orders issued against multiple Malaysian companies in the electronics and glove sectors. Three years of PH governance have not resolved the structural issues — they’ve just managed the optics.

🎯 PH Callout#

You signed the ART in October and called it a diplomatic success. Five months later, it’s dead. You’re now subject to a 10 per cent global tariff with no bilateral framework, and the US is investigating Malaysia under Section 301 for forced labour and excess capacity — the same issues you promised to address when you took office. Stop spinning collapses into victories. Tell Malaysians plainly: what is your trade strategy now that the ART is void, the US is investigating, and the global tariff regime is in flux? ‘We will explain to the Americans’ is not a strategy. It’s a press conference.
