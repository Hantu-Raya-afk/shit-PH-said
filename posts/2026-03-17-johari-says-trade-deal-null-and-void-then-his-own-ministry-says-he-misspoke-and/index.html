Johari Says Trade Deal ‘Null and Void’ — Then His Own Ministry Says He ‘Misspoke,’ and PH’s Own MPs Demand Answers

2026-03-17
#Economy 

Said by: Johari Abdul Ghani, Investment, Trade and Industry Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-17

📌 What Happened#

On Sunday March 16, Investment, Trade and Industry Minister Johari Abdul Ghani told reporters the Malaysia-US Agreement on Reciprocal Trade was “null and void” following the US Supreme Court’s decision striking down Trump’s tariff powers — “It is not on hold. It is no longer there, it’s null and void,” he said. Hours later, his own ministry issued a correction stating the minister had “misspoken,” without further explanation. By Monday, Communications Minister Fahmi Fadzil told reporters after Cabinet that MITI would “continue engaging” Washington, while eight PKR backbenchers issued a joint statement demanding the government clarify the deal’s status and warning the agreement was “one-sided.”

🔥 Why It Matters#

This is not a policy disagreement — it is a government that does not know, or cannot agree on, the status of its own flagship trade deal. The ART was signed by Anwar and Trump in October 2025 and presented as a major diplomatic achievement. Now the trade minister says it is dead, his ministry says he misspoke, the government spokesperson says talks continue, and the PM’s own party’s MPs are publicly calling the deal lopsided. When eight lawmakers from the ruling party have to issue a joint statement asking their own government what is going on, the problem is not communication — it is competence.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

PH defended this deal for months against critics who called it rushed, one-sided, and unnecessarily deferential to Washington. Wong Chen, a PH MP, called it “controversial and lopsided” at the time — and was ignored. Now the deal’s legal basis has collapsed, the trade minister cannot keep his story straight for 12 hours, and MITI’s idea of damage control is a one-line statement saying the minister “misspoken” — not even grammatically correct. The government that promised institutional reform and transparent governance cannot even coordinate a press statement about its biggest trade agreement.

📜 Previous Cases#

This follows a pattern. When the ART was first signed in October 2025, critics warned the 19% tariff rate was worse than what other ASEAN nations were getting. PH dismissed the criticism. When the US Supreme Court struck down Trump’s tariffs, the deal’s legal basis evaporated — but PH said nothing for weeks. Johari’s Sunday declaration appeared to be an unscripted admission of reality, which MITI then tried to stuff back into the bottle. Malaysia has a long history of ministers making definitive public statements that are then “clarified” by their ministries — from the 1MDB era to the present. The Madani government was supposed to be different.

🎯 PH Callout#

You told Malaysians you would bring professionalism, transparency, and institutional competence to government. Your trade minister declared your flagship US deal dead on live television, your ministry contradicted him within hours, your spokesperson said talks would continue, and your own backbenchers had to issue a public statement demanding answers. This is not governance — this is a government arguing with itself in public while the country’s trade relationships hang in the balance. If you cannot coordinate a press statement, how are you negotiating with Washington?
