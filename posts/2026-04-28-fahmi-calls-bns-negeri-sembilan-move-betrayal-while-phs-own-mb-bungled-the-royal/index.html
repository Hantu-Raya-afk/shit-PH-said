Fahmi Calls BN’s Negeri Sembilan Move ‘Betrayal’ — While PH’s Own MB Bungled the Royal Crisis That Triggered It

2026-04-28
#Politics 

Said by: Fahmi Fadzil, Communications Minister (Government Spokesperson / PH Communications Director)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-04-28

📌 What Happened#

On April 28, PH Communications Director Fahmi Fadzil issued a statement condemning all 14 UMNO-BN state assemblymen in Negeri Sembilan for withdrawing their support from Menteri Besar Aminuddin Harun, calling it ‘acts of betrayal’ and an attempt to seize the state government. The withdrawal came after Aminuddin’s handling of a crisis involving four Adat Perpatih territorial chieftains who declared they were deposing the Yang di-Pertuan Besar, Tuanku Muhriz — a crisis BN says Aminuddin mismanaged by taking sides instead of remaining neutral, leading to the indefinite postponement of the State Legislative Assembly on April 23.

🔥 Why It Matters#

This is the sharpest state-level fracture in Anwar’s unity government since it was formed in 2022. BN — PH’s key coalition partner — claims a simple majority in Negeri Sembilan with reported PN crossover support, turning a customary succession dispute into a full-blown test of the federal alliance ahead of GE16. Fahmi’s framing of the crisis as ‘betrayal’ rather than a legitimate confidence crisis exposes PH’s instinct to close ranks around its people, even when the problem started with its own appointee. The Straits Times and SCMP have both noted this could fester into broader mistrust within the unity government at the federal level.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

Fahmi says the reasons given by the 14 assemblymen ‘were not related at all to the performance of the menteri besar.’ BN says the opposite — that Aminuddin failed to manage the royal crisis properly and took a partisan position that disrespected traditional institutions. PH’s response is textbook: when your guy is in trouble, don’t address the substance — just call it a power grab. The word ‘betrayal’ is doing heavy lifting here, because the alternative framing — that PH’s own MB was incompetent and BN lost confidence for legitimate reasons — is far more damaging. Fahmi also insisted the crisis ‘does not affect the federal government,’ which is the political equivalent of saying ’the house isn’t on fire’ while smoke pours out the windows.

📜 Previous Cases#

PH knows what backdoor takeovers look like — they were victims of the Sheraton Move in 2020. But they also know the difference between a federal-level betrayal and a state-level confidence crisis triggered by their own appointee’s missteps. More recently, PH’s own coalition failed to deliver votes for Anwar’s PM term limit bill in March 2026, with 44 abstentions from within the coalition. PH’s pattern is clear: when allies don’t deliver, it’s ‘misunderstanding’; when allies withdraw, it’s ‘betrayal.’

🎯 PH Callout#

You promised a unity government built on shared governance and mutual respect. When 14 assemblymen from your coalition partner say they’ve lost confidence in your MB — after your MB took sides in a royal dispute he had no business picking — the answer is not to cry ‘betrayal.’ The answer is to ask why your appointee couldn’t read the room. You campaigned on reform, accountability, and good governance. Accountability starts at home, and right now, your home in Negeri Sembilan is on fire because of your own people.
