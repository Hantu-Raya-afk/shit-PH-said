Fahmi Fadzil Declares Unity Government ‘Stable’ While Negeri Sembilan Burns — 14 UMNO Assemblymen Walk Out and the Government Spokesman Says ‘Nothing to See Here’

2026-04-28
#Politics 

Said by: Fahmi Fadzil, Communications Minister & Government Spokesman (PKR)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-04-28

📌 What Happened#

On April 28, 2026, Communications Minister Datuk Fahmi Fadzil — the official spokesman of the unity government — held a press conference after the Asia Pacific Online Safety Dialogue 2026 and told reporters that the Negeri Sembilan political crisis “does not affect the federal government.” He described the situation as “something that may be unique to Negeri Sembilan” and insisted all other unity state governments were “functioning as usual.” This came just one day after all 14 UMNO and Barisan Nasional assemblymen in Negeri Sembilan unanimously withdrew support for Menteri Besar Aminuddin Harun, plunging the state into a leadership crisis triggered by a rare royal constitutional dispute.

🔥 Why It Matters#

The Negeri Sembilan crisis is the sharpest state-level test yet of Anwar Ibrahim’s fragile ruling alliance, and Fahmi’s dismissive framing is not just spin — it’s a template for how PH handles coalition rot. When your key coalition partner pulls all 14 of its state assemblymen and openly claims a simple majority with the opposition, calling it “unique” is not analysis — it’s damage control dressed as a talking point. The Straits Times reported that growing tensions within the ruling alliance could force Anwar to dissolve Parliament by end-2026, with BN also agitating to break its electoral pact with PH in upcoming Melaka and Johor state polls. This is not a local squabble; it is a preview of how Anwar’s unity government unravels.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

Fahmi’s “it doesn’t affect the federal level” line is insulting to anyone paying attention. UMNO didn’t just “express concerns” — they walked out, declared a new majority with the opposition, and tried to form a new state government. If your coalition partner can do that in one state while you insist everything is fine federally, you’re either lying or you’ve lost the plot. Fahmi’s entire job is to manage the government’s narrative, and the narrative he chose was denial. The PH Convention in Johor on May 17, which he pointed to as the forum for discussion, is weeks away — meaning PH’s official response to a coalition collapse is “we’ll talk about it later at a conference.” That is not governance. That is a holding pattern.

📜 Previous Cases#

This is not the first time PH has tried to contain a coalition fire by pretending it’s a campfire. When the Sheraton Move toppled the first PH government in 2020, the coalition was similarly caught flat-footed, insisting internal dynamics were manageable right up until they weren’t. Fahmi himself has repeatedly used the “functioning as usual” line as a rhetorical shield — including during earlier tensions over the MACC-Azam Baki controversy, where PH similarly insisted that calls for a Royal Commission of Inquiry were premature and the government was stable. The playbook is consistent: deny, defer, deflect.

🎯 PH Callout#

You promised reformasi. You promised a new kind of politics built on transparency, accountability, and coalition integrity. When 14 assemblymen from your key partner walk out and your official spokesman’s response is “it’s unique to Negeri Sembilan,” you are not reforming anything — you are managing the optics of decline. If PH cannot hold a state government together, it has no business telling voters it can hold the country together. Stop treating coalition crises like PR problems. Start treating them like what they are: evidence that the unity government was always a marriage of convenience, and the honeymoon ended long ago.
