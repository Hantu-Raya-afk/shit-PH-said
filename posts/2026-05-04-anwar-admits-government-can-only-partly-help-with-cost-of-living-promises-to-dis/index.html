Anwar Admits Government Can Only ‘Partly’ Help With Cost of Living — Promises to Discuss It at Cabinet on Wednesday After 2.5 Years in Power

2026-05-04
#Economy 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister & Finance Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-05-04

📌 What Happened#

Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim, speaking at a monthly assembly with ministry staff at Putrajaya on May 4, 2026, admitted that the government can only “partly” help Malaysians struggling with the cost of living. “Are we able to help? My answer is partly yes — we are not able to help everyone,” he said. He acknowledged that official reports claim prices are under control, but conceded that feedback from petty traders — food stall operators — shows prices are rising “gradually week by week” with significant impact on business operations. His big plan: raise the issue at Cabinet this Wednesday.

🔥 Why It Matters#

This is the Prime Minister of Malaysia, after two and a half years in power, publicly admitting his government cannot solve the cost of living crisis it promised to fix. Anwar ran on a platform of economic reform and defending the rakyat’s purchasing power. Now he’s telling food stall operators their best hope is a Wednesday meeting. The admission that official data and grassroots reality are diverging is damning — it means the government’s own monitoring apparatus is failing to capture what every Malaysian already knows: things are getting more expensive, and no one in Putrajaya has a plan.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

After 2.5 years, the response to a cost of living crisis is “I’ll bring it up at Cabinet on Wednesday”? This is not governance — it’s performative listening. Anwar frames himself as the PM who goes to the ground and hears from the shops, but the man is also Finance Minister. He controls the Treasury. He sets fiscal policy. If prices are rising and his own reports don’t reflect it, the failure is structural and it sits on his desk. Telling the rakyat “we cannot help everyone” while maintaining a RM5 billion monthly subsidy bill that mostly benefits the wealthy and the smuggling networks is not candour — it’s abdication dressed up as empathy.

📜 Previous Cases#

This is a pattern. In 2023, Anwar promised targeted subsidies would ease the cost of living. In 2024, he said the same. In 2025, he blamed global conditions. Now in 2026, he’s admitting the government can only partly help — the goalposts have shifted from “we will fix it” to “we cannot fix it for everyone.” Meanwhile, Mat Sabu told Malaysians just yesterday to “spend prudently” as food prices spike. The MADANI playbook on cost of living has always been the same: acknowledge, empathise, defer, repeat.

🎯 PH Callout#

You promised Malaysia Madani — a civilised, competent government that puts the rakyat first. Two and a half years later, your answer to rising prices is a Cabinet agenda item for next Wednesday. You are the Prime Minister and the Finance Minister. You have every lever. If your own monitoring systems can’t tell you what a makcik selling nasi lemak already knows, the problem isn’t the data — it’s the government. Stop touring the ground for photo ops and start governing with structural solutions. The rakyat didn’t vote for empathy. They voted for results.
