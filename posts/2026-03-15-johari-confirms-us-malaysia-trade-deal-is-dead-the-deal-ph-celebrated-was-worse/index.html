Johari Confirms US-Malaysia Trade Deal Is Dead — The Deal PH Celebrated Was Worse for Malaysia Than No Deal at All

2026-03-15
#Economy 

Said by: Johari Abdul Ghani, Investment, Trade and Industry Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened#

On March 15, 2026, Investment, Trade and Industry Minister Johari Abdul Ghani confirmed that the Malaysia-US Agreement on Reciprocal Trade (ART), signed in October 2025, is now “null and void” following a US Supreme Court ruling that struck down President Trump’s sweeping reciprocal tariffs. Speaking at an Aidilfitri contribution event in Desa Pandan, Johari said that under the now-defunct deal, Malaysia had agreed to a 19% tariff rate, whereas the current global baseline tariff stands at just 10% under Section 122 of the US Trade Act of 1974. He noted the US will review within five months whether to impose a new tariff under Section 301.

🔥 Why It Matters#

The ART was supposed to be a crown jewel of PH’s trade diplomacy — a Comprehensive Strategic Partnership with the US that Anwar’s government paraded as proof of Malaysia’s rising international standing. Now it’s dead, and the uncomfortable truth is out: the deal PH celebrated locked Malaysia into a 19% tariff rate that was nearly double the current 10% baseline. Malaysia’s exports to the US amount to RM233 billion annually, making trade policy far too important for the kind of performative deal-making PH engaged in. Meanwhile, the US has launched Section 301 investigations into 60 countries — including Malaysia — over forced labour and dumping, issues PH has had three years to reform and hasn’t.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

Johari announced the deal’s death like a weather update — flat, clinical, no accountability. But someone needs to answer for this: why did Malaysia sign a deal that imposed a higher tariff than the default? PKR’s own Wong Chen called the deal “controversial and lopsided” at the time. The opposition is now demanding a full parliamentary explanation. And Johari’s reassurance that Malaysia “only” faces 10% tariffs now is cold comfort when the Section 301 review looming over the next five months could produce something far worse — especially on forced labour, where Malaysia’s track record remains an international embarrassment.

📜 Previous Cases#

This is not the first time PH has dressed up a bad deal as a diplomatic win. The TPPA flip-flop under Mahathir’s first PH government set the precedent for Malaysia’s erratic trade positioning. Anwar’s government inherited an economy already export-dependent on the US and China and has done little to diversify. The October 2025 ART was rushed through with minimal parliamentary scrutiny and was criticised by trade economists for locking Malaysia into unfavourable terms. Now the deal is void, Malaysia faces a Section 301 investigation, and the government’s trade strategy looks reactive at best, reckless at worst.

🎯 PH Callout#

You celebrated this deal. You told the rakyat it proved Malaysia punched above its weight on the global stage. Your own backbencher called it lopsided. The US Supreme Court just killed it, and the math shows Malaysia was paying nearly double the baseline tariff under a deal you signed voluntarily. Now explain to Parliament and the public: who negotiated these terms, who approved them, and why Malaysia agreed to 19% when everyone else was paying 10%. The rakyat deserves more than a flat “null and void.” They deserve accountability for a deal that was bad before it was dead.
