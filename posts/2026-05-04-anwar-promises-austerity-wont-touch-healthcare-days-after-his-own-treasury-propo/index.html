Anwar Promises Austerity Won’t Touch Healthcare — Days After His Own Treasury Proposed Slashing RM3 Billion From the Health Budget

2026-05-04
#Health 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister & Finance Minister

Category: Health

Date: 2026-05-04

📌 What Happened#

Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim told a ministry assembly in Putrajaya on May 4 that austerity measures currently being rolled out across federal ministries will not affect “core programmes,” including critical healthcare services and the recruitment of new doctors and nurses. This came days after the Treasury issued a directive on April 29 ordering all ministries to review and cut their 2026 operating expenditures — with a suggested RM5.4 billion in cuts targeting the Health Ministry (RM3.06 billion from its RM46.5 billion allocation) and the Higher Education Ministry (RM2.39 billion). The cuts are driven by a subsidy bill that has ballooned from RM15 billion to a projected RM58.4 billion this year, as the Middle East conflict sends energy prices surging.

🔥 Why It Matters#

This is the MADANI government’s first real austerity exercise — and the gap between the Treasury’s numbers and the PM’s reassurances is a red flag. You do not slash RM3 billion from a health budget and then tell the public “healthcare won’t be affected” without explaining what, exactly, gets cut instead. The RM10 billion in total proposed savings is also a fraction of the RM43.4 billion subsidy shortfall, meaning even with these painful cuts, the fiscal hole remains massive. Former DAP Finance Minister Lim Guan Eng himself warned on April 30 that these austerity measures could dampen GDP growth and hurt essential public services. When your own coalition allies are publicly flagging the damage, the PM’s assurances ring hollow.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

Anwar is doing what every Malaysian PM does when austerity bites: promise that the pain will be surgical, painless, and somehow won’t touch the things people care about most. But RM3.06 billion is not a rounding error — it is 6.6% of the Health Ministry’s entire budget. You cannot remove that amount without affecting service delivery, procurement timelines, facility upgrades, or staffing pipelines. The promise to keep hiring doctors while slashing billions is either a lie or an admission that the cuts will gut something else in the health system that the public won’t notice until it’s too late. This is fiscal gaslighting: announce the cut, deny the consequence, and hope nobody does the math.

📜 Previous Cases#

Malaysia has a long history of austerity-era healthcare promises that quietly unravel. During the 2015-2016 oil price crash under Najib, the Health Ministry’s development expenditure was cut by over 20%, leading to delays in hospital construction and equipment procurement that took years to recover from. Anwar himself, while in opposition, hammered the BN government for exactly this kind of “cut now, reassure later” playbook. In his own 13th Malaysia Plan (tabled July 2025), he pledged RM40 billion for healthcare overhaul — a commitment now looking increasingly hollow as the government scrambles to cut the very budgets meant to deliver those reforms.

🎯 PH Callout#

You promised Malaysia MADANI — compassionate, reformist governance. You pledged RM40 billion for healthcare transformation. Now, less than a year after tabling 13MP, your own Treasury is proposing to rip RM3 billion out of the Health Ministry, and your response is to stand at a podium and say “don’t worry, it won’t hurt.” Malaysians have heard this before. From BN. From PN. From every government that treated austerity as a communications problem instead of a governance failure. If the cuts are real, show the public exactly what is being sacrificed. If the cuts are not real, retract the Treasury directive. You cannot have it both ways — and the rakyat deserves better than a PM who thinks reassurance is a substitute for transparency.
