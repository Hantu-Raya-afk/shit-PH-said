Anwar Tells Everyone to ‘Remain Calm’ and ‘Prioritise the Economy’ as His Own Coalition Fractures in Negeri Sembilan — The Unity Government’s Crisis Management Playbook Is Just ‘Shh, Don’t Look’

2026-05-01
#Politics 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister & PH Chairman

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-05-01

📌 What Happened#

On May 1, 2026, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim responded to the Negeri Sembilan political crisis — in which all 14 UMNO-BN assemblymen withdrew support for PH Menteri Besar Aminuddin Harun — by urging “all parties to remain calm” and insisting that “the priority now is to restore the national economy rather than escalate political issues.” He said the situation must be handled “in accordance with the state constitution” while respecting the Yang di-Pertuan Besar’s decree for Aminuddin to continue in office. Communications Minister Fahmi Fadzil had earlier on April 28 claimed the turmoil had “no impact on the federal level” and called it “something unique to Negeri Sembilan.”

🔥 Why It Matters#

This is not a minor state-level squabble. The Straits Times ran a headline saying the crisis “signals end is nigh for Anwar’s unity government.” BN’s withdrawal of support for a PH MB in Negeri Sembilan is the clearest sign yet that the PH-BN coalition pact is fracturing from within. UMNO is testing how far it can go in extracting concessions before the next general election. Anwar’s response — to tell everyone to focus on the economy — is a textbook deflection that treats a structural coalition failure as a communications problem. If the PM cannot hold his own coalition together in one state, how is anyone supposed to believe the unity government will survive a general election?

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

Anwar’s “remain calm” response is the political equivalent of a pilot telling passengers to enjoy the in-flight entertainment while the engine is on fire. Fourteen assemblymen from your coalition partner just pulled the rug out from under your MB, the state assembly was indefinitely postponed, and your response is “let’s focus on the economy”? This is the man who spent decades in opposition demanding accountability and transparency from BN governments. Now that BN is eating his coalition alive from the inside, his strategy is to pretend it’s not happening. Fahmi Fadzil’s claim that it has “no impact at the federal level” is gaslighting on a national scale — analysts are already speculating about an early general election because of this crisis.

📜 Previous Cases#

This is not the first time Anwar has deployed the “calm down and focus on governance” card when his coalition faces internal revolt. When the Sheraton Move toppled PH’s first government in 2020, the coalition was similarly caught flat-footed by defections it should have anticipated. Anwar’s entire political career post-2018 has been defined by an inability to manage coalition partners — first losing government entirely, then spending years negotiating a unity government that required him to embrace the very party that betrayed him. The NS crisis is the latest iteration of PH’s chronic weakness: it wins power but cannot hold it, because it refuses to confront the structural contradictions of governing with parties that have fundamentally different interests.

🎯 PH Callout#

You told Malaysians that the unity government was necessary for stability. You said the big tent was the only way to protect reform. Now your tent is collapsing in Negeri Sembilan, and your answer is “don’t escalate.” Escalate what, exactly? Your own coalition partner just declared no confidence in your MB. That is not an escalation — it is a fact. If PH cannot manage BN at the state level, stop pretending you can manage them at the federal level. Either confront the fracture honestly and tell Malaysians what the unity government actually costs, or stop calling it unity. Telling people to “remain calm” while the house burns is not leadership. It is cowardice in a press conference.
