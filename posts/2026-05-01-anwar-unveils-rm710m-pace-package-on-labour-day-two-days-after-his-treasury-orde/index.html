Anwar Unveils RM710M ‘PACE Package’ on Labour Day — Two Days After His Treasury Ordered RM5.4 Billion in Cuts to Health and Education

2026-05-01
#Economy 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-05-01

📌 What Happened#

On May 1, 2026, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim took the stage at the National Workers’ Day celebration at Unifi Arena, Bukit Jalil, to unveil the RM710 million PACE Economic Resilience Package — billed as a measure to “strengthen jobs, skills and wage resilience.” He acknowledged that oil prices had surged from US$70 to US$115 per barrel and pledged the government would “continue taking necessary measures” to face global economic uncertainty. This was delivered just two days after the Treasury Secretary-General Johan Mahmood Merican issued an internal directive on April 29 ordering sweeping budget cuts across all ministries: a 10% reduction on essential services, 20% cuts to statutory bodies, a hiring freeze, and a proposed RM5.4 billion in cuts to health and higher education — including RM3.06 billion slashed from healthcare alone.

🔥 Why It Matters#

The RM710 million PACE package is roughly 13% of the RM5.4 billion being cut from health and education. This is not stimulus — it is a rounding error dressed up as a Labour Day gift. The subsidy bill has ballooned from RM15 billion budgeted for 2026 to a projected RM58.4 billion, nearly four times the original allocation, driven by the Iran conflict’s impact on global energy prices. Former DAP lawmaker Charles Santiago called it “garlands and cuts” — the government celebrates workers with one hand and slashes the services they depend on with the other. Former Finance Minister Lim Guan Eng warned the austerity measures risk slowing growth and undermining public services. This is a government that has lost control of its own fiscal assumptions and is now scrambling to paper over the damage.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit#

Anwar stood on a Labour Day stage and told workers the government is protecting them — while the Treasury’s own directive, issued 48 hours earlier, is cutting RM3 billion from the hospitals those workers rely on. The PACE package doesn’t even cover a single month of the additional subsidy burden. If you’re spending RM4 billion a month just to keep RON95 and diesel prices down, a RM710 million jobs package is not policy — it’s a press release. The Galen Centre for Health & Social Policy noted that when health budgets are cut at this scale, “a patient may wait longer for cancer treatment, a child may not receive timely care, a dialysis centre may struggle with supplies.” But sure, hand the workers a garland and hope nobody reads the Treasury memo.

📜 Previous Cases#

This follows a well-established Anwar pattern: announce something shiny while quietly absorbing the hit elsewhere. In March 2026, Finance Minister II Amir Hamzah ruled out any stimulus package and told the public to “reduce energy usage” instead. On April 29, Fahmi Fadzil ordered a “soft austerity” — scaling down government events while refusing to retable the budget. Just days before this speech, the Finance Ministry itself classified RM5.4 billion in health and education cuts as “non-critical.” Each time, the response has been the same: platitudes at the podium, austerity in the back office. The government treats fiscal transparency as a threat to be managed, not a standard to be met.

🎯 PH Callout#

You promised reformasi. You promised the rakyat’s money would be spent wisely, transparently, and with their welfare at the centre. Instead, you’re standing on a Labour Day stage handing out RM710 million while your own Treasury is ripping RM5.4 billion from hospitals and universities. Workers don’t need speeches about resilience — they need a government that doesn’t cut their healthcare to pay for a subsidy bill it failed to plan for. If you can’t retable the budget, at least have the honesty to say so, instead of dressing up austerity as compassion. The rakyat can see through the garlands.
