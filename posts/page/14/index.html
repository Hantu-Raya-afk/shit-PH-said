Anwar Says ‘Let’s See’ on Cabinet Pay Cuts — The PM Who Hasn’t Drawn His Salary Wants Credit for Sacrifice While His Ministers Keep Theirs

2026-04-17
#Economy 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-04-17

📌 What Happened

On April 17, after Friday prayers at Surau Bomba in Cyberjaya, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim was asked whether Malaysia would follow Indonesia in cutting ministerial salaries amid the worsening global energy crisis. His answer: ‘Let’s see.’ Anwar told reporters that ministers’ salaries are ‘already relatively low’ and that ‘what matters is that they serve well.’ He said the government is ‘prepared to consider’ further pay reductions if the economy deteriorates, but pivoted to his own gesture of not drawing a salary since 2022. Two days later, Fahmi Fadzil confirmed Cabinet members are ‘ready for further pay cuts’ — without actually committing to them.

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Pada 16 April, Kementerian Dalam Negeri mengharamkan dua buah buku di bawah Akta Mesin Cetak dan Penerbitan kerana didakwa mengandungi “unsur dan ideologi komunis.” Salah satu tajuk yang diharamkan dikaitkan dengan memoir Shamsiah Fakeh, tokoh penting dalam sejarah Kiri Melayu Malaysia. Home Ministry Bans Books for ‘Communist Ideologies’ Under Reformist PH — More Books Banned Under Anwar Than Any Recent Government

2026-04-16
#Politics 

Said by: Saifuddin Nasution Ismail, Home Affairs Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-04-16

📌 What Happened

On April 16, Malaysia’s Home Affairs Ministry banned two books under the Printing Presses and Publications Act for allegedly containing “communist elements and ideologies.” One of the banned titles is linked to the memoirs of Shamsiah Fakeh, a prominent figure in Malaysia’s Malay Left history. The ban drew swift criticism from civil society, publishers, and opposition figures. A week later, on April 24, the ministry announced it would adopt a “more consultative approach” and engage publishers before issuing future bans — a clear sign of damage control after the backlash.
