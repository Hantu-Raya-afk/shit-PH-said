Category: Health

Date: 2025-05-28

Government approved a ‘communication plan’ — not enforcement reform. This matters because Malaysia abolished mandatory death for drug trafficking, then watched seizures hit record highs. Record seizures can mean better enforcement or a bigger problem — and when the UNODC says the regional supply is surging 24% year-on-year, it’s the latter.
