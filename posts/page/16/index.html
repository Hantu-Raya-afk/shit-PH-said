📌 What Happened

On April 10, PKR president and Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim was asked by reporters whether the party should sack former deputy president Rafizi Ramli, who had just received a second show-cause letter from PKR’s disciplinary board and publicly challenged the party to expel him. Anwar brushed aside the question, saying: “Nevermind, I just think everyone should calm down as focusing on the economic problem is more important than on a personal problem.” This came after months of Rafizi openly accusing Anwar of surrounding himself with “yes-men,” protecting vested interests, defending MACC chief Azam Baki, and failing to deliver on reforms. Human Resources Minister Ramanan had already called Rafizi a “failed minister” with an “unhealthy agenda to divide the party.”
