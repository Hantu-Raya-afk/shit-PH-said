Category: Politics

📌 What Happened

On February 19, Pandan MP Rafizi Ramli revealed he had received a show-cause letter from PKR secretary-general Fuziah Salleh after telling Sin Chew Daily he would contest in Pandan at GE16 but likely not as a PKR candidate. Rafizi responded saying the meaning of his comments was “understandable to anyone with a good command of Malay” and that he was free to make his own plans after Parliament was dissolved. On March 6, PKR conveyed its decision by email — no action taken, just a reminder to adhere to the party constitution and code of ethics.
