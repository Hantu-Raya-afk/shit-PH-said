Said by: DOSM / BNM / Consumer Groups

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-01-01

📌 WHAT HAPPENED: While nominal wages have increased slightly under PH — minimum wage raised to RM1,500, modest salary increments across sectors — the cost of living has risen faster. Said by: Khazanah Research Institute (KRI)

Category: Economy

Date: 2025-12-31

📌 WHAT HAPPENED: A Khazanah Research Institute (KRI) study published in late December 2025 found that Greater KL’s public transport system is “functional but fragile” — minor disruptions cause commuters to abandon it entirely for private vehicles. FMT’s analysis argued it is “an unfinished system, not infrastructure, that fails Malaysian commuters.” Yet the government continues announcing mega rail projects — MRT3, LRT3, ECRL — as if building more tracks alone will solve mobility.
