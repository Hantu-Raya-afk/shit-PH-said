[BM] Perbendaharaan Arah Pemotongan Bajet RM10 Bilion — Kesihatan Dan Pendidikan Paling Teruk Dipukul Sementara Bil Subsidi Meletup Dari RM5B Ke RM58.4B

2026-04-29
#Economy 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister & Finance Minister; Johan Mahmood Merican, Treasury Secretary-General

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-04-29

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Pada 29 April 2026, Ketua Setiausaha Perbendaharaan Johan Mahmood Merican mengeluarkan arahan kerajaan mengarahkan semua kementerian dan agensi persekutuan memotong bajet mengurus mereka untuk 2026, memetik kesan kenaikan harga tenaga akibat konflik Timur Tengah. Arahan itu mencadangkan pemotongan RM10 bilion secara keseluruhan, dengan Kementerian Kesihatan paling teruk dipukul pada RM3.06 bilion dan Kementerian Pengajian Tinggi pada RM2.39 bilion. Sebabnya: bil subsidi kerajaan telah meletup daripada RM5 bilion yang diluluskan asal dalam Bajet 2026 kepada unjuran RM58.4 bilion — lebihan 1,068% — didorong oleh kenaikan harga minyak global. Perdana Menteri Anwar Ibrahim, yang turut memegang jawatan Menteri Kewangan, bertanggungjawab secara langsung atas dasar fiskal yang menganggarkan RM5 bilion untuk subsidi sambil mengekalkan program subsidi sejagat yang mahal yang sepatutnya sudah direformasi.

#Economy 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister & Finance Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-04-29

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Pada 29 April 2026, Perbendaharaan Malaysia mengeluarkan arahan mencadangkan pemotongan belanja mengurus sebanyak RM5.4 bilion yang mensasarkan Kementerian Kesihatan (RM3.06 bilion) dan Kementerian Pendidikan Tinggi (RM2.39 bilion). Fahmi Calls RM10 Billion Budget Slash ‘Streamlining’ — Budget 2026 Won’t Be Re-Tabled Despite Subsidy Bill Ballooning 290%

2026-04-29
#Economy 

Said by: Fahmi Fadzil, Communications Minister & Government Spokesperson

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-04-29

📌 What Happened

On April 29, 2026, Communications Minister and government spokesperson Fahmi Fadzil confirmed at a weekly press briefing that all federal ministries had been ordered by the Finance Ministry to review and cut their operating expenditures for 2026. The directive, sent by Treasury Secretary-General Johan Mahmood Merican, proposed RM10 billion in savings — including RM3.06 billion from the Health Ministry and RM2.39 billion from Higher Education — after the government’s subsidy bill ballooned from RM15 billion originally budgeted to a projected RM58.4 billion, driven by fuel subsidies surging from RM700 million to RM7 billion per month due to the Iran-US conflict disrupting oil supplies through the Strait of Hormuz.
