[BM] Akmal Akui Bekalan Bahan Api Hanya Terjamin Sehingga Jun — Menteri Ekonomi Pergi Bloomberg Kata ‘Kami Masih Berunding’

2026-04-30
#Economy 

Said by: Akmal Nasrullah Mohd Nasir, Economy Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-04-30

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Pada 30 April 2026, Menteri Ekonomi Akmal Nasrullah Mohd Nasir tampil di Bloomberg Television — ditemu bual oleh Haslinda Amin — dan mengakui bahawa Malaysia hanya mampu mengekalkan bekalan bahan apinya sehingga akhir Jun 2026. Beliau mengesahkan bahawa Petronas “sedang mengadakan perbincangan dengan pembekal berpotensi” tetapi menekankan bahawa rundingan mesti mendapatkan “harga yang betul.” Beliau juga menyatakan kerajaan telah mengurangkan kuota bahan api bersubsidi dari 300 liter kepada 200 liter bagi setiap rakyat Malaysia dan memperkenalkan pengaturan kerja dari rumah penjawat awam untuk mengurus permintaan. Beliau mendakwa kerajaan “masih mempunyai keanjalan” untuk mengurus subsidi dan defisit fiskal.

2026-04-30
#Economy 

Said by: Finance Ministry / Treasury (under Amir Hamzah Azizan, Finance Minister II)

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-04-30

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Pada 29 April, Perbendaharaan mencadangkan pemotongan perbelanjaan operasi sebanyak RM5.4 bilion yang menyasarkan Kementerian Kesihatan (RM3.06 bilion) dan Kementerian Pengajian Tinggi sebagai sebahagian daripada usaha penjimatan RM10 bilion merentasi semua agensi persekutuan. Pemotongan ini — dibingkaikan sebagai tindak balas kepada kesan konflik Iran terhadap subsidi bahan api — termasuk menangguhkan acara, mengehadkan lawatan luar negara, menjimatkan utiliti, menangguhkan pengambilan kakitangan bagi “jawatan tidak kritikal,” dan mengoptimumkan rizab agensi. Pada 30 April, Kementerian Kewangan mengeluarkan kenyataan menegaskan bahawa “perkhidmatan penting tidak akan terganggu” dan pelarasan hanya melibatkan “perkara tidak kritikal.”

Akmal Admits Fuel Supply Only Secured Until June — Economy Minister Goes on Bloomberg to Say ‘We’re Still Negotiating’

2026-04-30
#Economy 

Said by: Akmal Nasrullah Mohd Nasir, Economy Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-04-30

📌 What Happened

On April 30, 2026, Economy Minister Akmal Nasrullah Mohd Nasir went on Bloomberg Television — interviewed by Haslinda Amin — and admitted that Malaysia can only sustain its fuel supply until the end of June 2026. He confirmed that Petronas “is having conversations with potential suppliers” but stressed that negotiations must secure “the right price.” He also noted the government has already reduced the subsidised fuel quota from 300 litres to 200 litres per Malaysian and introduced civil servant work-from-home arrangements to manage demand. He claimed the government “still has flexibility” to manage subsidies and the fiscal deficit.

Finance Ministry Says RM5.4B Cuts to Health and Education Are ‘Non-Critical’ — RM3 Billion From Hospitals Isn’t Essential?

2026-04-30
#Economy 

Said by: Finance Ministry / Treasury (under Amir Hamzah Azizan, Finance Minister II)

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-04-30

📌 What Happened

On April 29, the Treasury proposed RM5.4 billion in operational spending cuts targeting the Ministry of Health (RM3.06 billion) and the Ministry of Higher Education as part of a broader RM10 billion austerity drive across all federal agencies. The cuts — framed as a response to the Iran conflict’s impact on fuel subsidies — include postponing events, limiting overseas trips, saving on utilities, delaying recruitment for “non-critical positions,” and optimising agency reserves. On April 30, the Finance Ministry issued a statement insisting that “essential services will not be disrupted” and that adjustments only involve “non-critical items.”
