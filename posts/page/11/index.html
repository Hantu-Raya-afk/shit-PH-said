Akmal Says Malaysia Must ‘Accelerate’ Energy Transition — Three Years Into Power, and a War Had to Tell Them

2026-03-15
#Economy 

Said by: Akmal Nasrullah Mohd Nasir, Economy Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

Economy Minister Akmal Nasrullah Mohd Nasir told reporters in Johor Bahru on March 15, 2026, that Malaysia needs to “accelerate” the implementation of the National Energy Transition Roadmap (NETR), particularly the development of solar and hydropower, to reduce reliance on fossil fuels. He made the remarks after attending a “Jualan Rahmah Kasih Sayang” programme, responding to suggestions by academics that Malaysia should cut its fossil fuel dependence following the West Asia conflict that has pushed oil past $100 per barrel and effectively closed the Strait of Hormuz. Akmal noted that Malaysia currently has about 25% installed capacity from renewable sources and said the government is “continuously assessing measures” to accelerate the process.

Anwar Announces Extra Raya Holiday Amid Oil Crisis — Because Nothing Says ‘Austerity’ Like Another Day Off

2026-03-15
#Politics 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim announced an additional public holiday for Hari Raya Aidilfitri on March 15, 2026, speaking at the National Consumer Day celebration at Suria Sabah in Kota Kinabalu. The extra day off will fall on either Friday March 20 or Monday March 23, depending on the moon sighting for Syawal. Anwar framed it as a reward for the people’s “efforts and dedication” during Ramadan, saying the early announcement would help Malaysians plan their travel. The announcement was made alongside Domestic Trade Minister Armizan Mohd Ali, who simultaneously launched a 15-day festive price control scheme covering 22–24 essential goods.
