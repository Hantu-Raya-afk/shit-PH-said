Category: Politics

Date: 2026-04-23

📌 What Happened

On April 15, 2026, Malaysia’s Home Ministry under Minister Saifuddin Nasution Ismail banned two books published by Gerakbudaya — Memoir Shamsiah Fakeh: Dari AWAS ke Rejimen ke-10 and Komrad Asi (Rejimen 10): Dalam Denyut Nihilisme Sejarah — declaring them a danger to national security for containing “communist elements and ideologies.” A third book, Mao Zedong: China dalam Dunia Abad ke-20, a Malay translation of an academic study funded by the PKR-led Selangor state government, was also banned. Saifuddin Nasution Bans Three Books for ‘Communist Ideologies’ — Then U-Turns a Week Later After National Backlash

2026-04-23
#Politics 

Said by: Saifuddin Nasution Ismail, Home Minister (PKR)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-04-23

📌 What Happened

On April 16, 2026, the Home Ministry under Minister Saifuddin Nasution Ismail issued a prohibition order banning two books — Memoir Shamsiah Fakeh: Dari AWAS ke Regimen ke-10 and Komrad Asi Regimen 10: Dalam Denyut Nihilisme Sejarah — citing communist ideologies and threats to national security. A third book, a Selangor government-funded Malay translation of a text about Mao Zedong, was also banned days later. Independent bookstore and publisher Gerakbudaya, which received the order, announced it would challenge the decision through legal channels. Following fierce backlash from civil society groups, academics, lawmakers, and international press freedom organizations including ARTICLE 19, Saifuddin announced on April 23 that the government would reverse all three bans and that he would personally review future book prohibition decisions.
