Fahmi Fadzil Forces Social Media Licensing on Platforms — Critics Call It PH's Version of Internet Censorship

Said by: Fahmi Fadzil, Communications Minister

Category: Social

Date: 2024-07-28

📌 WHAT HAPPENED: On 28 July 2024, the PH government announced that social media platforms with more than 8 million users in Malaysia would be required to apply for a regulatory licence from MCMC, effective 1 August. Communications Minister Fahmi Fadzil said the measure was needed to combat cyber offences including online scams, child exploitation, and deepfakes. By late 2025, Fahmi was threatening legal action against non-compliant platforms. In January 2026, MCMC temporarily blocked X’s Grok AI feature over deepfake concerns. Category: Economy

Date: 2024-06-10

The impact was immediate: transport costs surged, food prices climbed, and businesses passed the costs on to consumers. Dual 5G Network: Billions Wasted on Political Compromise — One Network Would Have Sufficed

Said by: Telecom Analysts / Consumers

Category: Economy

Date: 2024-06-01

📌 WHAT HAPPENED: In mid-2024, the PH government shifted Malaysia’s 5G rollout from a single wholesale network (Digital Nasional Berhad, DNB) to a dual-network model — allowing a second 5G network to be built alongside DNB. The move was widely seen as a political compromise to accommodate major telecom operators with political connections who had opposed the single-network model. The dual-network approach doubles infrastructure costs, splits spectrum efficiency, and delays rural coverage expansion — all to solve a political problem, not a technical one.
