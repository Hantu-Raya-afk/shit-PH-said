Anwar Says ‘Let’s See’ on Cabinet Pay Cuts — The PM Who Hasn’t Drawn His Salary Wants Credit for Sacrifice While His Ministers Keep Theirs

2026-04-17
#Economy 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-04-17

📌 What Happened

On April 17, after Friday prayers at Surau Bomba in Cyberjaya, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim was asked whether Malaysia would follow Indonesia in cutting ministerial salaries amid the worsening global energy crisis. His answer: ‘Let’s see.’ Anwar told reporters that ministers’ salaries are ‘already relatively low’ and that ‘what matters is that they serve well.’ He said the government is ‘prepared to consider’ further pay reductions if the economy deteriorates, but pivoted to his own gesture of not drawing a salary since 2022. Two days later, Fahmi Fadzil confirmed Cabinet members are ‘ready for further pay cuts’ — without actually committing to them.

#Politics 

Said by: Saifuddin Nasution Ismail, Home Affairs Minister (PKR)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-04-16

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Pada 16 April 2026, Kementerian Dalam Negeri Malaysia mengumumkan pengharaman dua buah buku sejarah berbahasa Melayu — Memoir Shamsiah Fakeh: Dari AWAS ke Rejimen Ke-10 dan Komrad Asi (Rejimen 10): Dalam Denyut Nihilisme Sejarah — terbitan Gerak Budaya, dengan alasan mengandungi “unsur dan ideologi komunis” yang kononnya mengancam keselamatan negara. Seminggu kemudian, pada 23 April, Menteri Dalam Negeri Saifuddin Nasution Ismail mengaku beliau hanya tahu tentang pengharaman itu selepas penguatkuasaan sudah dijalankan, dan berkata beliau sedang dalam proses membatalkannya — bersama-sama sebuah lagi buku yang diharamkan, terjemahan Mao Zedong yang dibiayai oleh kerajaan negeri Selangor pimpinan PKR.
