Said by: Rafizi Ramli, Pandan MP / Former Economy Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-07

📌 What Happened

Former Economy Minister Rafizi Ramli, who quit the cabinet in May 2025 after losing the PKR deputy presidency contest, told Pakatan Harapan supporters in a Sin Chew Daily interview to “wait till June,” hinting that a new political option would be unveiled. He confirmed he will defend his Pandan parliamentary seat in the next general election but definitively not under the PKR banner. [BM] 238 Pelantikan Politik Ke Badan Berkanun Dan GLC Di Bawah Anwar — Laporan IDEAS Dedah 'Kenalan Mengatasi Kecekapan'

Said by: IDEAS Report / Anwar Ibrahim Administration

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-06

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Institut Demokrasi dan Hal Ehwal Ekonomi (IDEAS) mengeluarkan laporan bertajuk Who Sits at the Table: From Political to Public Appointees in Malaysia’s Federal Statutory Bodies pada 6 Mac 2026, mendedahkan bahawa 238 pelantikan politik dibuat ke badan berkanun persekutuan (FSB) dan syarikat berkaitan kerajaan (GLC) di bawah pentadbiran Perdana Menteri Anwar Ibrahim antara November 2022 dan Oktober 2025. Daripada jumlah ini, 222 ke FSB dan 16 ke GLC. Laporan itu mengkaji 135 agensi dan mendapati 78% jawatan pengerusi diisi berdasarkan hubungan politik dan bukannya kecekapan. Kebanyakan tidak mempunyai kriteria kelayakan yang jelas.
