📌 What Happened

On May 4, 2026, Communications Minister Datuk Fahmi Fadzil defended the continued use of the Sedition Act 1948, stating it remains “necessary” for cases involving race, religion, and royalty (3R) but should not target political criticism. Speaking during a live Facebook session titled “Tanya — Jangan Sentap!”, Fahmi said PM Anwar Ibrahim was “setting a new tone” by reserving enforcement action for issues affecting public order, national unity, and the institution of the Malay Rulers — while insisting political matters should be handled through political channels.

Nik Nazmi Forces Critic to Apologize in Court Over Flood Mitigation Corruption Claims — The Reform Coalition That Championed Free Speech Now Sues It Into Silence

2026-05-04
#Politics 

Said by: Nik Nazmi Nik Ahmad, Former Natural Resources & Environmental Sustainability Minister (PKR, Setiawangsa MP)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-05-04

📌 What Happened

On May 4, 2026, former Natural Resources and Environmental Sustainability Minister Nik Nazmi Nik Ahmad — still a PKR MP for Setiawangsa — settled a defamation suit against former PKR member Badrul Hisham Shaharin (Chegubard) at the Kuala Lumpur High Court. Chegubard read out a public apology in open court, formally retracting allegations he made in four Facebook posts in December 2023 that accused Nik Nazmi of corruption linked to flood mitigation projects, including claims that an open tender process was rigged and project costs had ballooned to RM16.6 billion. Nik Nazmi filed the suit on January 15, 2024, claiming the posts were malicious and damaged his credibility. The matter was concluded via consent judgment — Chegubard admitted his allegations were “false and baseless.”

Rafizi Reports to MACC Over RM1.1B Arm Deal He Pushed as PH Economy Minister — Expects Charges, Calls Probe’s Timing Suspicious

2026-05-04
#Politics 

Said by: Rafizi Ramli, Former Economy Minister (PKR, Pandan MP)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-05-04

📌 What Happened

Former Economy Minister Datuk Seri Rafizi Ramli reported to the Malaysian Anti-Corruption Commission (MACC) headquarters in Putrajaya on May 4 for questioning over alleged irregularities in a RM1.1 billion semiconductor investment deal between the Economy Ministry and UK-based chip giant Arm Holdings. MACC has recorded 22 statements so far in the probe, which began on February 13. Rafizi, who quit the cabinet in mid-2025 and became one of PM Anwar Ibrahim’s most vocal critics, said on May 1 that he received a summons notice at 3pm requiring him to appear at 10am on Monday — and that he expects to be charged. The probe centres on whether Rafizi pressured the Cabinet to approve the deal during his tenure as Economy Minister.

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Malaysia jatuh tujuh tangga — dari kedudukan ke-88 ke ke-95 daripada 180 negara — dalam Indeks Kebebasan Akhbar Dunia 2026 yang dikeluarkan Reporters Without Borders (RSF) pada 30 April.
