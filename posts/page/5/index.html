Fahmi Orders ‘Soft Austerity’ — Scale Down Events, But Don’t You Dare Retable the Budget

2026-04-29
#Economy 

Said by: Fahmi Fadzil, Communications Minister (Government Spokesperson)

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-04-29

📌 What Happened

On April 29, Communications Minister Fahmi Fadzil — speaking as government spokesman after a National Economic Action Council (MTEN) meeting — announced that all ministries, agencies, statutory bodies, GLCs, and GLICs must scale down official events and trim operating expenditure. The Finance Ministry has directed all ministries to propose budget cuts by May 15. However, Fahmi explicitly stated that Budget 2026 will not be retabled in Parliament, insisting the adjustments involve “optimising existing spending” without changing the overall framework.

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-04-28

📌 What Happened

On April 28, PH Communications Director Fahmi Fadzil issued a statement condemning all 14 UMNO-BN state assemblymen in Negeri Sembilan for withdrawing their support from Menteri Besar Aminuddin Harun, calling it ‘acts of betrayal’ and an attempt to seize the state government. #Politics 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister / Shamsul Azri Abu Bakar, Chief Secretary to the Government

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-04-25

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Pada pagi 25 April, Ketua Setiausaha Negara Shamsul Azri Abu Bakar mengumumkan bekas Hakim Mahkamah Tinggi Abdul Halim Aman akan menggantikan ketua SPRM Azam Baki berkuat kuasa 13 Mei — sehari selepas kontrak setahun Azam tamat. Pengumuman itu diwaktukan dengan tepat: hanya beberapa jam sebelum himpunan “Tangkap Azam Baki” yang dianjurkan oleh Bersih dan kumpulan masyarakat sivil. Ribuan hadir di pusat bandar Kuala Lumpur petang itu, meskipun hujan lebat, menuntut bukan sahaja penyingkiran Azam tetapi penangkapannya dan Suruhanjaya Siasatan Diraja (RCI) ke atas salah laku SPRM. Laporan penyalahgunaan dalam agensi itu mula timbul pada Februari 2026. Selama tiga bulan, kerajaan enggan menggantung Azam, menolak seruan RCI, dan membiarkan masa kontraknya habis.
