Government Names New MACC Chief Hours Before Protest — Replacement, Not Reform

2026-04-25
#Politics 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister / Shamsul Azri Abu Bakar, Chief Secretary to the Government

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-04-25

📌 What Happened

On the morning of April 25, Chief Secretary to the Government Shamsul Azri Abu Bakar announced that former High Court judge Abdul Halim Aman would replace MACC chief Azam Baki effective May 13 — the day after Azam’s one-year contract expires. The announcement was timed precisely: just hours before the “Tangkap Azam Baki” rally organised by Bersih and civil society groups. Thousands turned out in downtown Kuala Lumpur that evening, despite heavy rain, demanding not just Azam’s removal but his arrest and a Royal Commission of Inquiry (RCI) into MACC misconduct. Reports of abuse within the agency first emerged in February 2026. For three months, the government refused to suspend Azam, rejected calls for an RCI, and let the clock run down on his contract.

Anwar Says ‘Bring the Evidence’ on Stolen Funds Allegations — The PM Who Promised Transparency Now Demands Others Do the Investigating

2026-04-24
#Politics 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-04-24

📌 What Happened

On April 24, 2026, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim was confronted by reporters at the Temenggong Ibrahim Teachers Education Campus in Johor Bahru about a viral 49-second video by a woman named Sofia Rini Buyong, who alleged that a former officer of Anwar’s had been instructed to move stolen public money to Africa and possessed unexplained wealth worth “billions of ringgit.” Anwar dismissed the allegations outright, saying he would not entertain baseless claims from social media, asking “How do I entertain such allegations? If I have 1,000 comments, do I have to answer every one?” He challenged accusers to “bring the evidence,” adding that without proof, such allegations were merely a “breeding ground for lies.” Opposition politicians from PAS and Bersatu had called for an investigation. No evidence was provided in the video.
