Said by: Aminuddin Harun, Menteri Besar of Negri Sembilan (PH-Lenggeng)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-04

📌 What Happened

On March 4, 2026, Negri Sembilan Menteri Besar Datuk Seri Aminuddin Harun publicly admitted that his official car routinely runs red lights, after a viral video showed a vehicle believed to be his using blue strobe lights and a siren to blast through a red light on Jalan Rasah.
