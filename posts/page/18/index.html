Category: Economy

📌 What Happened

On May 1, 2026, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim took the stage at the National Workers’ Day celebration at Unifi Arena, Bukit Jalil, to unveil the RM710 million PACE Economic Resilience Package — billed as a measure to “strengthen jobs, skills and wage resilience.” He acknowledged that oil prices had surged from US$70 to US$115 per barrel and pledged the government would “continue taking necessary measures” to face global economic uncertainty.
