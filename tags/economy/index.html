Akmal Says Malaysia Must 'Accelerate' Energy Transition — Three Years Into Power, and a War Had to Tell Them

Said by: Akmal Nasrullah Mohd Nasir, Economy Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

Economy Minister Akmal Nasrullah Mohd Nasir told reporters in Johor Bahru on March 15, 2026, that Malaysia needs to “accelerate” the implementation of the National Energy Transition Roadmap (NETR), particularly the development of solar and hydropower, to reduce reliance on fossil fuels. He made the remarks after attending a “Jualan Rahmah Kasih Sayang” programme, responding to suggestions by academics that Malaysia should cut its fossil fuel dependence following the West Asia conflict that has pushed oil past $100 per barrel and effectively closed the Strait of Hormuz. Akmal noted that Malaysia currently has about 25% installed capacity from renewable sources and said the government is “continuously assessing measures” to accelerate the process.

Guan Eng Claims RM1.6 Billion 'Windfall' From 2019 Samurai Bond — The Profit Is Forex Luck, Not Policy Genius

Said by: Lim Guan Eng, DAP Chairman and MP for Bagan

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

Lim Guan Eng, DAP chairman and former Finance Minister, announced on March 15 that Malaysia stands to record a RM1.6 billion profit from the 200 billion yen samurai bond he issued in March 2019. Citing a parliamentary reply from Prime Minister and Finance Minister Anwar Ibrahim dated March 2, Guan Eng said the yen’s 27.8% depreciation against the ringgit — from RM3.62 per 100 yen at issuance to RM2.59 by December 2025 — has reduced the bond’s principal value from RM7.2 billion to RM5.2 billion. After deducting an estimated RM400 million in interest costs, the net foreign exchange gain is approximately RM1.6 billion. The bond matures in 2029.
