Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister & Finance Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-05-04

📌 What Happened

Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim, speaking at a monthly assembly with ministry staff at Putrajaya on May 4, 2026, admitted that the government can only “partly” help Malaysians struggling with the cost of living. “Are we able to help? My answer is partly yes — we are not able to help everyone,” he said. He acknowledged that official reports claim prices are under control, but conceded that feedback from petty traders — food stall operators — shows prices are rising “gradually week by week” with significant impact on business operations. His big plan: raise the issue at Cabinet this Wednesday.

📌 What Happened

Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim, speaking at the Natural Resources and Environmental Sustainability Ministry’s monthly assembly in Putrajaya on May 4, urged civil servants to help “explain” the government’s fiscal strain amid the Middle East energy crisis. [BM] Mat Sabu Suruh Rakyat ‘Berbelanja Berhemah’ Ketika Harga Makanan Bakal Melonjak — Selepas 2.5 Tahun, Pelan Keterjaminan Makanan Beliau Masih ‘Makan Kurang’

2026-05-03
#Economy 

Said by: Mohamad Sabu, Agriculture and Food Security Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-05-03

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Menteri Pertanian dan Keterjaminan Makanan Mohamad Sabu, pada 3 Mei 2026, mengakui bahawa tekanan kos dalam rantaian bekalan makanan mungkin mendorong harga lebih tinggi dalam bulan-bulan mendatang. Daripada mengumumkan campur tangan baharu atau pembaharuan struktur, preskripsi Mat Sabu untuk rakyat Malaysia ialah “berbelanja berhemah, elak pembaziran, dan utamakan keperluan asas.” Ini berlaku hanya beberapa hari selepas Menteri Ekonomi Akmal Nasrullah mengesahkan bahawa kos input makanan sudah meningkat — ayam naik 3.3%, telur naik 7.3%, dan sayur-sayuran turut mendaki — dan beberapa minggu selepas peniaga memberi amaran bahawa harga boleh melonjak sehingga 50% disebabkan kos bahan api yang melambung tinggi akibat konflik Iran.
