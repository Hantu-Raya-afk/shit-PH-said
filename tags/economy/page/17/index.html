Anwar Says ‘Let’s See’ on Cabinet Pay Cuts — The PM Who Hasn’t Drawn His Salary Wants Credit for Sacrifice While His Ministers Keep Theirs

2026-04-17
#Economy 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-04-17

📌 What Happened

On April 17, after Friday prayers at Surau Bomba in Cyberjaya, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim was asked whether Malaysia would follow Indonesia in cutting ministerial salaries amid the worsening global energy crisis. His answer: ‘Let’s see.’ Anwar told reporters that ministers’ salaries are ‘already relatively low’ and that ‘what matters is that they serve well.’ He said the government is ‘prepared to consider’ further pay reductions if the economy deteriorates, but pivoted to his own gesture of not drawing a salary since 2022. Two days later, Fahmi Fadzil confirmed Cabinet members are ‘ready for further pay cuts’ — without actually committing to them.

2026-03-17
#Economy 

Said by: Fahmi Fadzil, Communications Minister (Government Spokesperson)

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-17

📌 What Happened

On March 17, Communications Minister Fahmi Fadzil announced after a post-Cabinet meeting that the government’s decision on implementing work-from-home for civil servants would be delayed until after Hari Raya Aidilfitri. This came just four days after FMT reported on March 13 that the decision was supposed to be made on Tuesday, March 17. Fahmi said “several aspects still need to be refined” and the matter would go to the National Economic Action Council (MTEN) via the Chief Secretary to the Government. He stressed frontline services like healthcare and education would not be affected.

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-17

📌 What Happened

On Sunday March 16, Investment, Trade and Industry Minister Johari Abdul Ghani told reporters the Malaysia-US Agreement on Reciprocal Trade was “null and void” following the US Supreme Court’s decision striking down Trump’s tariff powers — “It is not on hold.
