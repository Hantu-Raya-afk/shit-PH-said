Toll Abolition Promise Broken — Govt Pays RM591M to FREEZE Hikes Instead

2025-12-18
#Economy 

Said by: Fahmi Fadzil, Communications Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2025-12-18

📌 WHAT HAPPENED: PH promised to abolish highway tolls in both GE14 and GE15. Instead, in December 2025, the government announced it would spend RM591 million just to prevent toll increases on 10 highways in 2026 — paying concessionaires with public money to not raise prices. Anwar Promised to End Hardcore Poverty by Q1 2026 — It’s March 2026 and Nobody’s Talking About It

2025-12-07
#Economy 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister and Finance Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2025-12-07

📌 What Happened

On December 7, 2025, at the closing ceremony of the Rancakkan Madani programme in Putrajaya, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim publicly declared that Malaysia’s hardcore poverty rate had fallen to 0.09 per cent — affecting roughly 7,000 households — and told civil servants that since the number was “so small,” the government could “end it by the first quarter of 2026.” Multiple outlets — Bernama, Malay Mail, The Star, Business Today — ran the claim as headline news. 1MDB Debt Still RM50 Billion — Malaysia’s Largest Fiscal Burden Under Anwar’s Watch

2025-12-03
#Economy 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2025-12-03

📌 WHAT HAPPENED: As of December 2025, Malaysia’s 1MDB debt still stands at approximately RM50 billion — the largest single fiscal burden in the country’s history. RM42 billion has already been paid in principal and interest. RM29 billion has been recovered through enforcement actions. Approximately RM13 billion remains to be settled. Under Anwar’s watch, Najib Razak — the PM who presided over 1MDB — had his jail sentence halved by the Pardons Board and was subsequently granted house arrest. The rakyat continues to pay the debt while the man responsible serves a comfortable sentence.
