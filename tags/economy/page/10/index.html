Anwar Boasts RM426.7B 'Record' Approved Investments — But 'Approved' Doesn't Mean 'Arrived'

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister and Finance Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-06

📌 WHAT HAPPENED

On March 6, 2026, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim announced that Malaysia recorded its highest-ever approved investments of RM426.7 billion in 2025 — an 11% increase from RM384.4 billion in 2024. According to MIDA, 8,390 projects were approved, with the potential to create 244,902 jobs. Nearly RM1 Billion Paid in Court Damages for Government Negligence — Anwar's Fix? Letters of Reprimand

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister and Finance Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-04

📌 What Happened

Prime Minister and Finance Minister Anwar Ibrahim disclosed in a written parliamentary reply — responding to PH’s own William Leong (Selayang) — that the Malaysian government paid out RM933,360,633 in court-awarded damages between 2020 and 2025. The payouts were the result of negligence, misconduct, and other wrongdoing by government officials and agencies.
