Fahmi Orders ‘Soft Austerity’ — Scale Down Events, But Don’t You Dare Retable the Budget

2026-04-29
#Economy 

Said by: Fahmi Fadzil, Communications Minister (Government Spokesperson)

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-04-29

📌 What Happened

On April 29, Communications Minister Fahmi Fadzil — speaking as government spokesman after a National Economic Action Council (MTEN) meeting — announced that all ministries, agencies, statutory bodies, GLCs, and GLICs must scale down official events and trim operating expenditure. The Finance Ministry has directed all ministries to propose budget cuts by May 15. However, Fahmi explicitly stated that Budget 2026 will not be retabled in Parliament, insisting the adjustments involve “optimising existing spending” without changing the overall framework.

[BM] Anwar Kata ‘Kita Lihat’ Soal Pemotongan Gaji Kabinet — PM Yang Tak Ambil Gajinya Mahu Pujian Atas Pengorbanan Sementara Menterinya Simpan Gaji Mereka

2026-04-17
#Economy 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-04-17

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Pada 17 April, selepas solat Jumaat di Surau Bomba di Cyberjaya, Perdana Menteri Anwar Ibrahim ditanya sama ada Malaysia akan mengikuti Indonesia memotong gaji menteri ketika krisis tenaga global yang semakin teruk. Jawapannya: ‘Kita lihat.’ Anwar memberitahu pemberita bahawa gaji menteri ‘sudah agak rendah’ dan bahawa ‘yang penting ialah mereka berkhidmat dengan baik.’ Beliau berkata kerajaan ‘bersedia untuk mempertimbangkan’ pemotongan gaji selanjutnya jika ekonomi merosot, tetapi beralih kepada isyaratnya sendiri tidak mengambil gaji sejak 2022. Dua hari kemudian, Fahmi Fadzil mengesahkan ahli Kabinet ‘bersedia untuk pemotongan gaji selanjutnya’ — tanpa benar-benar komited kepadanya.
