Amir Hamzah Rules Out Stimulus Package Amid Oil Crisis — Government’s Plan Is to ‘Wait and See’

2026-03-16
#Economy 

Said by: Amir Hamzah Azizan, Finance Minister II

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-16

📌 What Happened

On March 16, Finance Minister II Datuk Seri Amir Hamzah Azizan told reporters after launching the Malaysia Co-Investment Fund engagement day in Kuala Lumpur that the government will not introduce an economic stimulus package despite the escalating West Asia conflict. His reasoning: “We do not yet know whether the crisis in West Asia will continue or not. If it does not continue, I think there is no need to implement something like that.” His stated approach: “Look at the short term and ensure the stability that we have.” He confirmed the government is now spending RM3.2 billion a month on fuel subsidies — RM2 billion on petrol and RM1.2 billion on diesel — and encouraged the public and businesses to “reduce energy use.”

[BM] Guan Eng Dakwa ‘Durian Runtuh’ RM1.6 Bilion Dari Bon Samurai 2019 — Keuntungan Itu Nasib Forex, Bukan Kepakaran Dasar

2026-03-15
#Economy 

Said by: Lim Guan Eng, DAP Chairman and MP for Bagan

Category: Economy

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Lim Guan Eng, Pengerusi DAP dan bekas Menteri Kewangan, mengumumkan pada 15 Mac bahawa Malaysia bakal meraih keuntungan RM1.6 bilion daripada bon samurai bernilai 200 bilion yen yang diterbitkannya pada Mac 2019. Merujuk jawapan parlimen daripada Perdana Menteri dan Menteri Kewangan Anwar Ibrahim bertarikh 2 Mac, Guan Eng berkata susut nilai yen sebanyak 27.8% berbanding ringgit — daripada RM3.62 setiap 100 yen semasa terbitan kepada RM2.59 menjelang Disember 2025 — telah mengurangkan nilai pokok bon daripada RM7.2 bilion kepada RM5.2 bilion. Selepas menolak anggaran RM400 juta kos faedah, keuntungan bersih pertukaran asing adalah kira-kira RM1.6 bilion. Bon ini matang pada 2029.
