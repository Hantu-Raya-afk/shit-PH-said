[BM] Rafizi Jerit ‘Penganiayaan Politik’ Selepas Saman SPRM Atas Perjanjian ARM RM1.1B — Lelaki Yang Bina Kerjaya Atas Anti-Rasuah Kini Tak Tahan Disiasati

2026-05-01
#Politics 

Said by: Rafizi Ramli, Former Economy Minister (PKR, Pandan MP)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-05-01

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Rafizi Ramli, bekas Menteri Ekonomi dan Ahli Parlimen PKR Pandan, mengumumkan pada 1 Mei bahawa beliau menerima saman SPRM untuk memberi keterangan di ibu pejabatnya di Putrajaya pada hari Isnin jam 10 pagi. Siasatan berkenaan perjanjian usaha sama bernilai RM1.1 bilion antara kerajaan Malaysia dan firma semikonduktor UK Arm Holdings, yang ditandatangani pada Mac 2025 semasa tempohnya sebagai menteri. Rafizi berkata siasatan menumpukan sama ada beliau menekan Kabinet untuk menandatangani perjanjian secara tergesa-gesa, dan bahawa beliau menjangkakan kemungkinan pertuduhan termasuk pecah amanah jenayah atau memberi maklumat mengelirukan. Beliau menolak siasatan itu sebagai bermotif politik, menyatakan aduan dibuat oleh Perkasa, sebuah NGO yang beliau dakwa “tidak pernah sebelum ini menunjukkan minat dalam isu rasuah.”

Rafizi Cries ‘Political Persecution’ After MACC Summons Over RM1.1B ARM Deal — The Man Who Built His Career on Anti-Corruption Now Can’t Handle an Investigation

2026-05-01
#Politics 

Said by: Rafizi Ramli, Former Economy Minister (PKR, Pandan MP)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-05-01

📌 What Happened

Rafizi Ramli, former Economy Minister and PKR’s Pandan MP, announced on May 1 that he received a MACC summons to give a statement at its Putrajaya headquarters on Monday at 10am. The investigation concerns the RM1.1 billion joint venture agreement between the Malaysian government and UK-based semiconductor firm Arm Holdings, signed in March 2025 during his tenure as minister. Rafizi said the probe focuses on whether he pressured Cabinet to sign the deal hastily, and that he expects possible charges including criminal breach of trust or providing misleading information. He dismissed the investigation as politically motivated, noting the complaint was lodged by Perkasa, an NGO he claims has “never previously shown interest in corruption issues.”
