[BM] Anwar Umum Cuti Raya Tambahan Ketika Krisis Minyak — Kerana Tiada Yang Lebih 'Jimat Cermat' Dari Cuti Tambahan

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Perdana Menteri Anwar Ibrahim mengumumkan cuti umum tambahan sempena Hari Raya Aidilfitri pada 15 Mac 2026, ketika berucap di sambutan Hari Pengguna Kebangsaan di Suria Sabah, Kota Kinabalu. Cuti tambahan akan jatuh sama ada pada Jumaat 20 Mac atau Isnin 23 Mac, bergantung pada penentuan anak bulan Syawal. Anwar membingkaikan pengumuman ini sebagai ganjaran atas “usaha dan dedikasi” rakyat sepanjang Ramadan, sambil berkata pengumuman awal ini membolehkan rakyat merancang perjalanan. Pengumuman dibuat bersama Menteri Perdagangan Dalam Negeri Armizan Mohd Ali, yang turut melancarkan Skim Harga Maksimum Musim Perayaan selama 15 hari meliputi 22–24 barangan keperluan.

Anwar Announces Extra Raya Holiday Amid Oil Crisis — Because Nothing Says 'Austerity' Like Another Day Off

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim announced an additional public holiday for Hari Raya Aidilfitri on March 15, 2026, speaking at the National Consumer Day celebration at Suria Sabah in Kota Kinabalu. The extra day off will fall on either Friday March 20 or Monday March 23, depending on the moon sighting for Syawal. Anwar framed it as a reward for the people’s “efforts and dedication” during Ramadan, saying the early announcement would help Malaysians plan their travel. The announcement was made alongside Domestic Trade Minister Armizan Mohd Ali, who simultaneously launched a 15-day festive price control scheme covering 22–24 essential goods.

Said by: Jimmy Puah Wee Tse, PKR MP for Tebrau

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

On March 15, 2026, PKR MP Jimmy Puah Wee Tse — the Tebrau MP and Johor PKR vice-chief — told FMT that Pakatan Harapan’s core voter base, particularly the M40 group and urban electorate, has been “swinging.” Puah said the coalition must convince these voters that it has “actually delivered” on reforms, or at least is “in the midst of delivering,” before it can negotiate coalition partnerships for GE16. Political scientist Wong Chin Huat of Sunway University echoed the warning, noting that PH’s core supporters are closely watching how Anwar has managed Malaysia’s racial and religious balance, and that “flip-flopping between appeasing Muslim nationalists and wooing minorities and liberals” could alienate them further.
