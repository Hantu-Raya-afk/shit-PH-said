[BM] Puah Akui Pengundi Teras PH 'Bergoyang' — Tiga Tahun Berkuasa, Masih Perlu 'Yakinkan' Rakyat Mereka Reformis

Said by: Jimmy Puah Wee Tse, PKR MP for Tebrau

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Pada 15 Mac 2026, Ahli Parlimen PKR Jimmy Puah Wee Tse — AP Tebrau dan Naib Ketua PKR Johor — memberitahu FMT bahawa pengundi teras Pakatan Harapan, terutamanya golongan M40 dan pengundi bandar, sedang “bergoyang.” Puah berkata gabungan itu mesti yakinkan pengundi ini bahawa ia telah “benar-benar melaksanakan” reformasi, atau sekurang-kurangnya “sedang dalam proses,” sebelum boleh berunding tentang rakan gabungan untuk PRU16. Saintis politik Wong Chin Huat dari Universiti Sunway turut memberi amaran, kata penyokong teras PH memerhati rapat bagaimana Anwar mengurus keseimbangan kaum dan agama Malaysia, dan “berbolak-balik antara memuaskan hati nasionalis Melayu dan merayu golongan minoriti dan liberal” boleh mengasingkan mereka lagi.

🔥 Kenapa Ini Penting

Ini seorang AP PH sendiri yang mengaku secara terbuka bahawa penyokong gabungannya sendiri hilang keyakinan — bukan pengundi luar, bukan pengundi atas pagar, tetapi tapak bandar dan pelbagai kaum yang meletakkan PH di Putrajaya. Tiga tahun memerintah, jika AP sendiri memberitahu media bahawa pengundi teras perlu “diyakinkan” mereka masih reformis, jenama reformasi itu sudah rosak. Pengakuan ini datang ketika PH dalam tekanan hebat: rang undang-undang had penggal PM gagal, skandal Azam Baki, subsidi bahan api tiga kali ganda dan bukannya reformasi struktur, dan Kabinet yang suruh rakyat tanam sayur sendiri. Kejujuran Puah dialu-alukan. Masanya yang menyengat.

💩 Kenapa Ini Karut

Masalah sebenar bukan penyampaian mesej — ia adalah pelaksanaan. Puah membingkaikan seolah-olah isu PH adalah komunikasi: guna bahasa mudah, terangkan apa makna ringgit dalam harga limau mandarin. Tapi pengundi bukan keliru. Mereka faham betul-betul bila rang undang-undang had penggal PM gagal sebab AP kerajaan sendiri tak hadir, bila ketua SPRM dilindungi dan bukannya dipecat, dan bila “reformasi subsidi bersasar” jadi subsidi selimut RM2 bilion. Tak perlu terangkan ringgit kepada isi rumah M40 yang tengok bil runcit mereka naik. Masalah PH bukan pengundi tak faham reformasi. Masalahnya, reformasi itu tak wujud.

📜 Kes-Kes Sebelum Ini

Ini bukan kali pertama orang dalam PH bunyikan loceng amaran. Pada Mac 2023, Timbalan Menteri Hannah Yeoh akui PH perlu “buat lebih” untuk berhubung dengan pengundi soal isu sara hidup. Pada 2024, Lim Guan Eng DAP memberi amaran pengundi bandar “kecewa” dengan rentak perubahan. Pemberontakan terbuka Rafizi Ramli sejak pertengahan 2025 — memuncak dengan faksinya pecah barisan dalam undian had penggal — adalah gejala paling dramatik penyakit yang sama. Coraknya sentiasa sama: tokoh PH diagnos masalah dengan betul, cadangkan penyampaian mesej lebih baik dan bukannya dasar lebih baik, kemudian tertanya-tanya kenapa pilihan raya seterusnya terasa lebih sukar.

🎯 Panggilan Kepada PH

Kamu beritahu rakyat Malaysia kamu gabungan reformasi. Kamu janji perubahan institusi, disiplin fiskal, dan tadbir urus yang berkhidmat untuk rakyat bukan kroni. Tiga tahun kemudian, AP kamu sendiri beritahu akhbar pengundi teras “bergoyang.” Itu bukan masalah komunikasi — itu defisit kepercayaan yang kamu sendiri cipta. Berhenti terangkan ringgit dalam metafora limau mandarin dan mula laksanakan reformasi yang dijanjikan. Tapak sokongan kamu tak perlukan bahasa lebih mudah. Ia perlukan kerajaan yang tunaikan janji.

Puah Admits PH Core Voters Are 'Swinging' — Three Years In, And They Still Need to 'Convince' People They're Reformists

Said by: Jimmy Puah Wee Tse, PKR MP for Tebrau

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

On March 15, 2026, PKR MP Jimmy Puah Wee Tse — the Tebrau MP and Johor PKR vice-chief — told FMT that Pakatan Harapan’s core voter base, particularly the M40 group and urban electorate, has been “swinging.” Puah said the coalition must convince these voters that it has “actually delivered” on reforms, or at least is “in the midst of delivering,” before it can negotiate coalition partnerships for GE16. Political scientist Wong Chin Huat of Sunway University echoed the warning, noting that PH’s core supporters are closely watching how Anwar has managed Malaysia’s racial and religious balance, and that “flip-flopping between appeasing Muslim nationalists and wooing minorities and liberals” could alienate them further.

🔥 Why It Matters

This is a sitting PH MP publicly admitting that the coalition’s own supporters are losing faith — not outsiders, not swing voters, but the urban, multiracial base that put PH in power. Three years into government, if your own MP is telling the media that core voters need to be “convinced” you’re still reformists, the reform brand is already damaged. The admission comes amid a brutal stretch for PH: a failed PM term limit bill, the Azam Baki scandal, a tripled fuel subsidy instead of structural reform, and a Cabinet that told Malaysians to grow their own vegetables. Puah’s candour is welcome. The timing is damning.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit

The real problem isn’t messaging — it’s delivery. Puah’s framing suggests PH’s issue is one of communication: use simpler language, explain what the ringgit means in mandarin orange prices. But voters aren’t confused. They understand perfectly well when a PM term limit bill fails because government MPs don’t show up, when the anti-corruption chief is shielded instead of sacked, and when “targeted subsidy reform” turns into a RM2 billion blanket subsidy. You don’t need to explain the ringgit to an M40 household watching their grocery bill climb. PH’s problem isn’t that voters don’t understand the reforms. It’s that the reforms aren’t there.

📜 Previous Cases

This is not the first time PH insiders have sounded the alarm. In March 2023, then-Deputy Minister Hannah Yeoh acknowledged PH needed to “do more” to connect with voters on bread-and-butter issues. In 2024, DAP’s Lim Guan Eng warned that urban voters were “disillusioned” with the pace of change. Rafizi Ramli’s open rebellion since mid-2025 — culminating in his faction of PKR MPs breaking ranks on the term limit vote — is the most dramatic symptom of the same disease. The pattern is always the same: PH figures diagnose the problem correctly, propose better messaging instead of better policy, and then wonder why the next election feels harder than the last.

🎯 PH Callout

You told Malaysians you were the reform coalition. You promised institutional change, fiscal discipline, and governance that serves the rakyat instead of cronies. Three years later, your own MP is telling the press that your core voters are “swinging.” That’s not a communications problem — it’s a trust deficit you earned. Stop explaining the ringgit in mandarin orange metaphors and start delivering the reforms you promised. Your base doesn’t need simpler language. It needs a government that keeps its word.

[BM] Anwar Kata Rasuah 'Khianat Kepada Negara' — Sementara Kerajaannya Lindungi Ketua SPRM Dan Soal Siasat NGO Anti-Rasuah

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister and PKR President

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-14

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Pada 14 Mac, Perdana Menteri Anwar Ibrahim berucap kepada ahli PKR Sarawak di ‘konvensyen mini’ di Kuching, di mana beliau menyokong seruan Yang di-Pertuan Agong Sultan Ibrahim untuk memerangi rasuah, menyifatkan rasuah sebagai ‘khianat kepada negara.’ Beliau menggesa ahli parti kekal bersatu dan menegaskan semula komitmen PKR terhadap reformasi, keadilan, dan kemajuan inklusif. Kenyataan ini dibuat sebagai sebahagian daripada lawatan kerja dua hari ke Sarawak yang turut merangkumi pertemuan dengan pemimpin negeri dan majlis iftar Madani.

🔥 Kenapa Ini Penting

Perdana Menteri memanggil rasuah ‘khianat’ di konvensyen parti lazimnya tidak luar biasa — setiap PM Malaysia cakap benda yang sama. Yang menjadikannya penting ialah apa yang kerajaannya sebenarnya lakukan dalam dua minggu lepas. Laporan pasukan petugas pimpinan Peguam Negara mengenai dakwaan pegangan saham RM14 juta Ketua SPRM Azam Baki secara senyap dirujuk ke lembaga tatatertib JPA dan bukannya didedahkan kepada umum. Polis menyoal siasat CEO C4 Centre Cynthia Gabriel di bawah Seksyen 124B selepas NGO anti-rasuah itu menerbitkan penemuan kritikal terhadap penguatkuasaan kerajaan. Dan Menteri Komunikasi Fahmi Fadzil menolak seruan untuk Suruhanjaya Siasatan Diraja terhadap SPRM. Kata-kata Anwar bertentangan terus dengan tindakan kerajaannya.

💩 Kenapa Ini Karut

Anda tidak boleh dengan muka serius memanggil rasuah ‘khianat’ sementara respons kerajaan anda terhadap dakwaan rasuah paling menonjol membabitkan lantikan sendiri ialah menanam laporan itu dalam proses tatatertib perkhidmatan awam yang direka untuk hasil senyap. Fail Azam Baki sepatutnya menjadi ujian terbuka janji integriti Madani — sebaliknya, ia diurus dengan budi bicara yang sama seperti BN menguruskan skandalnya. Sementara itu, orang yang benar-benar melakukan kerja anti-rasuah — NGO seperti C4 — yang dapat lawatan polis. Anwar bukan memerangi rasuah. Dia menguruskan optik memerangi rasuah.

📜 Kes-Kes Sebelum Ini

Ini corak yang sudah lusuh. Di Konvensyen PKR Selangor pada Februari, Anwar guna bahasa hampir serupa tentang kerajaan mengambil ’tindakan berani dan konsisten’ terhadap rasuah. Pada Januari, beliau beri ultimatum satu minggu kepada pegawai penguatkuasa untuk tunjukkan hasil — tarikh akhir yang berlalu tanpa kesan ketara. Pada Mac 2025, beliau lancarkan GIACC 2.0 dengan gembar-gembur serupa. Setiap kali, retorik meningkat sementara tindakan institusi terbantut. Jurang antara ucapan konvensyen Anwar dan rekod penguatkuasaan sebenar kerajaannya kini menjadi salah satu ciri utama pentadbiran Madani.

🎯 Panggilan Kepada PH

Kamu kata kamu akan berbeza. Kamu kata Madani bermakna akauntabiliti, bukan sekadar slogan. Kamu kata zaman melindungi kroni dan menghukum pemberi maklumat sudah berakhir. Jadi kenapa pegangan saham RM14 juta Ketua SPRM diuruskan oleh lembaga tatatertib dan bukan siasatan terbuka? Kenapa C4 disoal siasat polis sementara Azam Baki dilindungi proses? Kalau rasuah itu khianat, mulakan dengan rumah sendiri. Rakyat tidak mengundi kerajaan yang bercakap seperti reformis di konvensyen dan mentadbir seperti orang yang mereka ganti.

Anwar Calls Corruption 'Treason to the Nation' — While His Government Shields MACC Chief and Interrogates Anti-Corruption NGOs

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister and PKR President

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-14

📌 What Happened

On March 14, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim addressed PKR Sarawak members at a ‘mini convention’ in Kuching, where he backed Yang di-Pertuan Agong Sultan Ibrahim’s call to fight corruption, describing graft as ’treason to the nation.’ He urged party members to remain united and reaffirmed PKR’s commitment to reform, justice, and inclusive progress. The statement came as part of a two-day working visit to Sarawak that included meetings with state leaders and a Madani iftar event.

🔥 Why It Matters

The Prime Minister calling corruption ’treason’ at a party convention would ordinarily be unremarkable — every Malaysian PM says this. What makes it matter is what his government has actually done in the past fortnight. The AG-led task force report on MACC chief Azam Baki’s alleged RM14 million in undisclosed shareholdings was quietly referred to PSD’s disciplinary board instead of being made public. Police questioned C4 Centre CEO Cynthia Gabriel under Section 124B after the anti-corruption NGO published findings critical of government enforcement. And Communications Minister Fahmi Fadzil rejected calls for a Royal Commission of Inquiry into MACC. Anwar’s words run directly opposite to his government’s actions.

💩 Why It’s Bullshit

You cannot credibly call corruption ’treason’ while your government’s response to the most prominent corruption allegation against your own appointee is to bury the report in a civil service disciplinary process designed for quiet outcomes. The Azam Baki file should be a public test of Madani’s integrity promise — instead, it’s being handled with the same discretion BN used to manage its scandals. Meanwhile, the people actually doing anti-corruption work — NGOs like C4 — are getting police visits. Anwar isn’t fighting corruption. He’s managing the optics of fighting corruption.

📜 Previous Cases

This is a well-worn pattern. At the Selangor PKR Convention in February, Anwar used nearly identical language about the government taking ‘bold and consistent action’ against corruption. In January, he gave enforcement officials a one-week ultimatum to show results — a deadline that came and went without visible consequence. In March 2025, he launched GIACC 2.0 with similar fanfare. Each time, the rhetoric escalates while institutional follow-through stalls. The gap between Anwar’s convention speeches and his government’s actual enforcement record is now one of the defining features of the Madani administration.

🎯 PH Callout

You told us you would be different. You told us Madani meant accountability, not just slogans. You told us the days of protecting cronies and punishing whistleblowers were over. So why is the MACC chief’s RM14 million in shares being handled by a disciplinary board instead of a public inquiry? Why is C4 being questioned by police while Azam Baki is being shielded by process? If corruption is treason, start with your own house. The rakyat didn’t vote for a government that talks like reformists at conventions and governs like the people it replaced.
