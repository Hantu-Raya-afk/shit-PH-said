Government Names New MACC Chief Hours Before Protest — Replacement, Not Reform

2026-04-25
#Politics 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister / Shamsul Azri Abu Bakar, Chief Secretary to the Government

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-04-25

📌 What Happened

On the morning of April 25, Chief Secretary to the Government Shamsul Azri Abu Bakar announced that former High Court judge Abdul Halim Aman would replace MACC chief Azam Baki effective May 13 — the day after Azam’s one-year contract expires. The announcement was timed precisely: just hours before the “Tangkap Azam Baki” rally organised by Bersih and civil society groups. Thousands turned out in downtown Kuala Lumpur that evening, despite heavy rain, demanding not just Azam’s removal but his arrest and a Royal Commission of Inquiry (RCI) into MACC misconduct. Reports of abuse within the agency first emerged in February 2026. For three months, the government refused to suspend Azam, rejected calls for an RCI, and let the clock run down on his contract.

Fahmi Kicks WFH Decision to After Hari Raya — Government’s Oil Crisis Response Is to Study, Review, Then Delay

2026-03-17
#Politics 

Said by: Fahmi Fadzil, Communications Minister (Government Spokesperson)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-17

📌 What Happened

On March 17, Communications Minister Fahmi Fadzil told reporters after a Cabinet meeting that a final decision on implementing work-from-home (WFH) arrangements for the public sector will only come after the Hari Raya Aidilfitri holidays. Fahmi said ministers had reviewed an ‘initial report’ on flexible working arrangements but that ‘several aspects still need to be refined.’ The matter will be referred to the National Economic Action Council (MTEN) via the Chief Secretary to the Government for a post-Raya decision. This WFH proposal was first floated as an austerity measure in response to the oil crisis triggered by the US-Iran conflict in West Asia, after PM Anwar directed the Chief Secretary and PSD director-general to study it.
