Rafizi Reports to MACC Over RM1.1B Arm Deal He Pushed as PH Economy Minister — Expects Charges, Calls Probe’s Timing Suspicious

2026-05-04
#Politics 

Said by: Rafizi Ramli, Former Economy Minister (PKR, Pandan MP)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-05-04

📌 What Happened

Former Economy Minister Datuk Seri Rafizi Ramli reported to the Malaysian Anti-Corruption Commission (MACC) headquarters in Putrajaya on May 4 for questioning over alleged irregularities in a RM1.1 billion semiconductor investment deal between the Economy Ministry and UK-based chip giant Arm Holdings. MACC has recorded 22 statements so far in the probe, which began on February 13. Rafizi, who quit the cabinet in mid-2025 and became one of PM Anwar Ibrahim’s most vocal critics, said on May 1 that he received a summons notice at 3pm requiring him to appear at 10am on Monday — and that he expects to be charged. The probe centres on whether Rafizi pressured the Cabinet to approve the deal during his tenure as Economy Minister.

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Pada 3 Mei 2026, Perdana Menteri Anwar Ibrahim mengisytiharkan di acara MARA di Kuala Lumpur bahawa agenda pemerkasaan Bumiputera telah dilaksanakan “lebih tegas, kukuh dan berani” di bawah kerajaan MADANI beliau. Beliau juga menetapkan tarikh akhir tiga bulan untuk Akta MARA 1966 yang disemak, manakala Pengerusi MARA Datuk Dr Asyraf Wajdi Dusuki mengesahkan draf pindaan sedang menjalani libat urus pemegang taruh.

Anwar Declares Bumiputera Agenda ‘Bolder Than Ever’ Under MADANI — The Same Man Who Promised to End Race-Based Affirmative Action in 2008

2026-05-03
#Politics 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister & Finance Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-05-03

📌 What Happened

On May 3, 2026, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim declared at a MARA event in Kuala Lumpur that the Bumiputera empowerment agenda has been pursued “more assertively, firmly and boldly” under his MADANI government. He rejected allegations that his administration subscribes to elitism or sidelines Malay interests, citing automatic matriculation placement for 10A students regardless of race and a rise in Bumiputera household income of more than 10 percent. He also set a three-month deadline for the revised MARA Act 1966, while MARA chairman Datuk Dr Asyraf Wajdi Dusuki confirmed the draft amendments are undergoing stakeholder engagement.
