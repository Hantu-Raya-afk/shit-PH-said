Category: Politics

Date: 2026-04-28

📌 What Happened

On April 28, PH Communications Director Fahmi Fadzil issued a statement condemning all 14 UMNO-BN state assemblymen in Negeri Sembilan for withdrawing their support from Menteri Besar Aminuddin Harun, calling it ‘acts of betrayal’ and an attempt to seize the state government. The withdrawal came after Aminuddin’s handling of a crisis involving four Adat Perpatih territorial chieftains who declared they were deposing the Yang di-Pertuan Besar, Tuanku Muhriz — a crisis BN says Aminuddin mismanaged by taking sides instead of remaining neutral, leading to the indefinite postponement of the State Legislative Assembly on April 23.
