Anwar Hides Behind Royal Decree to Keep PH in Power in Negeri Sembilan — After Losing Majority When 14 UMNO Assemblymen Walk Out

2026-05-04
#Politics 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister & Finance Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-05-04

📌 What Happened

On May 4, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim responded to the Negeri Sembilan political crisis by refusing to comment on UMNO’s decision to withdraw support from the PH-led state government, instead invoking the Ruler’s decree to maintain the status quo. “The Umno (Negeri Sembilan) statement is not yet clear. I do not wish to comment on it,” Anwar told reporters at a ministry assembly in Putrajaya. All 14 UMNO assemblymen in the state withdrew support for Menteri Besar Aminuddin Harun on April 27, citing his interference in a constitutional crisis involving traditional chieftains attempting to depose the state ruler Tuanku Muhriz. Rather than address the loss of majority, Anwar said: “We abide by the Ruler’s decree, which calls for stability and for the Menteri Besar to continue his duties.” DAP secretary-general Anthony Loke separately insisted the state government “remains intact and stable.”

Fahmi Defends Sedition Act PH Promised to Abolish — Now Says It’s ‘Needed’ for 3R Issues

2026-05-04
#Politics 

Said by: Fahmi Fadzil, Communications Minister & Government Spokesperson (PKR)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-05-04

📌 What Happened

On May 4, 2026, Communications Minister Datuk Fahmi Fadzil defended the continued use of the Sedition Act 1948, stating it remains “necessary” for cases involving race, religion, and royalty (3R) but should not target political criticism. Speaking during a live Facebook session titled “Tanya — Jangan Sentap!”, Fahmi said PM Anwar Ibrahim was “setting a new tone” by reserving enforcement action for issues affecting public order, national unity, and the institution of the Malay Rulers — while insisting political matters should be handled through political channels.

Loke’s Office Weaponises Police Report to Silence Critic Over Negeri Sembilan Crisis — Political Secretary Invokes ‘3R’ Card Against Chegubard

2026-05-04
#Politics 

Said by: Anthony Loke, Transport Minister & DAP Secretary-General (via political secretary Ho Weng Wah)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-05-04

📌 What Happened

On May 4, 2026, Ho Weng Wah — the political secretary to Transport Minister and DAP Secretary-General Anthony Loke — lodged a police report at the Seremban District Police Headquarters against former Bersatu division leader Badrul Hisham Shaharin, better known as Chegubard. The report was filed over a Facebook post titled “Anthony Loke must be investigated for funding an illegal entity — Majlis Dato Lembaga Adat Sungei Ujong,” which alleged that Loke had channelled a substantial amount of funds to an entity claimed to be illegal and linked the matter to disturbances involving the royal institution in Negeri Sembilan. Ho told reporters the post “touches on sensitive 3R issues (race, religion and royalty), which could potentially incite tension and divide the community.”

Nik Nazmi Forces Critic to Apologize in Court Over Flood Mitigation Corruption Claims — The Reform Coalition That Championed Free Speech Now Sues It Into Silence

2026-05-04
#Politics 

Said by: Nik Nazmi Nik Ahmad, Former Natural Resources & Environmental Sustainability Minister (PKR, Setiawangsa MP)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-05-04

📌 What Happened

On May 4, 2026, former Natural Resources and Environmental Sustainability Minister Nik Nazmi Nik Ahmad — still a PKR MP for Setiawangsa — settled a defamation suit against former PKR member Badrul Hisham Shaharin (Chegubard) at the Kuala Lumpur High Court. Chegubard read out a public apology in open court, formally retracting allegations he made in four Facebook posts in December 2023 that accused Nik Nazmi of corruption linked to flood mitigation projects, including claims that an open tender process was rigged and project costs had ballooned to RM16.6 billion. Nik Nazmi filed the suit on January 15, 2024, claiming the posts were malicious and damaged his credibility. The matter was concluded via consent judgment — Chegubard admitted his allegations were “false and baseless.”

Rafizi Reports to MACC Over RM1.1B Arm Deal He Pushed as PH Economy Minister — Expects Charges, Calls Probe’s Timing Suspicious

2026-05-04
#Politics 

Said by: Rafizi Ramli, Former Economy Minister (PKR, Pandan MP)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-05-04

📌 What Happened

Former Economy Minister Datuk Seri Rafizi Ramli reported to the Malaysian Anti-Corruption Commission (MACC) headquarters in Putrajaya on May 4 for questioning over alleged irregularities in a RM1.1 billion semiconductor investment deal between the Economy Ministry and UK-based chip giant Arm Holdings. MACC has recorded 22 statements so far in the probe, which began on February 13. Rafizi, who quit the cabinet in mid-2025 and became one of PM Anwar Ibrahim’s most vocal critics, said on May 1 that he received a summons notice at 3pm requiring him to appear at 10am on Monday — and that he expects to be charged. The probe centres on whether Rafizi pressured the Cabinet to approve the deal during his tenure as Economy Minister.
