[BM] Malaysia Jatuh 7 Tangga Dalam Indeks Kebebasan Akhbar Di Bawah Pengawasan Fahmi — Menteri Yang Kata Ranking RSF ‘Bukan Standard Emas’ Kini Mengetuai Tindakan Keras

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Malaysia jatuh tujuh tangga — dari kedudukan ke-88 ke ke-95 daripada 180 negara — dalam Indeks Kebebasan Akhbar Dunia 2026 yang dikeluarkan Reporters Without Borders (RSF) pada 30 April. Skor keseluruhan negara merosot dari 56.09 kepada 52.73, dengan kemerosotan merentasi petunjuk utama termasuk dimensi undang-undang dan sosial. Menteri Komunikasi Fahmi Fadzil, yang menyelia Suruhanjaya Komunikasi dan Multimedia Malaysia (SKMM), pernah menolak indeks RSF sebagai “bukan standard emas” — pendirian yang semakin nampak selesa ketika Malaysia terus merudum di bawah pentadbirannya.

Anwar Declares Bumiputera Agenda ‘Bolder Than Ever’ Under MADANI — The Same Man Who Promised to End Race-Based Affirmative Action in 2008

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister & Finance Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-05-03

📌 What Happened

On May 3, 2026, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim declared at a MARA event in Kuala Lumpur that the Bumiputera empowerment agenda has been pursued “more assertively, firmly and boldly” under his MADANI government. He rejected allegations that his administration subscribes to elitism or sidelines Malay interests, citing automatic matriculation placement for 10A students regardless of race and a rise in Bumiputera household income of more than 10 percent. He also set a three-month deadline for the revised MARA Act 1966, while MARA chairman Datuk Dr Asyraf Wajdi Dusuki confirmed the draft amendments are undergoing stakeholder engagement.

Malaysia Drops 7 Places in Press Freedom Index Under Fahmi’s Watch — The Minister Who Called RSF Rankings ‘Not the Gold Standard’ Now Presides Over the Crackdown

📌 What Happened

Malaysia dropped seven places — from 88th to 95th out of 180 countries — in the 2026 World Press Freedom Index released by Reporters Without Borders (RSF) on April 30. The country’s overall score fell from 56.09 to 52.73, with deterioration across key indicators including the legal and social dimensions. The Malaysian Media Council (Majlis Media Malaysia) on May 3 flagged the decline and called for stronger protections for journalists and media independence. Communications Minister Fahmi Fadzil, who oversees the Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission (MCMC), has previously dismissed the RSF index as “not the gold standard” — a position that looks increasingly convenient as Malaysia slides further down the rankings under his watch.
