Rafizi Claims UMNO Wants to Force GE by October — The Man Who Lost His Deputy Presidency Now Plays Coalition Strategist From the Backbench

2026-05-02
#Politics 

Said by: Rafizi Ramli, Former Economy Minister (PKR, Pandan MP)

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-05-02

📌 What Happened

On May 2, 2026, former PKR deputy president and Pandan MP Rafizi Ramli publicly claimed that the Negeri Sembilan political crisis — triggered when 14 UMNO-BN assemblymen withdrew support from the PH-led state government — is part of a deliberate UMNO strategy to force a general election by October 2026. Speaking as PH allies continued to reel from the episode, Rafizi warned that UMNO’s decision would have “unintended consequences” and reminded voters of UMNO’s poor performance in the last polls, positioning the crisis as an UMNO power grab rather than a failure of PH coalition management.
