Anwar Announces Extra Raya Holiday Amid Oil Crisis — Because Nothing Says ‘Austerity’ Like Another Day Off

2026-03-15
#Politics 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim announced an additional public holiday for Hari Raya Aidilfitri on March 15, 2026, speaking at the National Consumer Day celebration at Suria Sabah in Kota Kinabalu. The extra day off will fall on either Friday March 20 or Monday March 23, depending on the moon sighting for Syawal. Anwar framed it as a reward for the people’s “efforts and dedication” during Ramadan, saying the early announcement would help Malaysians plan their travel. The announcement was made alongside Domestic Trade Minister Armizan Mohd Ali, who simultaneously launched a 15-day festive price control scheme covering 22–24 essential goods.

Anwar Says Malaysia ‘Cannot Get Involved’ Militarily in Middle East — Nobody Asked, But Thanks for Clarifying

#Politics 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

At the National Consumer Day 2026 event in Kota Kinabalu on March 15, Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim declared that Malaysia “cannot get involved in what is happening in the Middle East, especially through military intervention.” He said Malaysia, as a “free country,” can voice its objections to war and violence, and stated: “We object to the attacks by Israel and the United States on Iran.” He acknowledged that the closure of the Strait of Hormuz would have “serious” implications that are “likely to worsen” and that “the cost of living would definitely rise.”
