Said by: Jimmy Puah Wee Tse, PKR MP for Tebrau

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Anwar Announces Extra Raya Holiday Amid Oil Crisis — Because Nothing Says ‘Austerity’ Like Another Day Off

2026-03-15
#Politics 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim announced an additional public holiday for Hari Raya Aidilfitri on March 15, 2026, speaking at the National Consumer Day celebration at Suria Sabah in Kota Kinabalu. The extra day off will fall on either Friday March 20 or Monday March 23, depending on the moon sighting for Syawal. Anwar framed it as a reward for the people’s “efforts and dedication” during Ramadan, saying the early announcement would help Malaysians plan their travel. The announcement was made alongside Domestic Trade Minister Armizan Mohd Ali, who simultaneously launched a 15-day festive price control scheme covering 22–24 essential goods.

Anwar Says Sabah 40% Revenue ‘Case Closed, Period’ — While Filing a Court Application to Reopen Parts of the Judgment

2026-03-15
#Politics 

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-15

📌 What Happened

At a Madani Iftar event at Universiti Malaysia Sabah on March 15, Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim declared that Sabah’s 40 per cent revenue entitlement under the Malaysia Agreement 1963 is “final, case closed” and that there will be “no appeal, no negotiations, period.” In the same speech, he explained that the federal government is going to court — not to appeal the 40 per cent, he insists, but to “seek clarification” on parts of the High Court judgment that he says unfairly questioned the roles of past leaders including Tunku Abdul Rahman, Tun Abdul Razak, and Sabah stalwarts like Tun Fuad Stephens and Tan Sri Harris Salleh. A stay of execution application is scheduled for hearing at the Court of Appeal on March 31. The Sabah government has indicated it will oppose the stay.
