Fahmi Blames Opposition 'Confusion' for Failed PM Term Limit Bill — While 8 Government MPs Didn't Even Show Up

Said by: Fahmi Fadzil, Communications Minister

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-03

📌 What Happened

On March 2, 2026, the Constitutional (Amendment) Bill 2026 to limit the Prime Minister’s tenure to two terms (10 years) was put to a bloc vote in the Dewan Rakyat. It needed a two-thirds supermajority of 148 votes. It got 146. Thirty-two MPs were absent, including eight from the government bloc, and 44 opposition MPs who were present abstained. The bill failed by two votes. The next day, Communications Minister Fahmi Fadzil blamed the opposition for “confusion” and said their abstentions proved they didn’t want to limit prime ministerial power — while glossing over the fact that his own government couldn’t even get full attendance for what was supposed to be a flagship reform.

PH's Own Term Limit Bill Falls 2 Votes Short — 32 MPs Absent, 44 Abstained, Zero Voted Against

Said by: Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister / Pakatan Harapan Coalition

Category: Politics

Date: 2026-03-03

📌 What Happened

On March 3, 2026, Malaysia’s parliament voted on the Constitution (Amendment) Bill 2026, which would have instituted a two-term, 10-year limit for prime ministers — one of Anwar Ibrahim’s flagship reform promises since taking office in November 2022. The bill needed a two-thirds supermajority of 148 votes. It got 146. House Speaker Tan Sri Johari Abdul announced the results: 146 in favor, 32 absent, 44 abstained. Not a single MP voted against the bill. It failed because the government’s own coalition could not get its members into the chamber.
