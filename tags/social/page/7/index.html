Online Safety Act Passes — MCMC Gets Sweeping Censorship Powers, No Oversight

Said by: Parliament / Fahmi Fadzil

Category: Social

Date: 2024-12-20

📌 WHAT HAPPENED: In December 2024, Parliament passed the Online Safety Act and CMA amendments giving MCMC sweeping censorship powers with penalties up to RM10 million for non-compliant platforms. The legislation empowers MCMC to order content removal, block websites, and penalise social media companies — all without independent judicial oversight. Communications Minister Fahmi Fadzil described it as necessary to protect children and combat cyber threats. Freedom House flagged it as a major regression in internet freedom. Orang Asli Land Rights Violations Continue — Indigenous Communities Displaced for Logging and Plantations

Said by: Amnesty International / Indigenous Rights Groups

Category: Social

Date: 2024-12-01

📌 WHAT HAPPENED: Throughout 2024, Orang Asli and indigenous communities in Peninsular Malaysia continued to face systematic violations of customary land rights. Amnesty International flagged indigenous rights as one of seven areas where Malaysia fell short of its human rights obligations.
