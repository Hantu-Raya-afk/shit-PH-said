📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Tiong King Sing Tells Critics to ‘Stop Politicising’ Government-Backed Water Rave — After His Own Ministry Drew PM’s Wrath Over Alcohol

2026-05-01
#Social 

Said by: Tiong King Sing, Tourism, Arts and Culture Minister

Category: Social

Date: 2026-05-01

📌 What Happened

On May 1, 2026, Tourism, Arts and Culture Minister Datuk Seri Tiong King Sing dismissed mounting criticism of the Rain Rave Water Music Festival 2026, held the previous night at Bukit Bintang under the Visit Malaysia 2026 campaign. Tiong told critics to “stop politicising” the event, claimed over 50,000 people attended, boasted about getting soaked on stage, and insisted the festival “is not Songkran” — while pointing to what he called a “double standard” because a Songkran celebration in Kedah on April 18 faced no similar backlash. He confirmed he had received a memorandum from critics calling for his resignation over the festival.

📌 Apa Yang Berlaku

Festival ini sebahagian daripada Tahun Melawat Malaysia 2026.
